# Retrospective

## What worked

- Progressive enhancement already supplied current screenshots without repeated
  tutorial markup edits.
- Comparing prose with authoritative menu, input-reference, and Shakedown
  builders exposed a small, focused stale set.
- Owner review covered the rendered desktop and narrow layouts.

## What changed during delivery

- The screenshot placeholders were retained because `site.ts` upgrades them
  after each image loads.
- The prose now lists Scenarios and Mods, describes functional Settings, and
  includes Settings in the pause menu.
- HUD and lock wording now matches notification chips, objective markers, and
  staged Ctrl clearing.
- The tutorial claim is limited to core flight and targeting verbs.

## Next time

- Treat placeholder markup as a capture manifest, not necessarily missing UI.
- Audit user-facing claims against canonical builders and input references
  before using source-text searches as completion evidence.
