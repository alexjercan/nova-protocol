Press A. conflict_for clears it — the only other holder of A is novaos_pan_left, whose Viewer context does not overlap Flight — so main_drive becomes A, W and Space stop burning, and persist_settings_on_change writes it to disk. The only route back is Reset Defaults.

The same live arm reaches the NOVA OS: typing map at the terminal prompt captures m.

The pad question Held

Rated Major, and separated because it is the one finding nobody could test. It is now the one finding deliberately left standing: the controller defaults are being reconsidered as a set, after a playtest.

The RCS stick has no deadzone, and its modifier is the stick's own click

The magnitude story is right. RCS_STICK_SCALE cancels the observer's mouse gain exactly, so full lean lands full intent and the burn model then takes about 1.3 s to reach the cap. That is a translation, not a twitch — the correct call.

The binding is hand-built from Scale and Negate, so it does not carry the DeadZone that Axial::left_stick()'s preset would have brought. The observer sets intent from the value every frame and the decay can never bleed it off while the stick is fired, so a worn stick resting 4% off centre applies a constant 4% thruster for as long as RCS is held. A held-position device needs the deadzone a delta device does not.

And rcs_modifier is LeftThumb — clicking the stick in. Pressing a stick down physically deflects it, so on a pad the gesture that enters RCS injects a spike into the axis that steers it, at the moment the player wants a null. The binding comment calls the pairing a virtue; mechanically it is the one pairing that guarantees a nudge on entry.

There is no controller on this machine. This is reasoned from the code and the burn model, and it is the finding most worth confirming by hand.
