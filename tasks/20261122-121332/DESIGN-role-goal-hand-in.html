Nova Role Goal Hand-in

A role goal step that is met today pays nothing, says nothing and asks for nothing. The Jobs pane row only swaps its text to the next line. The plan: each step of the role goal waits for a Hand in in the Jobs pane, from anywhere, pays a fixed credit reward with the job payout sound and count-up, and posts GOAL-1 PAID 3,000 CR. The pane lists the whole chain, done steps with their play time.

Recommendation

H1 + C1 + R1 + E1

A role goal is a chain of authored steps. Each step, once its line is met, waits for a Hand in on its row in the Jobs pane, from anywhere. The hand-in pays the step's reward_cr (3,000 then 10,000 on every base role), plays the payout sound with the count-up, and posts GOAL-n PAID. The last paid step stamps the goal met. Nothing else in the world changes.

End state

  • Content (nova_scenario): RoleGoal { title, steps: Vec<RoleGoalStep> }, RoleGoalStep { kind: RoleGoalType, reward_cr: u32 }, RoleGoalType::StationsOwned { stations: u32 }. Every field required.
  • Session (nova_gameplay::goal): GoalRecord { paid_at: Vec<f64>, hulls_taken: u32 }; met is paid_at.len() == steps.len(). GoalProgress { title, steps: Vec<GoalStep> } with GoalStep { line: GoalLine, reward_cr: u32 }. OpenObjectiveType::Milestone { step: usize, line: GoalLine, ready: bool } is the first unpaid step.
  • Pure plan (nova_gameplay): plan_goal_hand_in(progress, record, step) -> Result<u32, GoalHandInRefusalType> with refusals NotCurrent, NotMet, NoGoal.
  • Events: GoalStepDone { step }, raised by track_role_goal on the frame the current step's line turns met, never on a Load; GoalStepHandedIn { step, reward_cr }, raised by the pane. Scenario events OnGoalStepDone and OnGoalStepHandedIn (generated worlds only). Credit cause GoalReward: the credits come from no station.
  • UI: the goal block in "Your jobs" (title row with steps paid, one row per step, GoalHandIn-n button); the Map block shows the current step with done in Accent while ready; HUD notices GOAL-n done: hand in and GOAL-n PAID x CR.

Call graph

BEFORE
Jobs pane hand-in -> Jobs::complete -> JobHandedIn -> "JOB-n PAID"
track_role_goal (every frame) -> GoalProgress.lines [own, station]
                              -> GoalRecord.met_at   (all lines met only)
open_objectives -> Milestone(first unmet line) -> Jobs row | Map | save header

AFTER
track_role_goal -> GoalProgress.steps
                -> current step turns met: trigger GoalStepDone
                       -> HUD "GOAL-n done: hand in"   -> OnGoalStepDone
Jobs pane "Hand in" (GoalHandIn-n) -> GoalHandInCommand { step }
   -> plan_goal_hand_in -> ShipCredits += reward (cause GoalReward)
   -> BalanceCountUp + Paid cue -> GoalRecord.paid_at.push(clock)
   -> trigger GoalStepHandedIn -> HUD "GOAL-n PAID x CR" (silent)
                               -> OnGoalStepHandedIn, OnCreditsChanged(GoalReward)
open_objectives -> Milestone { step, line, ready } -> Jobs block | Map | save header

What dies

  • RoleGoal.kind, STATIONS_OWNED_LABEL, STATIONS_OWNED_NEED, GoalRecord.met_at, GoalProgress.lines, OpenObjectiveType::Milestone(GoalLine), the one-row goal_row.
  • The rule "a met line changes nothing". The world rule "a met goal changes nothing in the world" stays.

What may break

  • system_role_goals claims 2 to 6 (the stamp moves from the station buy to the second hand-in); system_world_chart claim 9 (JobsMilestone row); news_0160_jobs_pane screenshot; system_start_roles goal reads.
  • nova_menu load screen and world setup tests (GoalLine rows, role step lists the goal), save/tests.rs goal records, open_objectives tests, lint tests, nova_editor asset form tests for the goal kind (asset/tests.rs:1369-1382), start_role_census (calls goal_progress).
  • Base RON regenerated; the example mod's surveyor migrates by hand.

What must fail loudly

  • Lint, then load: a goal with no steps; a step count of zero; a reward_cr of zero (if decision 3 keeps credits on every step).
  • Save check refuses the world: more paid steps than the role has; a stamp ahead of the clock or out of order; a stamp not finite.
  • A hand-in of a step that is not current or not met is refused with the Refused cue and pays nothing. A step index past the chain is a warn! and a refusal, as a stale button.
  • GoalStepDone must not fire on Load, New Game or the first derived frame. Proof below.

Decisions for the owner

  1. How is a met step collected?
    • H1 Hand in button on the goal row in the Jobs pane, from anywhere.
    • H2 Auto-pay on the frame it is met, with the same notice and sound.
    • H3 A HUD key.

    Recommend H1. It is what you asked for and reuses the job hand-in moment.

  2. Where does the chain live?
    • C1 Steps in content, each with its kind and reward; StationsOwned becomes a kind every base role lists as step 2.
    • C2 Keep one kind plus the code-rule station line; one reward field plus a code constant.

    Recommend C1. One owner per step and per reward; it reverses the 2026-10-24 "rule in code" note, not your "one station after one flavour goal" rule.

  3. How much does each step pay?
    • R1 3,000 cr then 10,000 cr, every role.
    • R2 1,500 cr then 5,000 cr.
    • R3 Credits plus a non-credit reward (merit, title, unlock): each needs its own design.

    Recommend R1: about three job rewards and 18% of a station.

  4. Does the Jobs pane show the whole chain?
    • Yes every step: done dim with play time, current lit, later dim.
    • No only the current step, as today.

    Recommend Yes. The jump to Stations owned is the surprise you reported.

  5. What happens after the last step?
    • E1 Finite: the goal is met, the record stays, the world plays on.
    • E2 Repeating tiers.
    • E3 A second chain, designed later.

    Recommend E1.

  6. Mod surface?
    • Full OnGoalStepDone, OnGoalStepHandedIn and cause GoalReward, mirroring the job events.
    • Cause only GoalReward on OnCreditsChanged.

    Recommend Full. The HUD needs both events anyway; the scenario bridge is two rows.

Left out on purpose: a music sting, a HUD chip that stays, a rank or title, any world change on a met goal. Each goes against the minimal HUD, the 2026-10-18 "no world refresh" call or the "numeric goal that changes nothing" pitfall.
