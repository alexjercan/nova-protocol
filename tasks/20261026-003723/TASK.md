## Implemented 2026-11-02 (429a7a8d1, 304f45e3d, a9ed5bb35, 2a57fba43)
- Station traffic (`crates/nova_world_base/src/clusters.rs`): the cell that owns a living station plans `STATION_TRAFFIC_HULLS` (2) hulls 2 to 4 km from it on its own `station_traffic` stream, after every other body, so no other body moves or changes id. Ids `<cluster>_traffic_<i>`; slots from 64, apart from every cluster hull's streams. A hull past its cluster's extent or failing the cell checks is skipped like any hull; it needs no companion and takes a patrol. It belongs to the station's civilization (`HullLineageType::Station`), so it never spawns hostile or as a wreck beside an Ally start station.
- Departure lifecycle (`crates/nova_world_base/src/station_visits.rs`): after the 60 s dwell the visit enters `Departing`; the station stays reserved while the ship flies a Move order 1 km past both hull envelopes; the visit ends when it reports, another order replaces it, 300 s pass or the station leaves the window. The next visitor never plans its corridor through a departing hull. DockApproach and NoSafeDockPath are unchanged.
- Failed approach (2a57fba43): a Dock order that fails or a 900 s walked approach moves the visit to `Withdrawing`; the ship flies clear like a departing visitor and the station stays reserved until it stands 1 km past both hulls. A departing or withdrawing ship that already stands that far out frees the station at once. Reproduced first in a seed 7 New Game play: a failed visitor sat 0 m along the corridor and the next visitor bound the same frame and failed NoSafeDockPath.
- Continuity: `StationVisit::live_s` counts only walked live time; the 900 s approach, the 60 s dwell and the 300 s departure read it, so a frozen or saved visit resumes with the time it had left.
- Load: a living station that offers Trade must carry a `TraderMarket` that `TraderMarket::validate` accepts (`check_saved_markets`), so a malformed save refuses before the writer would panic.
- Proof: `a_frozen_visit_resumes_its_approach_and_dwell_with_the_time_they_had_left`, `a_departing_visitor_keeps_its_station_until_it_flies_clear`, `a_load_refuses_a_living_trade_station_with_no_market_or_an_unpriceable_one`, `a_living_station_cell_plans_two_own_traffic_hulls_and_moves_no_other_body`, `a_failed_approach_keeps_its_station_until_its_ship_stands_clear`.
- Matched census (disposable scratch test, same tree with traffic 0 vs 2, seeds 1-40, real base content, 125-cell start window): seeds with a profiled visitor within 20 km 10 -> 36; profiled ships within 20 km 15 -> 73; intact ships per window summed 251 -> 315 (+25%, at most 2 per living station); Enemy ships within 20 km 3 -> 3 (cluster hulls only). ## Implemented 2026-11-03 (c570f0f1e, 741a9f6c8, cadd7f240, ff95d5510)
- Station-frame forecast (c570f0f1e, `crates/nova_ship/src/flight/dock_forecast.rs`): a DOCK approach steps its station and every field body for 90 s under the dominant-well rule, once per second per docking ship, and keeps each body's path relative to the station as spheres. Leg A routes round them; a corridor whose centre-of-mass segment they come within clearance of is refused (NoSafeDockPath naming the body). A planet the station orbits closely becomes the ring of its orbit. The near-cap crawl starts at 2 u, not 6 u.
- Corner search (cadd7f240, `navigation.rs` `search_leg`): leg A holds a corner while its leg is clear, else takes the shortest corner whose leg is clear and that sees the corridor entry, else the shortest clear pair (the 96 shortest of each, ff95d5510). Every corner stands outside each bubble by the clear clearance plus the arrival window. The push-out corner hopped between the planet ring and the station bubble (diagnosis `/tmp/nova-diag29/REPORT.md`, not kept).
- Deadline (cadd7f240, ff95d5510): armed on the first tick, then re-armed once, from a fresh search's whole route, on the first tick the hull flies within 2.6 u/s of the station's velocity. Approaches armed 13-26 u/s off the station overshot while braking and overran 4-6 u from the berth. A hull that never settles keeps its first deadline. Debug lines: `armed` / `settled its DOCK approach`.
- One approach per station (cadd7f240, `dock_turns`): an armed approach keeps the turn, else the lowest entity bits; the others hold the station's velocity with no deadline. Two hulls closing on one station both overran (seed 26).
- Proof: `a_dock_order_at_a_station_orbiting_close_to_a_planet_captures_without_touching_it` (R 148, planet 88.5 u, mu 24 700; at 3222c2cc4 the hull touched the planet at tick 3947, after it docks 124.9 u from the planet's centre), `a_planet_an_orbiting_station_circles_refuses_a_corridor_its_path_crosses`, `a_goal_past_a_ring_with_a_filled_hole_is_reached_by_clear_corners`, `a_dock_approach_armed_off_the_stations_velocity_rearms_once_when_it_settles`, `a_second_dock_approach_to_a_station_waits_until_the_first_ends`; `a_visitor_docked_at_an_orbiting_station_thaws_with_its_motion` freeze test in `crates/nova_world/src/tests/frozen.rs` (741a9f6c8; compares velocity and spin with a never-frozen control). `nova_ship` flight 204/204 at ff95d5510.
