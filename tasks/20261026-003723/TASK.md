## Implemented 2026-11-02 (429a7a8d1, 304f45e3d, a9ed5bb35, 2a57fba43)
- Station traffic (`crates/nova_world_base/src/clusters.rs`): the cell that owns a living station plans `STATION_TRAFFIC_HULLS` (2) hulls 2 to 4 km from it on its own `station_traffic` stream, after every other body, so no other body moves or changes id. Ids `<cluster>_traffic_<i>`; slots from 64, apart from every cluster hull's streams. A hull past its cluster's extent or failing the cell checks is skipped like any hull; it needs no companion and takes a patrol. It belongs to the station's civilization (`HullLineageType::Station`), so it never spawns hostile or as a wreck beside an Ally start station.
- Departure lifecycle (`crates/nova_world_base/src/station_visits.rs`): after the 60 s dwell the visit enters `Departing`; the station stays reserved while the ship flies a Move order 1 km past both hull envelopes; the visit ends when it reports, another order replaces it, 300 s pass or the station leaves the window. The next visitor never plans its corridor through a departing hull. DockApproach and NoSafeDockPath are unchanged.
- Failed approach (2a57fba43): a Dock order that fails or a 900 s walked approach moves the visit to `Withdrawing`; the ship flies clear like a departing visitor and the station stays reserved until it stands 1 km past both hulls. A departing or withdrawing ship that already stands that far out frees the station at once. Reproduced first in a seed 7 New Game play: a failed visitor sat 0 m along the corridor and the next visitor bound the same frame and failed NoSafeDockPath.
- Continuity: `StationVisit::live_s` counts only walked live time; the 900 s approach, the 60 s dwell and the 300 s departure read it, so a frozen or saved visit resumes with the time it had left.
- Load: a living station that offers Trade must carry a `TraderMarket` that `TraderMarket::validate` accepts (`check_saved_markets`), so a malformed save refuses before the writer would panic.
- Proof: `a_frozen_visit_resumes_its_approach_and_dwell_with_the_time_they_had_left`, `a_departing_visitor_keeps_its_station_until_it_flies_clear`, `a_load_refuses_a_living_trade_station_with_no_market_or_an_unpriceable_one`, `a_living_station_cell_plans_two_own_traffic_hulls_and_moves_no_other_body`, `a_failed_approach_keeps_its_station_until_its_ship_stands_clear`.
- Matched census (disposable scratch test, same tree with traffic 0 vs 2, seeds 1-40, real base content, 125-cell start window): seeds with a profiled visitor within 20 km 10 -> 36; profiled ships within 20 km 15 -> 73; intact ships per window summed 251 -> 315 (+25%, at most 2 per living station); Enemy ships within 20 km 3 -> 3 (cluster hulls only).
