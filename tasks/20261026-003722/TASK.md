- Owner requires a visiting NPC to **remain docked** across save/load and freeze/thaw. Save/load must preserve its dock, pose, order/visit phase, inventories, receipt, clocks and job state without a visible discontinuity or duplicate transfer; do not release a visitor as a save/retire shortcut.
- Owner approved: a completed visit adds a temporary extra job to the station board at once. It does not remove the three slot postings (`JOBS_PER_BOARD`, `crates/nova_gameplay/src/jobs.rs:89`). A board renewal removes extras the player has not accepted.

## Owner decisions 2026-11-02 (controlling)
- Genuine AI station docking; a visiting NPC stays docked across save/load/freeze/thaw with no pause, duplicate transfer or receipt. Committed DockApproach and strict NoSafeDockPath stay. No world-radius change.
- Passive thaw restock stays separate. A visit transfers actual cargo and credits; no off-window visit is fabricated; no cargo or credit is created.
- Authored trade profiles: assigned deterministically to generated ships and explicitly by scripts. Each profile has independent trade and job lists; either or both may be empty; an empty profile is valid traffic that only visits.
- Trades: multiple items, explicit buy/sell intention, quantity and price limit; ship cargo, credits, capacity, station stock and pricing constrain each transaction.
- A successful dock posts the profile's jobs in list order until the station holds 3 unaccepted extras; the rest are skipped, not queued. The 3 slot postings stay; extras never replace them. Accepted extras survive cleanup and expiry and stay completable. An unaccepted extra expires at the first window-caused slot renewal of its station or 20 play minutes after its posting; a hand-in or paid refresh does not clear extras.
- The owner authorized agent defaults for every other decision ("use defaults for rest").

## Selected defaults 2026-11-02 (agent, owner-authorized)
- Profile catalog: `Content::TradeProfile(TradeProfileDesign)` keyed by `TradeProfileDesignId`, registered as `GameTradeProfiles`; a mod replaces a profile by id like an item. Fields: `generated` (role, weight) draw weights, `trades` (item, `ItemTradeType` from the visitor's side, quantity, per-unit `limit_cr`), `jobs` (closed `VisitJobType::Procure { item, count, reward_cr }`). Lint then load refuse unknown items, zero quantity/limit/count/reward/weight, a repeated (item, side) and a repeated role.
- Assignment: `TraderMarket::profile: Option<TradeProfileDesignId>`. None on stations (generation refuses one) and on a ship whose role no profile lists. The planner draws it from its own stream `sector_ship_trade_profile`, by weight among profiles listing the role in id order, so no earlier draw moves. Scripts use `SetTradeProfile { id, profile }`; an unknown or non-trader ship refuses loudly.
- Visit selection: intact, unneutralized AI generated ship with a profile, no helm order, free of a pending visit, play clock past its next-visit stamp, picks the nearest live living generated station that offers Trade or Jobs, is not player-owned, is within 20 km and has no other pending visit. One visitor per station bounds docking traffic by live stations. First stamp: a seeded 60-300 s of play, counted from the start of play, so a ship first seen later is due at once; after each visit end: seeded 300-600 s.
- Lifecycle: bind (Approaching, Dock order key `<visit id>`) -> capture -> the same system runs the trade list then the job list and stores the receipt (Docked) -> 60 s dwell -> order cancel + release (Departing; the station stays reserved) -> a Move order 1 km past both hulls' envelopes reports, another order replaces it, or 300 s pass (`VISIT_DEPART_MAX`) -> visit ends, next stamp set. Dwell, approach and departure limits count only live time the ship walked (`StationVisit::live_s`), so a frozen or saved visit resumes with the time it had left. A failed Dock order or no dock after 900 s of walked approach (`VISIT_APPROACH_MAX`) withdraws the visit with a warning and no transfer: the ship flies clear like a departing visitor before the station frees (2a57fba43). A script order, or a ship or station that stops being free AI traffic or a living station, ends the visit at once with a warning and no transfer; a visitor docked at a station that stops living is released; `ondestroyed` ends visits of a despawned body. After thaw or Load the writer installs the Dock order again from the ledger; a Docked visit is never traded again.
- Trade rule: each intention in list order, revalidated against live hold, credits and the station's priced quote; count = largest count up to quantity that `plan_item_trade` accepts with lot price within `limit_cr x count` (Buy at most, Sell at least). A zero count is skipped and recorded.
- Extra job ids: `JobId::source` is `Slot { slot, posting }` or `Visit { visit, index }` (index in the profile's job list). Extras are kept per station in `JobPostings` with their own stamp. No paid refresh for extras.
- Save: `WORLD_SAVE_VERSION` string unchanged (dev layout change refuses as another build). Load refuses a visit naming no saved ship or station, a visit stamp after the clock, an unknown profile, a receipt job that is not the visit's own, a visit posting under another station or a slot id, and a posted or held visit job that no docked receipt lists and no ended visit could have posted. A receipt exists only in the Docked and Departing phases by type.

## Implemented 2026-11-02 (9fb0a8760, example 826d0187e)
- Catalog: `TradeProfileDesign` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/trade_profile.rs`), `Content::TradeProfile`, merge into `GameTradeProfiles` with faults as lint errors (`crates/nova_assets/src/merge.rs`), five base profiles from `crates/nova_authoring/src/base_content/trade_profiles.rs` -> generated `assets/base/trade_profiles/base.content.ron`.
- Assignment: `TraderMarket::profile`, drawn in `NovaLayeredWorld::generate` (`crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs`, `sector_ships::draw_trade_profile`); persists through freeze/thaw/save inside `TraderMarket`. Generation refuses a station with a profile. Script: `SetTradeProfile` (`crates/nova_scenario/src/actions/ship.rs`), lint refuses an unknown profile.
- Writer: `drive_station_visits` + `end_visits_on_loss` (`crates/nova_world_base/src/station_visits.rs`), chained after the thaw restock and stance sync, before `post_station_boards`. Ledger: `StationVisits` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/station_visits.rs`) with phases Approaching / Docked { receipt }. A visit also ends when a script replaces its Dock order before the capture.
- Extras: `JobId::source` Slot/Visit, `JobPostings` visit postings, `VISIT_EXTRAS_MAX` 3, `VISIT_EXTRA_OFFER_SECONDS` 1200. Only a slot renewal whose reason is `expired` retires open extras (`crates/nova_world_base/src/jobs.rs`); a hand-in, a drop or a paid refresh does not. Jobs UI reads `JobPostings::remaining` for both sources.
- Save: `WorldSaveState::station_visits`; Load refuses unknown ship/station, a stamp after the clock, an unknown profile on a visit or a saved trader, a station market with a profile, and a living Trade station with no valid market (`check_saved_markets`). `open_world`/`check_world` take `GameTradeProfiles`.
- Selected defaults not in the list above: a drop-caused slot renewal keeps open extras (only natural expiry retires them); an extra posted on the renewal frame stands; a frozen visitor keeps its station reserved until it thaws or its approach times out (no off-window fabrication); extras show no paid-refresh `X`; the visit limits count walked live time, not frozen play time (429a7a8d1); Load refuses a living station that offers Trade with no `TraderMarket` or with one `TraderMarket::validate` refuses, the same check generation runs (`check_saved_markets`, 429a7a8d1).
