Nova Protocol / design research / OPEN

A ship visits. The station changes.

One connected slice for purposeful traffic, finite stock movement, and job renewal. This is a decision document, not an approved interface or a claim that the feature exists.

Executive recommendation

Contract first in development; connected slice at release.

Define and prove a named station-visit/transfer commitment before routing ships. Then make an autonomous ship physically travel to a living station and produce a visible, conserved stock change. Only after that connect eligible job renewals to the same accepted visit. Do not ship an unused event, a randomly changing market with no arrival, or a hull that appears at the station without flying there.

Traffic first? Not as a standalone release. Traffic without a verified station arrival and consumer remains patrol with a new destination. A traffic-first plan cannot assume generic AI station docking; source shows an admission/request mechanism and pre-parked hull berthing, not a generic AI flight-to-dock order. Docking is a separate capability to approve, not a free premise. See two bounded visit choices.

End state: an observed arrival changes a real inventory and can make an eligible offer change. Retain existing held jobs, paid refresh and identity rules. No active-radius change. This report does not approve any interface, rate, budget or new test.

01 / What the code does now

Station board

JobPostings stores session boards, slots, posting numbers and stamps; a world save carries it. Held jobs pin slots. Hand-in, paid refresh or expired windows renew eligible slots; dropped offers wait for their window unless refreshed. Station processing waits for the station's cell and its 26 neighbors to settle, then sorts stations by ID. crates/nova_gameplay/src/jobs.rs:107-148,164-211,239-326; crates/nova_world_base/src/jobs.rs:133-154,222-355; crates/nova_world_base/src/save/mod.rs:286-313.

Stock and prices

Current passive stock recovery runs on thaw only, for a trader whose hold just lands. It changes items toward a target, subject to room, and skips dead hulls. There is no active-window event-driven restock in that system. Price reads held count versus target. Player Buy/Sell already plans bilateral cargo and credit changes. crates/nova_world_base/src/economy.rs:1-79; crates/nova_gameplay/src/market.rs:1-21,446-470; crates/nova_gameplay/src/inventory.rs:953-1005,2598-2610.

Movement is real but not a station visit

Generated intact ships have AI, stock and stable seeded slot draws. Their seeded patrol tries four nearby marks with holds; fewer than two valid marks leaves no loop. ShipOrderDirective::Move and AutopilotAction::Goto can approach a target or mark, but no dock directive appears in the ship-order variants. crates/nova_world_base/src/sector_ships.rs:1-33,65-87,142-154,358-393; crates/nova_ship/src/flight/order.rs:48-139; crates/nova_ship/src/flight/state.rs:391-411.

Docking is not autonomous routing

The station has dock admission, including occupied/hostile refusal. A DockingConnectionRequest exists; the parked-hull path places hulls at berths and requests connection. That is not proof an ordinary AI flies to a station, selects a free port and docks. Existing OnDocked reports a real clamp and OnShipOrderComplete reports a completed flight order, not a trade. crates/nova_ship/src/sections/docking_section/admission.rs:1-75; crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/parked_hulls.rs:646-703; crates/nova_scenario/src/events.rs:132-180.

Sequence comparison. Traffic-first demands a real moving ship, but cannot treat a docked parked hull or docking request as a ready-made autonomous docking policy. Event-first addresses the missing contract and thaw-only recovery, but is incomplete if the contract ships without a physical producer and an observable consumer. The recommended development sequence uses both constraints; release is coupled.

03 / Proposed contract and invariants

All names, shapes, ownership, precedence, error rules and tests below are candidates, not approved APIs. The owning interface would be set first on implementation; compiler errors and caller searches would drive updates. The slice owner is the generated world for route/visit and saved state; the inventory owner validates bilateral movement; the board owner applies slot policy. Do not add an adapter for an unshipped format.

Candidate event shape

StationVisitTransferCommitted

Candidate fields: visit identity derived from world seed, stable generated ship ID, stable station ID and a monotonic visit ordinal; item design ID, direction, actual whole-unit count, and observed gate (docked pair or approved standoff). Event means the mutation already committed, never merely planned. Do not use ECS Entity as a durable ID. An approach, refused entry, interrupted order or duplicate receipt emits no commitment.

Candidate owner: world transaction creates one durable receipt and applies both holds in one decision. Observers get a fact, not permission to apply the inventory change twice. Consumer: market stock/pricing immediately; eligible job posting change follows under approved policy. See stable generated identities and current posting IDs in crates/nova_world_base/src/sector_ships.rs:1-10 and crates/nova_gameplay/src/jobs.rs:111-149.

Candidate gate + failure

Prove presence, then move stock

Require a live intact ship and live service station, compatible stance, a completed physical approach, the chosen proximity/connection gate, available source units and destination capacity. Plan quantity from both holds. Commit ship decrement + station increment (or reverse) with no negative stock; keep finite station stock. Refuse with an inspectable reason if absent, hostile, blocked, full or empty. Never teleport or silently grant items. Station destruction or loss during approach aborts; route retreats to a safe eligible state or refuses visibly, without a fake visit. Existing station admission has hostility and occupied-port precedence: crates/nova_ship/src/sections/docking_section/admission.rs:33-75.

State and order: a receipt is a replay barrier

  1. Entry: seeded generated route picks a station by stable ID within the existing live window, with a valid ship and available service. Route is authored/seeded without a new default rate; placement, hostile status and ports are checked again at use time. Preserve seeded ship slot identity across skipped cells.
  2. Transit: the hull flies under existing movement physics. A completed Move is only an approach result, not proof of docking. Only the selected visit gate can commit. Combat interruption, lost route or station failure cannot count as arrival. crates/nova_ship/src/flight/order.rs:67-131; crates/nova_ship/src/input/ai/mission.rs:1-64.
  3. Commit: before board evaluation and save snapshot, validate and atomically move real units. Record a stable receipt with transaction status. Update visible inventory/price. Board reads committed receipts only; keep held-slot precedence and paid refresh behavior. One visit must not repeatedly renew all slots. Decide precise job precedence and whether a visit selects one eligible slot before implementation.
  4. Retire/load: freeze holds, route state and receipt/progress together; restore before observing new arrivals. On replay of the same identity, no second inventory mutation or board renewal. Save currently carries frozen sectors, postings and clock; extending that snapshot requires a design-approved format decision. crates/nova_world_base/src/save/mod.rs:280-316; crates/nova_world/src/frozen.rs:676-735.
Do not count twice. Today restock_thawed_traders modifies stock only on thaw. A visit-managed station must not also receive an unexplained thaw refill of the same units. Choose an explicit source/sink policy for passive recovery (replace/scope it for affected holds, or account for separately); do not call passive recovery "ship cargo." Off-window cells remain frozen: no invisible visits, transfers, or catch-up visit receipts. Existing passive thaw recovery stays a distinct mechanism only where approved. Active-window stock changes must come from actual visits/trades. crates/nova_world_base/src/economy.rs:1-79; crates/nova_world/src/frozen.rs:676-735.

Offer windows: do not erase the current board's three slots, incrementing posting IDs or their saved stamps. A held job remains pinned, a paid refresh keeps its fee and next-pass request semantics, and a completed job continues to renew. Decide whether a visit renews one unheld, unexpired slot, only a naturally due slot, or adds eligible variety without resetting its timer. No rate, weighted mix, new item, job type or renewal precedence is approved. crates/nova_gameplay/src/jobs.rs:107-148,164-211,239-326; crates/nova_world_base/src/jobs.rs:222-355.

04 / What counts as a visit?

Option A / larger, strongest intent

Actual autonomous station docking

Build a new AI port-approach, berth-selection and connection capability. Commit only after the real dock clamp, then transfer real cargo. Existing admission and occupied-port refusals apply. On undock the ship flies away. This meets literal "ship visits station" and supports a clear docking visual. It is not implemented today by the parked-hull berthing helper. Bound the first slice to one route, one selected station and one commodity; scope route recovery, port loss and collision safety before approval.

Evidence: crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/parked_hulls.rs:646-703; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/docking_section/connection.rs:275-332.

Option B / smaller, weaker promise

Approach and verified standoff exchange

Fly a real hull to a safe station-relative standoff. Check arrival distance and speed over a bounded hold, station validity and capacity; show a short cargo-transfer affordance, then commit the bilateral stock movement and depart. No dock claim and no OnDocked proxy. This is lower cost, but may not meet an intent to watch AI dock; label it a rendezvous, not docking. If a safe gate is not physically reachable, refuse and depart instead of teleporting.

Evidence: crates/nova_ship/src/flight/state.rs:391-411; crates/nova_ship/src/flight/order.rs:67-84.

Recommendation: approve A if the requested fantasy specifically includes docking; otherwise approve B only with the explicit rendezvous wording and a convincing inspected visual. Do not quietly substitute B for A during implementation.

05 / Delivery, proof and cost

1. Design the transaction boundary

Decide event name/fields, stable receipt identity, owning saved state, validation order, refusal policy, applicable stock and the sole write path. Propose the smallest named unit/ECS tests for conservation, duplicate receipt, lost station and hostile refusal for approval. Do not ship this step by itself. Audit all runtime-ID consumers and saved-version behavior; invalid authored item/route IDs fail lint, then load, not by silent fallback.

2. Make one physical route and immediate consequence

Use a generated ship with real stock and a stable destination ID. Respect current world streaming and AI helm authority. Implement the approved A or B visit gate, with a departure/retreat. Apply one conserved transfer and make the stock/price delta visible at the station; the trace must show the ship travel, gate, receipt, before/after holds and no duplicate on replay. If this is only a background event, it is not done.

3. Connect a bounded job response

Board policy reads the same committed receipt, not an invented periodic ship. Update eligible offers while keeping held slots and paid refresh windows. Keep generated JobId station/slot/posting identity, settled-neighbor gate and deterministic station order. Remove superseded refresh/restock paths only after explicit policy approval; preserve current mechanisms that remain valid.

4. Save, expose, verify, then expand

Persist route/receipt alongside holds and postings; test freeze, thaw, load and revisit without reapplication. Add a small map/radar ship destination cue and one market stock-delta marker or changed stock/price line; use existing panes, not prose-heavy UI. Audit authoring builders, generated content, UI, docs and changelog only in the future implementation. No generated base RON hand edit.

Risks and explicit failure rules

What dies: only approved, superseded passive stock/renewal paths for the affected stock or offer; no parallel recharge that duplicates a shipment. What can break: AI pathing, port occupancy, combat interruption, station inventory and pricing, job IDs, saved state, encounter density and frame time. Fail loudly: invalid authored item, destination, ship or route identity at lint/load; incompatible saved schema at load under an approved migration policy. Runtime refusal: missing/dead station, hostility, occupied berth, blocked leg, inadequate source stock or capacity produce no commitment; display a short inspectable refusal/retreat. Do not change active radius, assume off-window simulation, or invent throughput and cargo values.

Approval required / no defaults implied

Owner decisions before any gameplay change

  1. Visit gate: A - genuine new autonomous station docking, or B - physical approach and explicitly named standoff exchange? Recommendation: A for literal docking intent; B only if rendezvous is acceptable.
  2. Renewal trigger: A - a visit selects one eligible unheld offer before its ordinary window, or B - visits only influence the next ordinary eligible renewal? Decide how paid refresh/hand-in precedence works. Recommendation: B for first slice to avoid surprise resets; require observable stock consequence immediately.
  3. Stock source and off-window rule: A - visit-managed stock receives only conserved active transfers and existing thaw recovery is scoped away for it, or B - retain distinct explicitly accounted passive source/sink? Recommendation: A for the first station transfer, freeze off-window.
  4. Contract and proof: approve or rename the proposed committed-visit identity, receipt owner, refusal policy, save format handling and named tests. Approve seeded route density, cargo quantity, visual cue and matched-reference resource budget explicitly; this document supplies no default value.
