## Proposed loop, in dependency order (not approved)

1. **Make one visited place matter.** Decide what persists across sector retirement, retry, quit, version/content changes and platform storage. Minimum useful state is the player's ship condition and explicit consumed/claimed world-body identity before introducing mineable rewards. The existing seed still reproduces *pristine* sectors; it cannot tell whether a rock has been mined or a hull salvaged. Test retire/revisit/restart, no double pickup, a changed mod/content set, and an interrupted write. Design in `20260824-125938` and `125943` together; do not invent a `WorldState` format in either task.
2. **Give one wreck or rock a choice, not just a counter.** Potential loop: identify an interesting field or inert hull, risk a physical approach, claim a bounded useful material/component, then choose repair, trade, or a ship modification at a dock. Cargo capacity/volume could make ship design matter, but it is not chosen yet; material IDs, ownership of the claim, and disposal at death need decisions. An always-available free repair would erase the repair payoff. Proof: collect once, leave/revisit, use the resource at a destination, see the ship's changed capability, and refuse repeat claims. Do not ship collectible filler to pad empty space.
3. **Make docking an actual destination.** A station candidate is a specially identified, dockable authored or generated ship/structure with bounded services and a themed surface; it is not a generic inert derelict. Start by choosing one service after the free-repair/reload policy, then test docking and undocking, broken-port auto-release (`release_broken_docking_connections`), correct partner identification, state changes and no service from the wrong hull. Any in-progress service needs rollback/refusal when `OnUndocked` fires. Fixed authored hub versus streamed station is an owner decision with different residency and mutation costs.
4. **Make travel and conflict readable.** Distinct fields could offer high-yield danger, a quiet recovery route or an encounter opportunity; explicit map/chart cues should communicate the difference before travel. Small faction/fleet reactions could reuse existing Allegiance/contact/patrol mechanics before a standing simulation. If encounters change on repeated visits, require stable event IDs, a visit/time policy and persistence so the player cannot farm reloads. Distinguish an authored mission trigger from a persistent world event.
5. **Offer a tangible ship project.** Candidate: recover a part or design and fit it at an appropriate service, changing the actual section graph/mass/power/controls. There is no live section-swap runtime. Decide whether a complete design swap is a smaller first proof than per-section retrofits; persist changed identity and damage; reject invalid connections, lost equipment and unsafe swaps loudly. Preserve the physically credible ship-flying focus.

The order above is a hypothesis about dependencies, not a release schedule. These loops should be tested for pleasure, readability and scarcity via player flows before expanding to economy/factions. Avoid crew simulation and skill trees/level-ups: they distract from controlling a physical ship. Do not promise fuel, quests, credits or ammo trading just because a station could display them.
