# Comparable games and Nova's progression hypothesis

Research, 2026-09-25. These are **mechanic comparisons**, not borrowed designs, endorsed assets, or promises to add them. Official game/store descriptions were fetched on 2026-09-25 with `curl`; third-party wikis and the asset scout's unverified claims were not used as evidence. Games were not played for this spike. No competitor's code or art is included.

**Nova differentiator to test:** the player's physically piloted, damageable modular ship changes as the result of *where and how it flies*, not a level-up or a free inventory counter. The published Saturn setting supports legitimate independent salvage, powerful station employers, processing/transport bottlenecks and local conflicts (`web/src/lore/economy.md:25-75`). Civilian workships are normally unarmed; companies have local escorts, not independent sovereign fleets (`web/src/lore/law-and-power.md:36-60`). Factions should be organizations and situated relationships, not a replacement three-color label alone. Proposals must leave room for lawful salvage permits, contested claims, and protection contracts without making every derelict a pirate or every contractor a combatant.

## Candidate experience slices for owner review

1. **The useful field.** Locate a bounded asteroid group, interpret scanners/geometry, pick a safe trajectory, recover one candidate material, carry it home. Mining is a ship piloting and exposure problem before it is an ore-pricing problem. Decision: extraction by existing weapons/carving versus a dedicated tool; a gun should not silently award resources. Proof: two distinctly typed rocks lead to different navigational/cargo decisions, and a claimed body stays claimed after sector return if persistence is promised. This depends on fixed gravity/orbits if rocks become mobile.
2. **The choice at the dock.** A station serves as a definite, reachable place with a UI for inspection and one costed, visible service; the alternative is to retain free repair and sell another service. Source conflict: free `Repair`/`Reload` anywhere and base ammo idle refill (`crates/nova_os_ui/src/ship/sections.rs:542-592`; `crates/nova_authoring/src/base_content/sections/mod.rs:302-325`). Proof: undocked/wrong-partner refusal, meaningful before/after hull change, no double charge or duped goods, equivalent mouse/keyboard selection. Do not add an untested multi-currency market first.
3. **Physical ship project.** Obtain or buy a component/design at a dock; fitter checks graph, collision, mass/power and missing parts; returning to flight shows altered handling and silhouette. Current editor saves authored designs, not live ship modifications (`crates/nova_editor/src/bundle.rs`). A full design exchange and one bounded part swap are *alternative* first slices, not concurrent requirements. Proof: refit/ship-change survives retirement and a save if claimed; invalid fit refuses before losing stock or corrupting physics.
4. **Consequential encounter.** Rather than random hostile spam, a claim or route can produce an identifiable patrol, escort request, salvage dispute or damaged neutral hull; relation and reward reflect a named organization. Current `Allegiance` is `Player/Enemy/Neutral` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/relations.rs:26-61`), and authored scenarios have triggers, but world-scoped encounter state does not exist. Proof: stable encounter identity, no duplicate reward on re-entry, no farm-on-reload, and AI resumes route after contact. No authority for a new faction schema yet.
5. **Distinctive destinations and hulls.** Few known stations/services and a catalog of recognizable lawful workships, escorts and rarer damaged hulls support routes and encounters. Compare reusable hand-authored WFC-derived hulls against runtime WFC only after measuring failure and preparation costs. Existing ship derelicts in the generator are not stations; 'derelict-only' groups currently contain no asteroid (`crates/nova_world_base/src/clusters.rs:164-194,680-695`). Owner's proposed `P* A+ S*` per nonempty cluster needs a post-placement guarantee and a pacing test; an empty sector can remain intentional.

**Supplies and ammo:** food/oxygen/water supplies make sense in the lore but crew upkeep is not approved gameplay. A first supplies loop could represent consumables that matter to the *ship's operation* only if failure/repair consequences are designed; fuel/propellant mechanics are not currently defined. Base ammunition is an idle-replenished rate limit, so ammo purchases require an explicit redesign and combat-balance proof, not merely a shop button. Credits/trading are likewise new state, not free infrastructure.

**Quality gates before ranking tasks:** play a loop from discovery to physical reward to changed flight, observe confusion, repetition and exploitability, and compare with the same ship without the resource/service. A single coherent route is more useful than a broad list of 'quests'. The diagrams in `artifacts/` deliberately render the missing/contradictory edges and are not evidence that the proposed features work in Nova.
