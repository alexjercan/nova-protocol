Greeble surface solve: diagrams

Companion to RESEARCH.md in this folder. One solver cell per plate, four edge slots per cell, sockets on tile sides, a seeded min-entropy solve.

1. Cell graph across folds (cross-section)

Grey squares are structure cells. Each blue bar is a plate: it stands in an empty cell and faces one out direction. Arcs are graph links, coloured by edge context. The step plate and the deck plate meet a wall: the deck plate rises to full height and links up the wall (Concave). Open corners link round the fold (Convex). Where nothing continues the slot is a border.

2. Cell frame and socket matching

Every cell uses up = link_point_up(n) and right = up x n. Slots run E, N, W, S counter-clockwise seen from outside on every face, so a turn means the same on +Y and -Y. A tile with turn r shows sides[(k - r) mod 4] on slot k. Two cells agree when the two sockets on the shared edge are listed in each other's pairs and the edge context is allowed for both.

3. Solver loop

No RNG stream: every choice hashes (cell, out, style id, attempt). Hard rules are sockets (propagated) and factor 0.0 near terms (applied as bans when a tile is placed). Soft near terms scale weights at draw time.
