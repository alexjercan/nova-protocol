# Static-well gravity: orbit and migration research (2026-09-25)

Read-only source research against master `f802ca58a`. No simulation, test, scenario replay, or timing comparison was run. This document gives design alternatives, not permission to edit gameplay.

## What exists and what would die

`crates/nova_gameplay/src/gravity.rs:376-459` applies a **single dominant** static well's central acceleration per fixed step to an opted-in rigid body. It explicitly excludes any entity also carrying `GravityWell`; the per-body query loops over all loaded wells. `DominantWell` is tracked with hysteresis, and a well's removal clears the owner reference. `well_accel` fades outside the core, clamps inside the surface, and `GravityWell::from_mass` limits surface acceleration (`gravity.rs:64-108,119-138,174-217,306-373`). Gun rounds use separate non-rigid-body well sampling (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/rounds.rs:1194-1223`). Ships with player or AI pilot and torpedoes opt in; unpiloted hulls, small asteroids and debris do not (`gravity.rs:110-125,252-278`; `crates/nova_ship/src/input/ai/mod.rs:347`). Thus projectile attraction and one-way static gravity **already work**; the missing change is body participation and safe initial state.

Well creation currently doubles as a pinning rule: an asteroid with `mass: Some(...)`, even `Some(0.0)`, or an implicit mass when radius is >=50 m, gains both `GravityWell` and `RigidBody::Static` (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/asteroid.rs:468-491`, `gravity.rs:194-217`). Planet wells also pin planet bodies (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/planet.rs:191-212`), including authored moons whose authored mass yields little meaningful reach. Anchors may carry an inert zero-mass well. The shipped `webmods/the-ledger/*.ron` uses explicitly zero-mass asteroids for pinned scenery. Deleting mass or equating 'well' and 'moving rock' without an authored-content migration breaks those pins. `crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:183-246` spawns streamed rocks with `mass: None`, so approximately the 50-60 m part of the base generator's 30-60 m nominal radius band becomes static wells (`crates/nova_world_base/src/clusters.rs:82,613`). This fraction is an unmeasured distribution inference, not a spawn census.

The user wants immovable **planet/anchor** wells to pull *every mobile ship, projectile and asteroid*, not mobile-body attraction or N-body simulation. They clarified that the existing neutral-hull and rock exemptions are problems to eliminate, not optional permanent shortcuts. It is an open decision how explicit asteroid wells and pinned props migrate to true stationary sources; some authored content depends on them today. 'All ships' adds neutral/derelict hulls, changing a baseline in which these were deliberately unaffected. 'All asteroids' requires separating any pinned well source from the mobile asteroid category. Carved pieces and section debris need classification by what kind of mobile body they actually are, not exemption by spawn path. Performance must be addressed through measured algorithms, spatial filtering or bounded active physics, not by leaving some mobile ships or asteroids immune.

## Orbital stability is not guaranteed by spawning near a well

A circular tangent speed `sqrt(mu/r)` is available (`gravity.rs:337`), and ORBIT chooses an unfaded ring (`crates/nova_ship/src/flight/guidance.rs:259-314`). No authored or streamed asteroid spawn sets an orbital `LinearVelocity` (`crates/nova_scenario/src/actions/spawn.rs:75-114`, `crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:183-246`). A newly opted-in body starts at rest and will fall toward a well. Adding a tangent velocity is necessary for a free circular orbit but not sufficient for a *stable mined field*: collision, surface clamp, SOI fade, overlap of wells, a collision deflection, and integration error all change the orbit.

In overlap, ORBIT plans circular speed around its target while the rigid-body physics follows only the dominant well (`crates/nova_ship/src/flight/autopilot.rs:710`, `gravity.rs:448-457`). A planning path also sums gravitational pulls despite single-well physics (`autopilot.rs:358-364`). Streamed asteroid wells inside a planetoid's SOI make this a concrete overlap risk; its frequency and in-game effect need a measured census and ORBIT run. Do not claim stable orbits from formula inspection alone.

A cluster member may be owned by a different sector from its parent (`crates/nova_world_base/src/clusters.rs:743,772`), and independently ordered cell materialization/retirement can show a mobile body before the well or unload the well first (`crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:463,653-680`). Dynamic motion can then cross the sector boundary; reloading currently regenerates pristine seed positions (`crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:18-22`). Neither a plausible analytic phase nor a persistent physical trajectory exists yet. A player mining and returning to a field raises the same world-state question in `tasks/20260824-125938/TASK.md` and `tasks/20260824-125943/TASK.md`.

## Options to prototype, not decide yet

1. **Dynamic at spawn:** position a rock on a well-centered orbit with seeded orbital plane and velocity. Gravity and impacts remain real. Require a well present before activation, a stable interior ring, collision-free trajectories over tested periods, and a rule for wells that retire while an orbiter lives. The simulation's SOI policy matters more than an ideal two-body formula.
2. **Seeded analytic rails far away, dynamic near play:** keep a body on an explicit static-well orbit described by elements and epoch; promote it to one physics body before any collision or interaction. This can keep sparse distant scenery coherent through streaming, but changes the collision and identity model and needs promotion/demotion and persistence tests. It must still use static wells, not N-body forces.
3. **Migrate pinned backdrops to true stationary planet/anchor sources:** preserve intentional immovable objects through an explicit source/prop role while changing all *mobile* asteroid spawns to gravity participants. Do not keep a covert gravity-immune asteroid category; validate every shipped zero-mass rock use before converting it.

Do not tie `mass` silently to `RigidBody::Static` if the selected model needs a body both massive and mobile; conversely, do not release the Ledger's pinned rocks or authored planets before a reviewed migration. A preliminary two-stage content conversion (explicit pinning; then affected-body/orbit policy) is an **agent proposal** only, not owner approval. Rebuild generated base RON from Rust builders, lint loaded and hand-authored mods, and refuse invalid gravity quantities before physics.

## Named proof gates

- **Baseline inventory:** enumerate shipped pinned planets, rocks/zero-mass pins, anchors, free neutral hulls and projectiles; measure location and SOI proximity in repeated seeded windows. Preserve current scenario and frame artifacts before modification.
- **Physics example:** compare pure circular/tangent and real fixed-step orbits over multiple periods in a gravity field including fade and clamp, rock/planet SOI overlap, collision perturbation, and a no-well region. Assert radius/energy/identity/collision state, not a wall-time threshold. Verify a 50 m class rock and a neutral unpiloted hull actually *move and are pulled by the same well*, including after contacts and sector lifecycle changes.
- **Streaming lifecycle:** assert one physical owner per moving body, initial collision readiness after its well, no dangling `DominantWell` after retirement, and a defined return phase/mutation policy; exercise opposite materialization orders. Preserve atomic sector readiness.
- **Shipped flow:** test authored fixed-well scenarios and the Ledger's zero-mass rocks, free-world neutral hulls, planet-plus-rock ORBIT/arrival, and projectiles/asteroid carving. Inspect rendered results for space readability.
- **Performance:** compare matched quiet-host repeat sets with a named reference for wells x affected bodies, awake physics/contact work and sector fill; no assertions on elapsed time or optimization claims from one capture.

Risks not yet checked: floating-origin/proximity physics, cross-platform orbit phase bit identity, saved orbit epoch/clock representation, zero/negative/non-finite mass load validation, and whether carving fragments should inherit gravity. These are decisions and prototypes, not assumptions of correctness.
