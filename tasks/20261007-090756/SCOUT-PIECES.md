# Detached section-piece source map

## Boundary and durable inputs

- The inspected tree is `/home/alex/.cache/sprouts/nova-protocol/resumable-worlds`, branch `resumable-worlds`. Only the requested report path was written; no Cargo command or stage operation was run. [branch verified with `git branch --show-current`]
- `SectionKind` has nine kinds: Hull, Thruster, Controller, Turret, Torpedo, Railgun, Docking, CargoIntake, and Mining. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:442-459]
- The destroyed explodable's world transform becomes the new piece transform; it is not a child of the old ship. The piece gets `DetachedPieceMarker(old_entity)`, `Name`, `Transform`, visible state, kinematic body, `CenterOfMass`, `NoAutoCenterOfMass`, inherited-plus-kick linear velocity, random angular velocity, `ChunkGrace`, and `TempEntity`. [crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:395-400,420-447]
- The detacher reparents every direct child to that piece, preserves deeper parent links, recursively strips `Collider` from every descendant, then despawns the original section. It does not mesh, copy, or rebuild the art. [crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:450-477]
- The piece root gets no `Health`, `SectionMarker`, or `SectionBuildConfig`; the detacher's root bundle lists its components, while `SectionBuildConfig` is inserted on the original section by `section_body`. [crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:420-447; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:534-545,559-564]
- `SectionBuildConfig(SectionConfig)` retains the resolved content config on the original section. It is the source of authored asset refs, section-kind config, base collider, damage effects, and authored animation tracks, but the detacher does not copy it. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:534-545,559-564,618-647]
- Render-mesh configs store an `AssetRef` and optional `RenderMeshTransform`; observers resolve the ref into `WorldAssetRoot` and place the transform on the child, so source config is needed for a durable asset path and authored local transform. `SectionRenderOf(Entity)` is instead a runtime back-reference to the old section entity. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:314-337; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/hull_section.rs:105-127]
- **Unverified asset detail:** this map inventories the section-authored wrapper entities, not every node/material created when referenced GLTF scenes resolve. The checked section code inserts `WorldAssetRoot`; the resolved scene's internal `Mesh3d`, `MeshMaterial3d`, and node tree depend on the referenced asset. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/hull_section.rs:105-127; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/render.rs:327-357]

## Per-kind child tree at detach

The lists below describe source-created children. Any extra descendants inside an authored scene are asset-dependent as noted above.

- **Hull:** one `Hull Section Body` child. Authored path: `HullSectionConfig.render_mesh`; local pose: `SectionRenderMeshTransform`; authored branch adds `Name`, local `Transform`, `SectionRenderOf(old_section)`, `WorldAssetRoot`; fallback adds `Mesh3d` and `MeshMaterial3d` from `PlaceholderArt`. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/hull_section.rs:87-127]
- Any section can have ship-skin plate fixture children; decor fixtures are children of plates. A plate carries `SectionFixture`, `ShipSkinMarker(ShellShape)`, pose, `Health`, and collider; a decor root carries `SectionFixture`, `ShipDecorMarker(AssetRef)`, `DecorColliderSize`, pose, `Health`, and collider. Each plate surface is a child with `SkinSurfaceMarker`, `Mesh3d`, and `MeshMaterial3d`. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/shell_skin.rs:618-655,679-700,1080-1109; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_decor.rs:550-585]
- **Controller:** one `Controller Section Body` child in the authored branch, or two children (`Controller Section Body (A)` and `Controller Section Window (B)`) in the placeholder branch. The authored child has `Name`, configured local `Transform`, `SectionRenderOf(old_section)`, and `WorldAssetRoot`; placeholders have `Mesh3d` and `MeshMaterial3d`. Config path and pose are on controller config/render-transform components. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/controller_section.rs:576-668]
- **Turret:** the section owns the recursively configured `TurretJoint` tree. Each joint has `Name`, `TurretSectionPartOf(old_turret)`, render ref/pose, offset `Transform`, and inherited visibility; hinged joints also have `SmoothLookRotation` and `TurretSectionAimHeading`; muzzle joints also have muzzle marker, fire timer, and effect ref. Each joint may have an art child (`WorldAssetRoot`, or placeholder `Mesh3d`/material for structural unmeshed joints); muzzle points have no placeholder. A configured muzzle-effect child has `ParticleEffect` and `EffectProperties`. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/setup.rs:44-100; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/render.rs:294-359,664-719]
- **Torpedo:** two sibling children: `Torpedo Section Body` and `Torpedo Section Spawner`. Both carry `TorpedoSectionPartOf(old_section)`; the body has marker, identity transform, and inherited visibility. The spawner has marker, cooldown, launch-effect and sound refs, configured spawn offset/rotation, and visibility. The body gets one scene or placeholder mesh/material art child. The spawner can get a `Torpedo Launch Effect` child with `ParticleEffect` and `EffectProperties`. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/bay.rs:129-158; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/render.rs:16-68,760-800]
- **Railgun:** one authored-or-placeholder `Railgun Section Body` child plus a direct `Railgun Charge Glow` child. The body has the same render-child metadata as Hull; the glow has marker, transform, visibility, and `PointLight`. Charge position/brightness is runtime state. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/railgun_section/render.rs:170-235]
- **Thruster:** authored-or-placeholder body child(ren), plus a direct `Thruster Exhaust` child carrying `SectionRenderOf(old_section)`, exhaust shape/config, and configured transform. Render setup generates outer plume mesh/material and an inner plume child; the config carries geometry, dimensions, placement, colors, and plume values. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/thruster_section.rs:728-812,814-875]
- Thruster mesh generator is `exhaust_mesh(shape, hx, hz, height)`, called through `ExhaustMeshes::mesh`; its key stores geometry and the bit patterns of `hx`, `hz`, and height. The inner mesh uses the same generator and its own derived extents/height. Those inputs are recoverable from the exhaust config. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/thruster_section.rs:249-301,834-875]
- **Cargo intake:** one direct `Cargo Intake Section Body` scene child, with `Name`, authored render transform, `SectionRenderOf(old_section)`, and `WorldAssetRoot`. Its trigger is a separately spawned sensor with `CargoIntakeTriggerOf(intake)`, not a child, and linked-spawn relationship semantics remove it with its intake. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/cargo_intake_section.rs:194-200,389-447,622-643]
- **Mining:** one direct `Mining Section Body` scene child with `Name`, configured render transform, `SectionRenderOf(old_section)`, and `WorldAssetRoot`. Its emitter arm/door pose is driven through section animation tracks. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/mining_section.rs:205-232,271-292]
- **Docking:** one authored-or-placeholder `Docking Section Body` child with `Name`, configured render transform, `SectionRenderOf(old_section)`, and either `WorldAssetRoot` or placeholder `Mesh3d`/material. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/docking_section/render.rs:15-55]
- The section's configured render path/local transform are recoverable from its `SectionBuildConfig`; fallback placeholder meshes/materials are shared code resources, not authored per-piece data. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:534-545,559-564; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/hull_section.rs:105-127]

## Reproducibility and missing piece-only data

- Skin plate surface mesh generator: `ShellShape::surfaces()` returns per-surface meshes. `SkinAssets` caches meshes by `ShellShape` and materials by style id and surface role. A plate's `ShipSkinMarker` supplies shape; the `ShipStyle` id lives on the ship root, not on the plate or detached piece. Capture that id to reproduce the palette; no RNG is needed for a saved fixture tree. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/shell_shape.rs:460-518; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/shell_skin.rs:974-1037,1040-1077; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_style.rs:568-585]
- Scatter is deterministic from cell, outward face, and fixture id; style fixtures author model ref, collider size, placement, and health. Existing frozen fixture data stores plate shape or decor name/model/collider size, pose, health, and recursive fixture children. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_decor.rs:486-503,724-755; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_style.rs:239-274; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:111-148]
- Existing `FrozenSection` is not an art-only thaw: it captures section `Health`, ammo/state, animation tracks, turret hinge angles, and fixtures, then thaws onto a live section built from its config. Its frozen plate/decor constructors restore fixture `Health` and colliders. For the requested no-Health/no-section-gameplay result, reuse only the data facts, not the live-section body/thaw path. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:164-176,295-313,320-325; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/shell_skin.rs:658-676; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_decor.rs:588-613]
- `FrozenWreckFragment` separately serializes ship style and each surviving fragment section's config, pose, and `FrozenSection`; it is not a detached-piece snapshot, and its state still includes section Health. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/integrity.rs:157-186]
- **Not reproducible from the detached piece alone:** all nine kinds lose the `SectionBuildConfig` with the despawned source section; `ShipStyle` is on the ship root. The child `SectionRenderOf`, `TurretSectionPartOf`, and `TorpedoSectionPartOf` values reference old runtime entity IDs, not durable identities. They must not be treated as replayable data. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:334-337,534-545; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_style.rs:568-577; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/setup.rs:51-58; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/bay.rs:129-149]
- Skin surface materials are derived from style palette and role; damage-crack materials are derived from original material and damage bucket. These are rebuildable from config/style/source art, but the current child material handles are runtime assets, not durable asset IDs. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/shell_skin.rs:981-1035; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/damage_cracks.rs:356-423]
- `nose_cone_mesh(radius, body_length, nose_length)` is not part of any section's static art subtree. Its observed call sites build turret rounds, torpedoes, and railgun slugs; those are projectile entities, not section children. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/mod.rs:119-137; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/render.rs:184; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/render.rs:134; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/railgun_section/render.rs:94]
- Child fixture `Health` currently survives detachment because the recursive cleanup removes only `Collider`; the piece root itself has no `Health`. An art-only thaw must omit fixture gameplay components too. [crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:420-447,461-477; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/shell_skin.rs:628-655; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_decor.rs:566-585]

## Post-detach visual changes

- Damage cracks use a saved source material and damage bucket; when an async crack material resolves after its owner section is gone, the render code assigns the fully damaged bucket and drops the pending marker. This is an intentional post-detach art change. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/damage_cracks.rs:356-423]
- `DamageLevel` and the owning `Health` are on the dead section, so the normal live-section damage grader no longer supplies changing levels; the orphan material resolution path above handles late crack assets. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:618-647; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/damage_cracks.rs:373-384,408-423]
- Thruster plume appearance depends on damage level and elapsed time for flicker. The render updater detects that the detached exhaust has no owning section and sets wanted throttle to zero, then swaps to the zero bucket; capture the current bucket only to reproduce the instant before that update. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/damage_plume.rs:68-84; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/thruster_section.rs:641-679]
- Section animation resolution finds a section ancestor and its `SectionAnimations`; despawning that section stops its animation driver, leaving the already-written scene-node transforms as the detached pose. Existing freeze state records track progress/target; the track config and scene node names/transforms still come from `SectionBuildConfig` and its asset. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/section_animation.rs:408-423,426-493,495-510; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:164-176]
- Turret aiming/firing needs the turret root, but `sync_turret_joint_rotation` queries retained joint components directly and continues writing the saved hinge output onto joint transforms. Snapshot hinge target/output and tree identity if reproducing exact pose; the existing `FrozenTurretHinges` record does this without entity IDs. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/mod.rs:389-447; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/aim.rs:602-619; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/setup.rs:180-220]
- Railgun glow is spawned dark and updated from charge state; after root destruction that driver has no section state to query, so preserve the glow/charge pose only if the snapshot must match a charge already in progress. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/railgun_section/render.rs:214-255]
- All fixture and scene descendant colliders are stripped on detach, including plate/decor colliders. `ChunkGrace` carries the original section collider and computed clearance; clearance uses ancestor `IntegrityEnvelope`, transformed center of mass, current piece position, collider reach, and configured daylight margin. Recompute it before removing the ancestor, or store the result/collider in the detached record. [crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:175-203,410-418,441-445,461-470]

## Render-observer safety, closest proof, and open limits

- Most section render observers are registered only when their plugin's `render` flag is true and react to a kind/render marker; the hull observer needs its render config, placeholder resource, and asset server, not `Health`. Ship skin dressing similarly keys on `ShipSkinMarker` and needs shape/style/resources, not section Health. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/hull_section.rs:76-93; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/mining_section.rs:302-327; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/shell_skin.rs:1040-1077,1158-1170]
- An art-only bundle can invoke the render half if it supplies the marker/config/helper components and render resources. Do not add a full gameplay section marker under every section plugin: Torpedo's always-registered `insert_torpedo_section` builds a cooldown spawner/body, and turret setup builds a live joint tree; these are outside a no-gameplay art bundle. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/mod.rs:882-890; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/bay.rs:104-158; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/setup.rs:44-100]
- Closest detach test: `a_dead_body_takes_its_own_art_with_it` asserts a synthetic render descendant is reparented, a piece is made, and the original body despawns; it does not load or assert a real section GLTF tree. [crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:641-670]
- `a_wreck_carries_only_the_shape_the_section_was` verifies collider removal across a plate/decor-shaped tree, the piece's `ChunkGrace`, and retained art parenting; its fixture nodes are synthetic. [crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:772-818]
- The destruction example observes `DetachedPieceMarker` and the old entity origin, while the collapse example records detached-piece counts/physics; neither cited observer asserts reconstructed child art. [examples/systems/system_destruction_finale.rs:167-171; examples/systems/stress_hull_collapse.rs:758-758,886-886,1094-1094,1196-1198]
- No inspected test/probe proves durable snapshot/thaw of a detached art-only piece. A focused proof would need to assert the thawed child hierarchy, asset refs/transforms, fixture surface count/style, omission of Health/section gameplay components, and restored animation/hinge pose; this is a proof gap, not a claim that no other test exists. [crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:641-670,772-818; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:320-423]

## 10-line reproducibility summary

1. Hull: authored body is reproducible from section config; exact skin needs fixture records and the ship's `ShipStyle` id; animated pose needs track state. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:534-545; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_style.rs:568-585; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:164-176]
2. Thruster: body and generated exhaust are reproducible from config; the detached plume is driven to its zero bucket, while exact pre-update appearance needs runtime bucket/state. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/thruster_section.rs:249-301,641-679,728-812,834-875]
3. Controller: authored body/placeholder and local transform are reproducible from section config; no child hinge state is shown. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/controller_section.rs:576-668]
4. Turret: authored joint/art tree is reproducible from config; exact pose needs frozen hinge/animation state, and `TurretSectionPartOf` IDs are not durable. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/setup.rs:44-100,180-220; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:534-545]
5. Torpedo: authored body/effect are reproducible from config; exact door pose needs animation state, and part-of IDs are transient. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/bay.rs:129-158; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/render.rs:16-68,760-800]
6. Railgun: body/glow are reproducible from config; an in-flight charge pose needs charge/glow state. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/railgun_section/render.rs:170-235]
7. Docking: body and local transform are reproducible from section config; any animated node pose needs track state. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/docking_section/render.rs:15-55; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:164-176]
8. CargoIntake: body and local transform are reproducible from config; door pose needs track state; its sensor trigger is not a reparented child. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/cargo_intake_section.rs:389-447,622-643; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:164-176]
9. Mining: body and local transform are reproducible from config; emitter pose needs track state. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/mining_section.rs:205-232,271-292; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:164-176]
10. From the detached piece alone, **none** is fully reproducible: `SectionBuildConfig` and `ShipStyle` are not copied, runtime entity backrefs are stale, and active animation/hinge/plume/charge states need explicit snapshot data. With those source inputs plus allowed transient-state records, authored static art for all nine kinds is reconstructible; resolved GLTF internals remain asset-dependent. [crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:334-337,534-545; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/skin_style.rs:568-577; crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:164-176]
