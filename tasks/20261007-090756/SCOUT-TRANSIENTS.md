# ECS transient and save-system source map

## Summary (10 lines)
1. Hand-swept rounds are non-rigidbody entities advanced in `FixedPostUpdate` after physics; turret and railgun spawn paths attach authored `TempEntity` lifetimes.
2. Round dedup is split across an 8-collider cross-step ring, a 32-hit step cap, and exact per-flight rake sets for its backward-reaching sweep.
3. Torpedoes are dynamic bodies with launch-time owner/type/arming/guidance state; cold ignition and arming are separate from the authored projectile lifetime.
4. Detonation creates an owner-attributed sensor blast: eight fixed physics ticks resolve overlaps, and a one-second `TempEntity` is only a backstop.
5. `TempEntity` initializes a private one-shot timer and ticks it with `Update` frame delta; it is not a snapshot/resume timer.
6. Detached sections and carved chunks become physical only after `ChunkGrace`; both live 30 seconds, while shed fixtures reuse their entity and live 12 seconds.
7. Pyre particle effects are two timed ECS entities per selected death; their separate light is a `LightFlash`/transient-light entity.
8. Mining clocks and hit state are section-local `Update` data; beams are persistent child visuals, while owed ore and canister queues have frozen representations.
9. Sector/save snapshots cover persistent ships, asteroids, canisters, wreck fragments and ore drops; transients are skipped or culled, never reconstructed.
10. Strong proof anchors are cited below; no cargo, staging, or commit was performed, and no runtime check was run by this read-only scout.

## Ownership, entry points, clocks

- `nova_gameplay::NovaRoundPlugin` owns `advance_rounds` in `FixedPostUpdate.after(PhysicsSystems::Last)` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/rounds.rs:247-270`). It integrates `RoundVelocity`, then sweeps; the round has no rigid body (`rounds.rs:273-315`). Turret spawn carries owner, velocity, damage, source/render metadata and configured lifetime, but no collider/body (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/turret_section/firing.rs:335-358`). Railgun spawn carries the same sweep state, Pierce power, authored lifetime and optional `RoundRake` (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/railgun_section/firing.rs:189-214`).
- `RoundBitten` retains eight collider `Entity`s across steps (`rounds.rs:42-80,213-232`); each `TipWalk` has a 32-hit per-step bound and a step-local found list (`rounds.rs:570-604,624-640`). Rake remembers exact armed bodies and charged colliders because its trailing sphere sweeps backward (`rounds.rs:84-179`). A round that spends its damage despawns at impact; an authorless round is consumed by its first tangible non-owner collider (`rounds.rs:765-819,1143-1189`). These identities and dedup state belong only to the live ECS entity.
- `TorpedoSectionPlugin` launches dynamic rigid bodies with `ProjectileOwner`, `TorpedoArming`, `TorpedoColdLaunch`, guidance and `TempEntity(config.projectile_lifetime)` (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/bay.rs:358-375,416-455`). A target position is added only after a lock (`torpedo_section/projectile.rs:405-407`). Arming tracks elapsed time, launch distance and live-launcher clearance; clearance is snapshotted at launch (`torpedo_section/mod.rs:810-852`; `bay.rs:436-453`). Cold ignition counts down on the fixed clock and reenables child colliders (`torpedo_section/projectile.rs:85-124`). Detonation is sampled after physics in `FixedPostUpdate` (`torpedo_section/mod.rs:958-977`); a targetless torpedo has no target position and cannot detonate (`torpedo_section/projectile.rs:210-225`). Thrust follows nose alignment and headroom to configured max speed (`torpedo_section/projectile.rs:578-606`; `torpedo_section/mod.rs:318-329`); no separate fuel countdown appears in these paths. Lifetime is the configured `TempEntity` ceiling.
- Generic lifetime owner: `TempEntity(pub f32)` is the total; its private `TempEntityState(Timer)` is initialized on insert and ticked by `Update`/`Time::delta()`; completed entities use `try_despawn` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/lifetime.rs:57-88,91-134`). This frame clock is distinct from rounds/torpedo fuze/blast fixed sampling. Pausing virtual time therefore pauses the generic countdown (the retry case below demonstrates it).

## Damage, dedup, debris, effects

- `nova_blast` is a static sensor with `BlastTicksLeft(8)` plus `TempEntity(1s)` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/damage.rs:545-567,784-827`). Collision events collect `(blast, blast collider, target collider)`; resolution sorts/deduplicates events, groups by blast, and applies one blast carve after summing collider hits (`damage.rs:583-615,700-782`). Sensors are excluded. Tests pin equal fixed-tick lifetime across frame lengths and one crater for multi-collider targets (`damage.rs:1415-1467,1550-1575,1601-1605`). Torpedo detonation transfers owner attribution to its blast; a test asserts it (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/projectile.rs:880-940`). Shot-down torpedoes are removed without detonating (`torpedo_section/bay.rs:1325-1360`).
- Render blast entities are separate from the damage volume: authored/fallback effects live 2s, fallback core 1.2s; light duration is separate (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/torpedo_section/render.rs:551-605`).
- Destroyed sections spawn new detached wreck bodies with `DetachedPieceMarker(original Entity)`, inherited point velocity plus kick/spin, `ChunkGrace`, and 30s lifetime; their art is reparented and descendant colliders stripped before the source despawns (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:79-119,420-477`). `a_dead_body_takes_its_own_art_with_it` and grace/clearance tests cover detach and activation (`explode.rs:644-670,708-765,1017-1059`).
- Shed fixtures differ: the same fixture entity is detached, keeps its art/section attribution, strips colliders, gains inherited motion and a 12s lifetime (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/fixture.rs:34-40,202-220,303-355`). Tests cover one-time shedding, art, and collider removal (`fixture.rs:461-489,528-549,711-727`).
- Severed chunks are kinematic/colliderless during `ChunkGrace` (default flat grace 0.5s, scaled by clearance), then land dynamic with collider/gravity in batches of at most 24 per frame; each carries a 30s `TempEntity` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/chunk.rs:86-133,190-197,221-253,337-377`). `a_fresh_chunk_touches_nothing` and `a_chunk_that_has_drifted_clear_becomes_physical` assert both stages (`chunk.rs:414-479`).
- Pyre `burn` spawns core and ejecta particle entities, each with `TempEntity(scale.linger)`, then requests an independent `LightFlash(scale.light_secs)` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/pyre.rs:977-1018`). Impact sparks live 0.28s (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/impact_spark.rs:70,175-190`); damage sparks live 0.35s and are kinematic/colliderless (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/damage_sparks.rs:48,181-193`). Transient lights use request duration as lifetime and separately age their brightness curve (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/transient_light.rs:153-204`). Impact-carve chips use pooled Hanabi emitters, not ECS transient entities (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/spew.rs:43-60,582-630,741-760`).
- Railgun wake emitters are particle emitters, not projectiles or per-particle ECS transients. On slug removal they consume the final pose/segment, then receive a linger `TempEntity` equal to particle lifetime plus margin (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/railgun_section/wake.rs:385-435`); `a_slug_that_ends_its_flight_hands_the_wake_the_segment_it_died_on` asserts retirement (`wake.rs:812-857`).

## Mining state and persistence boundary

- Each deployed emitter has its own `MiningPulseClock`; `pulse_mining_beams` publishes/removes `MiningBeamHit`, decrements by frame delta and emits at most one pulse in an `Update` pass (`crates/nova_scenario/src/mining.rs:190-253,387-480`). Inactive/undeployed sections lose clock and hit state (`mining.rs:421-455`). `MiningSystems` runs after `MiningSectionSystems`, and pulse processing chains before canister ejection (`mining.rs:1036-1063`). Render creates a persistent beam laser and optional particle emitter as children of the mining section, not one `TempEntity` per pulse (`mining.rs:617-667`); draw/fade and pulse flash are separate systems (`mining.rs:731-826`).
- Owed ore and FIFO canister queues are serde-enabled (`mining.rs:199-248`). `FrozenAsteroid` stores damage/carved field, pending ore and queue; thaw restores them (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/asteroid.rs:436-458,560-590,721-735`). Exhausted-rock queues move to a `MinedOreDrop` whose frozen payload is the queue (`mining.rs:255-305,857-917`). Emitted canisters are persistent bodies: `FrozenCanister` stores runtime ID, contents, health and model (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/cargo_intake_section.rs:273-323`). `CargoCanisterRuntimeId` is monotonic and resumes past the saved allocator value (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/inventory.rs:625-669,882-905`). Beam hit/clock and visuals are runtime state, not saved pulse progress: `FrozenSection` persists health, ammo/reload, fixtures and other listed state, but not mining clock/hit (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:66-82`; `mining.rs:190-253`).

## Snapshot, stable identity, fidelity edges

- Persistent body allowlist is ships, asteroids, cargo canisters, wreck fragments and mined ore drops (`crates/nova_world/src/frozen.rs:561-590`). `freeze_body` keeps optional `EntityId`/name, physics pose, visibility, velocity and owner payload; supported owner variants are explicit and unsupported persistent bodies panic (`frozen.rs:424-480`). Wreck fragments freeze section IDs/names/config/pose/connectivity/state (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/integrity.rs:160-187,201-254`). Canister runtime IDs are retained by freeze/thaw (`cargo_intake_section.rs:273-323`). By contrast, torpedo ownership and detached-piece/fixture source markers use Bevy `Entity` handles (`torpedo_section/bay.rs:372-375`; `explode.rs:110-119`; `fixture.rs:185-190`), not durable IDs; this makes those references live-world-only.
- `snapshot_sectors` clones the ledger, snapshots persistent bodies in live roots and top-level waiting persistent bodies, and errors on unsettled/unowned bodies (`frozen.rs:724-812`). It does not serialize live projectiles, blasts, detached debris, effects, or timer/dedup state. Sector retirement skips child `TempEntity`; top-level transients outside desired sectors are despawned (`frozen.rs:670-682,1010-1039`). Save waits for unsettled bodies only while virtual time advances, then fails after a bounded count; an unowned persistent body fails immediately (`crates/nova_world_base/src/save/session.rs:296-360`).
- Fidelity edge (verified): transient sector culling uses `Transform`, while persistent-body freeze substitutes Avian `Position`/`Rotation` for an interpolated pose (`frozen.rs:443-451,670-681`). A transient near a sector edge can therefore be culled by render/interpolated pose rather than physics pose; this is an inference from those two code paths, not a reproduced defect.
- Fidelity edge (verified): generic TempEntity lifetime is total authored seconds plus a newly initialized private timer; snapshot records have no remaining timer field (`lifetime.rs:61-72,91-134`; `frozen.rs:306-323`). Current transient state is deliberately ephemeral, so save/load does not resume an in-flight shot, blast, debris lifetime, mining pulse cadence, or `RoundBitten`/rake hit history.

- Player example: `examples/playable/carve_asteroids.rs:5-34` describes real PDC rounds, a shipped torpedo and a severing cut; its assertions observe paid rounds/crater and torpedo damage (`:448-517,529-572,627-640`). `examples/playable/railgun_wake_bench.rs:618` authors a wake-lifetime lane. `nova_probe` snapshot capability records `TempEntity` total/remaining and torpedo owner/target/armed fields as observations, not save payload (`crates/nova_probe/src/capabilities/snapshot.rs:1600-1630`).
