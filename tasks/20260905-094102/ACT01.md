# Act one: The job

The script of act one of the Nova Protocol comic: beats 1 and 2 of
`STORY.md`, from the start of the shift to the first hour after the strike.
The pages at `web/src/comics/nova-protocol/` are drawn from this file. The
game plays the same shift and the same strike with its own lessons and its
own words. It takes the events, the names and the facts from here, not the
lines.

## How to read this file

- One scene is one page. One description paragraph is one picture.
- A dialogue line is `SPEAKER [channel]: words`. The channels are `cabin`
  (inside Cutter One, no radio), `deck` (the boat deck), `work` (Cutter One
  and Meridian Control), `ship` (Meridian's all-hands), `guard` (the open
  emergency channel every ship keeps on speaker), `open` (the skiffs, in the
  clear) and `recorder` (Meridian's certified recorder, shown to the reader
  as recovered later).
- `CAPTION` is the narrator. It states facts. It does not explain the company
  and it does not tell the reader what to feel.
- Radio the cutter hears in pieces is written in pieces, with `...` for what
  the cutter does not hear.
- The captain has no face: helmet on, seen from behind or from the side, the
  visor a mirror. Halloran, Okoro, Brandt and Demir have faces.

## What the reader knows at the end

A reader who starts here, without the prologue, leaves act one knowing:

- Cutter One is a three-person work boat of the EWI carrier Meridian, at a
  junk site in Saturn's rings that the company is clearing for new stations.
- The captain came from Earth four years ago and still believes the rotation
  home will come. Halloran flew for Kestrel, the company EWI finished, and
  says what everyone thinks. Okoro is a company lifer whose loyalty is to
  people. He learned the boat deck from Pell and boat fitting from Brandt.
- Section nine of the contract is the renewal clause and, under it, the Nova
  Protocol: roster closed, no recovery. It is the crew's standing joke.
- Dorian Pell ran Meridian's boat deck, refused a no-rescue order when
  Kestrel's Shelter died, took a tender across and went dark. He has a plaque
  next to the WE ARE EXPANDING banner. The crew believes he died a hero.
- Every crewed ship carries a certified recorder that outlives it and beacons
  when it is unseated. The desk logs the beacon and does not pay to fetch it.
- Fleet has been calling on the guard channel for weeks about something it
  lost, and nobody at Saturn cares.
- A Fleet-built hull with no Fleet code came out of the moonlet's shadow,
  read Meridian Control its own clause over the guard channel, and killed the
  carrier in the time the reading took. Then it left. The crew heard one word
  of the reading. The reader heard all of it and does not know the voice.
- 412 died, Brandt and Demir among them. Scavenger skiffs came to strip the
  wreck within the hour. Cutter One is dark in the junk with forty hours of
  air, and the crew has decided to go across and look when the skiffs leave.

The reader who has the prologue also knows what the crew does not: which
ship Meridian is, and what section nine was used for four years ago.

## Cast in this act

- The captain. Pilot of Cutter One. Even, dry, still amused. Asks the
  questions. Touches the plaque on the way to the boat, every shift.
- Nadia Halloran. Copilot. Sharp, says the thing. Calls Datum the Shelter.
  Flew Kestrel's supply boat Harrier.
- Bastian Okoro. Engineer. Practical, warm, loyal to people. Learned the deck
  from Pell and boat fitting from Brandt.
- Aurelio Brandt. Deck chief. Rounds up for everybody. Dies in the strike.
- Yusra Demir. Meridian Control. Dry and exact. Dies mid-sentence.
- Captain Vance. Commander of Meridian. One all-hands call. Dies with the
  ship.
- The voice. Reads eleven words on the guard channel. Not named in this act.
- Ledger and Rotation. Two skiffs that come to strip the wreck.

## Scenes

### 1. The boat bay

Meridian's boat deck at the top of shift three. The wall: WE ARE EXPANDING in
company green, three metres tall. Beside it, smaller, the plaque: DORIAN
PELL, BOAT DECK, EWI MERIDIAN, LOST IN RESCUE, "ALL HANDS, TENDERS AWAY", WE
REMEMBER OUR OWN. The captain walks under both toward the boat, helmet on,
seen from behind, and lays two fingers on the plaque's edge in passing
without slowing down. Nobody else looks up at the wall.

- CAPTION: EWI carrier Meridian. The junk site, Saturn's rings. Four years
  after the Shelter.

Cutter One on its clamps: a work boat with a glass nose, a truss aft, the arm
folded. Okoro is under it to the waist in an open access panel. Brandt stands
at the clamps with a board, grey, broad, the deck chief. Halloran comes up
behind the captain pulling on her gloves.

- HALLORAN [deck]: You still do that.
- CAPTAIN [deck]: It's on the way to the boat.
- HALLORAN [deck]: It's a plaque.
- OKORO [deck]: Leave it.

Brandt taps the board against the clamp.

- BRANDT [deck]: Cutter One. The boat has been ready since the top of the
  hour. Your engineer has been under her since before that.
- OKORO [deck]: Forty minutes. He rounds up.
- BRANDT [deck]: I round up for everybody. Thirty-one years on decks, and
  nobody has been early yet.
- HALLORAN [deck]: What's the work?
- BRANDT [deck]: The north field. Hulls nobody can name. Control wants every
  one of them tagged by the end of your shift so the tugs can start pulling
  tonight. Azimuth is ten days out with four hundred new hands and
  everything they are going to bolt to this rock, and Vance wants a clean
  rock to show them.
- OKORO [deck]: Ten days to clear twenty years of other people's junk.
- BRANDT [deck]: Then tag fast.

Okoro slides out of the panel and closes it. Brandt puts a hand flat on the
hull, the way he does on every boat he sends out.

- BRANDT [deck]: Twelve hours out, forty in the bottles, and in thirty-one
  years nobody has needed the forty. Go on. Somebody has to fit the next
  boat.

### 2. Released

Cutter One drops out of the open bay. From outside: Meridian long and lit,
the banner glowing in the bay mouth. Past it the field, dead hulls and scrap
and ring ice out to the horizon, and the moonlet, a grey lump with a shadow
side as black as the sky.

- DEMIR [work]: Cutter One, Meridian Control. You are clear of the bay. The
  work channel is yours. Keep the guard channel on speaker. That is a
  regulation, not a suggestion.
- CAPTAIN [work]: Control, Cutter One. Clear. Guard is on speaker.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: At a volume.
- DEMIR [work]: I heard that, Halloran.
- HALLORAN [work]: You were meant to.

The cabin. Three seats behind the glass: the captain left, Halloran right,
Okoro behind them at the arm controls and the engineering board. The guard
speaker hisses and gives up a voice in pieces.

- GUARD [guard]: ...Earth Fleet... any vessel with contact... Resolute...
  report to...
- OKORO [cabin]: Fleet has lost something.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: Fleet has always lost something.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Then it can look out here with the rest of us.

She turns the speaker down. The field slides past the glass.

- CAPTAIN [cabin]: Two renewals down. One more and I'm on the rotation home.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Read section nine.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: I have read section nine. Renewal at the company's option.
  They will run out of options eventually.
- OKORO [cabin]: They never have.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: On Earth they would have to give you notice.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Rights are an Earth thing. Different gravity.

The captain laughs. The helmet turns toward the other two. Neither of them is
laughing, and neither of them is angry. They have heard it.

- CAPTION: The captain still laughed at section nine. Halloran and Okoro had
  stopped years ago. Four years in one cabin, and it was the only thing they
  did not share.

### 3. Hulls nobody can name

The north field. A hauler, broken behind the cabin, its name half gone from
the plate: ...ARD ICE. Cutter One's arm presses a tag onto the plate. The
tag's light starts to blink.

- OKORO [cabin]: Tag is live. That's four.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Halvard Ice? Hallard? Anybody?
- OKORO [cabin]: Before my time.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: Everything out here is before your time.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Somebody had their name on that. Now it's a tag number.
- OKORO [cabin]: A tag number with a working seal on the back half. Control,
  Cutter One. Tag four is carrying a sealed cargo module. It's worth a tug.
- DEMIR [work]: Cutter One, Control. Logged. We pull it tonight. And Datum
  wants the tag list by the end of your shift, not the end of the day.
- HALLORAN [work]: Tell the Shelter it gets the list when the list is done.
- DEMIR [work]: Halloran.
- HALLORAN [work]: Datum. Tell Datum.
- DEMIR [work]: Thank you.
- CAPTION: EWI's Saturn headquarters stands on the rock where Kestrel's
  Shelter died. Policy bans the old name. Kestrel hands use it off the
  radio. Halloran uses it on.

The cutter turns to line up on the module and the glass sweeps across the
moonlet's dark side: a wedge of black with ring ice going into it and not
coming out. In the black, a straight edge that is not ice. Nobody in the
cabin is looking that way.

### 4. The one with him in it

The arm cuts the cargo module free of the hauler's spine. Behind it, in the
opened spine, a squat armoured box under a certification stencil: RECORDER.
Intact. Seated. Twenty years of nobody.

- OKORO [cabin]: Recorder is still seated.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: What's on it?
- OKORO [cabin]: Everything they said and everything the ship did. They are
  built to outlive the hull. Certification.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Kestrel had one on every boat. Cost more than the boat.
- OKORO [cabin]: You would know.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: I flew one.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: Does anybody ever come for them?
- OKORO [cabin]: The moment you unseat it, it beacons, and the desk has to
  log a fetch. Then the fetch costs. So it sits.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: It would tell you who they were.
- OKORO [cabin]: It would tell a lawyer who they were. Leave it. It's not on
  the order.

The module comes in against the truss. The cabin waits for the clamps to
bite. The captain watches the plate go past the glass: ...ARD ICE.

- HALLORAN [cabin]: Four years you have touched that plaque, and you have
  never asked who he was.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: I read the plaque. Lost in rescue.
- OKORO [cabin]: He was the deck officer. Brandt taught him to fit a boat,
  and taught me after. Pell taught me what the deck is for. When the Shelter
  went up, the desk said section nine, hold your posts. He said all hands,
  tenders away, took Tender Four across to see who was left, and went dark
  at forty-one minutes. That's the story.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: That's the company's story.
- OKORO [cabin]: It's the one with him in it.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: And nobody went after the tender.
- OKORO [cabin]: Section nine. Roster closed, no recovery, a letter home,
  costs against remaining term. The Nova Protocol. It's the paragraph under
  the renewal clause. Same page.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: That's the joke.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: That's the joke.

Three faces in the cabin light, one of them a helmet. The clamps bite.

- OKORO [cabin]: Module's home. Tag five.

### 5. Out of the shadow

Late in the shift. Meridian small and bright in the distance, its bay a green
mark. The cutter turns for home with the module on the truss, the moonlet off
its right side, the field between.

- VANCE [ship]: Meridian, this is the captain. Good shift. Sixty tags, and
  the tugs start pulling tonight. Azimuth is on the lane. Let's have a rock
  to show them. Vance out.
- BRANDT [deck]: Cutter One, deck. I see you on the board. Bring that module
  in gentle. I painted the clamps this morning.
- OKORO [deck]: You paint the clamps every morning.
- BRANDT [deck]: And every evening somebody scrapes them. Come home.

The guard speaker, low, gives up a voice. It is not Fleet's cadence and it is
not Demir's.

- GUARD [guard]: ...Meridian Control...
- HALLORAN [cabin]: That's not Fleet.
- GUARD [guard]: ...nine...
- OKORO [cabin]: Who calls Control on guard?
- GUARD [guard]: ...roster...

The word sits in the cabin. Nobody says anything.

- DEMIR [work]: Cutter One, Control. Hold your position. We have a drive
  plume clearing the moonlet's limb and no transponder on it. Stay off the
  work channel until I clear you.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: There. Right side, low. Coming out of the shadow.

Through the glass: the moonlet's limb, a plume, and a long grey hull sliding
out of the dark where nothing had been. Two rail apertures forward. A chevron
on the flank. Where the name should be, a patch of newer paint and a stencil
in gold the crew cannot read at this range.

- HALLORAN [cabin]: That's a Fleet hull. Look at the lines.
- OKORO [cabin]: Fleet doesn't come out here without telling everyone for a
  week first.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: It isn't telling anyone anything.
- OKORO [cabin]: It said roster.
- DEMIR [guard]: Unidentified vessel, this is EarthWorks carrier Meridian,
  civilian registry, four hundred and twelve aboard. We are unarmed.
  Identify yourself and state your intent.

### 6. The reading

Shown to the reader as a printout: MERIDIAN, CERTIFIED RECORDER, GUARD
CHANNEL, RECOVERED LATER. What the cutter heard as one word.

- UNIDENTIFIED [recorder]: Meridian Control. Section nine.
- DEMIR [recorder]: Unidentified vessel, say again. Who is this?
- UNIDENTIFIED [recorder]: Roster closed. No recovery.
- DEMIR [recorder]: Say again your... identify yourself. Who is this?
- UNIDENTIFIED [recorder]: Hold your posts.
- DEMIR [recorder]: All stations, brace. Brace. Bra
- CAPTION: End of entry.

Full page. The grey hull's rails fire twice, two lines of light across the
field into Meridian amidships. The carrier opens along its length. The bay's
green goes out with the rest of it: banner and plaque and deck and boats in
their clamps. The junk between the two ships throws long shadows toward the
moonlet.

- CAPTION: It read Meridian the company's own clause and killed it in the
  time the reading took. Eleven words on the guard channel. It never said a
  twelfth.

The hull turns away with its drive lit and is a plume, then a spark, then
nothing, into the far side of the field, in less than a minute.

- CAPTION: It did not stay to look.

The cutter's cabin, the glass full of the light.

- HALLORAN [cabin]: Lights. Transponder. Now.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: Control is still...
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Control is gone.

The captain kills the lights and the transponder. The cabin goes dark except
for the fire in the visor.

### 7. Survivors unknown

Meridian in two pieces, the stern venting, embers drifting where the bay was.
On the guard channel the automatic beacon, flat and patient, over and over.

- BEACON [guard]: Any vessel. Any vessel. This is EarthWorks carrier
  Meridian. Hull breach, all decks. Survivors unknown. Any vessel.
- OKORO [deck]: Deck, Cutter One. Brandt. Brandt, deck channel, answer me.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Bastian.
- OKORO [deck]: Deck, anybody.

Nothing but the beacon.

Lamps in the junk. Three of them, coming in from the side of the field where
the hull went: small ships built out of other ships' plates, one lamp each,
spread out and slow. Scavengers. They are talking in the clear.

- HALLORAN [cabin]: Radio. Everything. Off.

The captain kills the rest. The cutter receives and sends nothing.

- LEDGER [open]: Rotation, Ledger. The big one is in two. Stern is still
  warm, take the bow.
- ROTATION [open]: Bow. Bridge glass is company glass, we can use it.
  Anything moving?
- LEDGER [open]: Boats in their clamps. Nobody got to them.
- ROTATION [open]: Then nobody is arguing. Pick clean. We are not here long.

Cutter One dark in the junk, a dead barge between it and the lamps. In the
cabin, the visor lights only.

- HALLORAN [cabin]: Scavengers. Inside the hour. They knew where to be.
- OKORO [cabin]: There could be people on the deck. The deck is armoured.
  Brandt could be...
- HALLORAN [cabin]: If we light the drive, we're the next plaque. Wait.

Okoro looks at the captain. The helmet does not turn from the glass.

- CAPTAIN [cabin]: We wait. When they leave, we go across and look.
- CAPTION: Four hundred and twelve on Meridian's roster. Brandt, Demir,
  Vance, and the deck. The captain had known most of them four years.

### 8. Forty hours

The cutter in the junk with everything off. Past the glass, on the dead
hauler, the morning's tag is still blinking. The three sit in the dark. The
air gauge is the brightest thing in the cabin.

- OKORO [cabin]: Twelve hours out, forty in the bottles.
- HALLORAN [cabin]: And nobody has ever needed the forty.
- OKORO [cabin]: Thirty-one years.

The guard speaker, turned down to nothing, gives up its voice in pieces
anyway.

- GUARD [guard]: ...Earth Fleet... any vessel with contact... Resolute...
  report to...
- HALLORAN [cabin]: Fleet has lost something.
- OKORO [cabin]: It's here.
- CAPTAIN [cabin]: Turn it off.
- CAPTION: Nobody at Saturn had been told a warship was missing. Now three
  people knew, and they had forty hours of air.

End of act one.

## Continuity

Facts fixed by this act. Act two and the game keep them.

- Meridian: 412 aboard. Shift three. Azimuth ten days out on the lane with
  four hundred workers.
- Cutter One: twelve-hour shifts, forty hours in the bottles for three.
- Brandt: thirty-one years on decks. Taught Pell to fit a boat, then Okoro.
- Pell: Tender Four, dark at forty-one minutes.
- The tagged hauler: ...ARD ICE, tag four, a sealed cargo module and a seated
  recorder left in place.
- The reading: eleven words. "Meridian Control. Section nine. Roster closed.
  No recovery. Hold your posts."
- The hull: two rail apertures, a chevron, a gold stencil over new paint. It
  fires twice and is gone in under a minute.
- The skiffs: Ledger and Rotation, in the clear, inside the hour. The boats
  were still in their clamps.
- The crew's decision: wait for the skiffs to leave, then go across and look.
