Wreck loot canisters: 1,000 kg mixed candidate vs shipped 200 kg per item

Task 20261026-003724 (v0.17.0, OPEN). Design-only proposal written 2026-10-30 against master at 112b79a0d, revised the same day for the owner-preferred 1,000 kg mixed candidate. No gameplay change on this page is implemented. This report is a design document, not a game capture. The task record is in TASK.md; the earlier bench is in 20261025-214714/TASK.md.

Implementation status (added 2026-10-30, after approval). The owner approved candidate M with M-P: a global 1,000 kg CARGO_CANISTER_MAX_MASS_G, mixed whole-item packing of one wreck drop, and partial pickup of whole items that keeps the canister and its id. Implementation is in progress on master and is not committed or proven at runtime. Owner-preferred candidate M proposed, not approved. Raise the canister cap to about 1,000 kg. The wreck producer packs the stacks of one drop into mixed canisters of whole items. Each canister holds a variable amount of loot, at most the cap. This proposal interprets "dynamic range" as actual filled mass below a fixed maximum, not a cap that scales with the player's hold. That interpretation remains open for confirmation. Goal: fewer canisters to collect.

Short answer.

  1. Canisters can already hold several item types. CargoCanister stores a BTreeMap of stacks, and Jettison already tops up a waiting canister with a different item shipped. Only the wreck and mining producers pack one item type per canister.
  2. M packs per drop, not per wreck. A ship destroyed whole leaves one drop. A section shot off earlier leaves its own drop shipped. Under M, a drop of 1,000 kg or less becomes one canister, and separate drops stay separate canisters.
  3. Code can merge only the canisters of one drop, and only before they are born. A canister gets its durable CargoCanisterRuntimeId at birth, and saves and travel locks key on that id shipped.
  4. M keeps whole-fit, so a canister needs free hold at least its whole mass shipped. Bigger canisters are refused more often. With the fixture, the smallest hold that takes anything rises from 30 kg to 300 kg, and the largest canister needs 1,000 kg free modeled. Every start ship holds 1,800 kg or more, so an empty one can take a full canister modeled.
  5. The cap is one global constant. Raising it also changes mining and jettison canister sizes, the item lint limit, the save check and the wiki shipped. Shipped saves stay valid modeled.
  6. The historic bench failed on contact, not on fit or count: 13,400 kg free, nearest canister 49.2 m, 36 separate drops measured. M does not change that result.

Recommendation. Keep M as the direction and pair it with partial pickup of whole items (M-P, section 9), which removes the low-hold regression and keeps the hold limit. If partial pickup is rejected, use a fixed target fill T below the cap (M-T). Reproduce a starter-ship salvage run first. Its missed rewards may be no drop, missed contact or refusal, and only refusal and count are what M changes. The cap value, the packing rule and the whole-fit semantics are not approved.

3. One drop vs many canisters

shipped "One drop" means one MinedOreDrop entity with one queue. It does not mean one canister.

  • A ship destroyed whole by a structural collapse leaves one drop. The fixture test name says so, and the test asserts 10 canisters in that one drop (crates/nova_scenario/src/wreck_cargo/tests.rs:175-236).
  • Only sections that die while their root collapses join the root's drop (wreck_cargo.rs:101-108). A section lost earlier made its own drop, at its own place and time, with its own velocity (:109-126).
  • A drop releases one canister per frame when its birth point is clear (mining.rs:1088-1166). After that, each canister is an independent rigid body with a minted id.

proposed A merge is safe only inside one queue, before the ejector. There, canisters are plain values in a VecDeque with no id, lock or save reference.

modeled Merging born canisters means despawning ids and minting new ones. A SavedTargetRef::Canister(id) to a retired id fails validation (save/controls.rs:257-260) or finds no live canister (save/controls.rs:709-712, save/transients.rs:665-672). A travel lock and the probe's id-keyed canister list (crates/nova_probe/src/capabilities/snapshot.rs:50, 87) would lose their target. Merging separate drops before birth means the game must decide which place, direction and velocity the merged queue takes. It must also decide how long one drop waits for another.

8. Empty drops

shipped An empty stack set spawns no drop (wreck_cargo.rs:132-134). For a section, emptiness is fixed by its recipe at full share (inventory.rs:1572-1580). It does not depend on packing.

modeled Every base recipe read (recipes.rs:36-146) has at least one part with floor(count * strip_yield) >= 1, so no base section drop is empty at present. An empty root drop is still possible: a ship whose sections were all shot off one by one, with an empty hold, has nothing to drop when its root dies. A recipe from other content can still strip to zero. Candidate M and its variants do not change any of these cases. Only a yield or rounding change (Option D) does.

10. Global cap impact at 1,000 kg

shipped CARGO_CANISTER_MAX_MASS_G (inventory.rs:1234) is one global constant. If M raises it, every consumer below changes. A wreck-only cap would need a second constant, and the save check cannot tell wreck canisters apart: FrozenCanister keeps only id, contents, health and mesh (cargo_intake_section.rs:282-296). So a wreck-only cap still makes the save check accept 1,000 kg for every canister, or it adds an origin field to the save.

11. End state, deaths, breaks, failures (candidate M)

End state proposed. A destroyed ship's one drop holds as few canisters as the approved packing rule gives. Each canister holds whole items, may mix item types, and weighs what it holds, at most the cap (1,000 kg proposed). Mass is conserved exactly. Ejection, freeze, save and ids work as they do now. Section drops of a standing ship stay separate. Whole-fit is kept unless M-P is approved.

What dies. The per-item CargoCanister::split call in wreck_cargo.rs:136-139. The module doc line "Each item packs into whole canisters by split" (wreck_cargo.rs:20). The 10-canister expectation in wreck_cargo/tests.rs:212-225. The 200 kg value and every doc that states it (section 10). split itself stays for mining (mining.rs:1056) and jettison (inventory.rs:1450), at the new cap. With M-P, also the whole-fit tests (cargo_intake_section/tests.rs:460-500, 502).

What may break. Pickup with a hold below the canister mass (section 7). Small ships, if a start ship's hold is under 1,000 kg. Mining and jettison canister sizes. Bench and probe readers that expect one item per wreck canister. The station test in wreck_cargo/tests.rs:268-282, which filters canisters by item. Player readability, because the tags read Mixed cargo. Loss size when one canister is destroyed.

What must fail loudly. A canister over the cap: the existing new/add asserts panic (inventory.rs:1274-1278, 1319-1324). An item over the cap: content lint first (crates/nova_assets/src/items.rs:147-154), then the packer panics. Counts that do not sum back to the input stacks: assert in the packer. A saved canister over the cap: the existing save refusal (save/mod.rs:940-951). A full hold refuses whole and leaves the goods in space, unless M-P is approved.

13. Proof plan

proposed Each new test needs owner approval first. Each one names a stable behavior.

  1. Reproduce first (task gate). Run a nova-bench starter-hold defeat-then-salvage flow on the current build. Keep score.json, audit.jsonl and game.log. Record each refusal with free mass and canister mass, and count drops and origins as in section 4. This shows whether the failure is refusal, reach or yield.
  2. Pure packing (unit, inventory.rs tests). For the 1,300 kg fixture stacks, assert the exact list of the approved rule (2 canisters, 1,000 + 300 kg under fill-to-cap). Also assert: every canister is at most the cap, counts sum back to the input, a repeat call gives the same list, and an over-cap item panics.
  3. Deterministic drop (ECS example, wreck_cargo/tests.rs). Update the whole-ship test to assert one drop and the approved list. Drain it and assert FIFO contents, unique ids and no second drop. Keep the section-alone test as the per-drop isolation check.
  4. Intake (ECS example). With a hold 150 kg free and M1 and M2 in contact: whole-fit refuses both, the door stays shut and hold mass is unchanged. With M-P: the hold gains the approved items up to 150 kg, the canister keeps its id and the rest.
  5. Freeze and save. Freeze a waiting drop with mixed canisters, thaw it, and drain it (existing pattern mining.rs:2127-2261). Save and load a live 1,000 kg canister with a travel lock. A canister over the cap must still be refused (save/mod.rs:940-951). Load a shipped v0.16.0 save unchanged.
  6. Player pickup (nova-bench). Defeat, salvage and spend on the same fixture as step 1, with a start ship. Compare canister count, received mass and refusals with the step 1 artifacts. Inspect pickup-sight and tag frames on an isolated Xvfb display only.

Run affected checks only, with nix develop --command: the nova_gameplay inventory tests, the nova_scenario wreck_cargo and mining tests, the nova_ship intake tests, the nova_assets item lint tests and the nova_world_base save tests. Then the bench.

14. Decision

Owner preference. Candidate M: canisters up to about 1,000 kg, filled with mixed items, to cut collection work. Nothing is approved for implementation.

Recommendation. Keep M as the design direction, and pair it with M-P (partial pickup of whole items). M alone takes 0 kg of the fixture at 150 kg free, where shipped takes 140 kg, and it locks out every ship whose hold is below the canister mass. M-P removes that regression and keeps the hold limit. If partial pickup is rejected, use M-T with an approved T, and accept that T sets the smallest hold that can take anything. The reproduction in section 13 step 1 still gates the work: M does not fix the measured missed-contact case.

Unapproved parameters. The cap value (1,000 kg is "maybe"). Global cap or wreck-only cap. The packing rule (fill-to-cap in id order, decreasing unit mass, or balanced). Whole-fit or partial pickup, and for partial pickup the item order, the ready meaning and the door rule. Whether M-T exists, and its T. Whether a mixed tag lists its items.

Decision for a later implementation: approve candidate M at 1,000 kg with partial pickup of whole items (M-P), or with whole-fit kept and the low-hold regression accepted.
