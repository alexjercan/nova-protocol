## Agent findings
- These findings describe `master` at `112b79a0d`, before the approved change.
- Wrecks call `strip_parts()` at full share; it floors each recipe-part yield independently (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/inventory.rs:1563-1580`). Health share applies to station stripping, not wreck drops. Root drops can be empty after all sections were lost separately; empty-drop frequency is unmeasured. `wreck_cargo` gathers a collapsed root's hold, station storage and surviving sections into one drop, then splits each item type into separate canisters (`crates/nova_scenario/src/wreck_cargo.rs:61-159`). Sections shot off earlier leave separate drops. One drop is not one canister.
- `CargoCanister` already stores mixed stacks and caps each physical canister at 200 kg (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/inventory.rs:1234-1327`). The wreck producer does not use its mixed-stack capability. The intake requires contact and enough free hold mass for the entire canister; it has no partial take (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/cargo_intake_section.rs:511-646`). Pickup sight is guidance, not auto-collection (`crates/nova_hud/src/pickup_sight.rs:175-227`). Save checks the canister cap and saved references can target its durable ID (`crates/nova_world_base/src/save/mod.rs:879-955`; `save/controls.rs:396-397`).
- Existing collapse fixture produces ten canisters holding 1,300 kg in one drop (`crates/nova_scenario/src/wreck_cargo/tests.rs:175-235`; `crates/nova_gameplay/src/test_support.rs:78-125`); no 200 kg-capped policy can make this one physical canister. Seven is the theoretical minimum and attainable with a specified mixed packing; this is a model, not an implemented result. An earlier bounded range run logged 36 separate one-stack wreck drops and ended with 17 live SectionPanel canisters, nearest 49.2 m, 13,400 kg free hold and no pickup. Thus neither multi-item packing within one drop nor whole-fit refusal explains this case; the fate of other cans and the exact missed-contact cause are unproven (`tasks/20261026-003724/evidence/`). Its score records 5,000 PDC rounds spent, contradicting the pilot's 4,000-round account.
## Decisions
- Approved 2026-10-30: M with M-P at a global 1,000 kg cap (no wreck-only cap, no target fill T). The implementation and asserted ECS tests are in this change; runtime pickup remains unproven.
- Implemented rule (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/inventory.rs` `CargoCanister::pack`, `take_fitting`, `fits_any`): pack fills each canister in item id order and skips a stack whose next item does not fit; a take moves `floor(free / mass)` of each stack in id order; the door opens and `ready` is true when at least one whole item fits. Jettison and mining use the same pack at the new cap.
- Candidate M (interprets the owner's dynamic range as actual filled mass under a fixed maximum; `REPORT.html` sections 5-14): the wreck producer packs one drop's stacks into mixed whole-item canisters up to the cap. Fixture 1,300 kg: shipped 10 canisters; M 2 (1,000 + 300 kg, modeled). M is per drop, not per wreck: a drop of 1,000 kg or less is one canister; separate section drops stay separate. The 36-drop bench (13,400 kg free, no contact) does not change.
- Superseded by the approval above. Unapproved parameters were: the cap value (1,000 kg); global `CARGO_CANISTER_MAX_MASS_G` (also mining, jettison, item lint, save check, wiki) or a wreck-only cap (save cannot tell canister origin); the packing rule (fill-to-cap in id order, or other); optional fixed target fill T below the cap; mixed tag text.
- Whole-fit question, resolved by M-P: with whole-fit kept, 150 kg free takes 0 kg of the fixture vs 140 kg shipped (regression); smallest useful hold rises from 30 to 300 kg. Partial pickup (M-P) takes up to 150 kg but needs an item order, `ready` and door rule. Recommendation: M with M-P (approved).
- Implementation proof: [`REPORT-implementation.html`](REPORT-implementation.html) links fresh test logs, the dirty-tree diff/hash and code assertions. One 1,300 kg drop becomes two mixed canisters (1,000 + 300 kg); with 20 kg free, the intake takes two 10 kg items and preserves the remainder id through freeze/thaw. This is not live-player proof. The original [`REPORT.html`](REPORT.html) is the dated pre-approval proposal.
- Still required before CLOSED: reproduce a starter-ship salvage run with before and after artifacts; classify each missed reward as no drop, missed contact or refusal; inspect rendered pickup frames and run defeat -> salvage -> spend. M changes only count and refusal. No guaranteed drop, recipe, reach or default is approved.
