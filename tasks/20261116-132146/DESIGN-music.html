Nova Music Plan

Background music for Nova Protocol. One theme plays on the main menu. Type 42 songs play in flight, and the song changes with what happens to you. This page is a proposal for review before the build.

Each play button plays a 40 s excerpt with 2 s fades. The excerpts are evened to one loudness, as the game evens every clip. Nobody has listened to the Type 42 songs for this plan yet: the picks come from titles, the author's text and machine measures. The excerpts are there so you can judge by ear.

When each song plays

The rule, first match wins

The game checks these in order on each frame. It plays the songs of the first line that is true. Pause is not on the list: it lowers whatever plays.

  1. Loading screen at boot (GameStates::Loading).Silence
  2. Main menu (GameStates::MainMenu). Backdrop scenes behind the menu do not count, also when they fight.Menu
  3. Editor or Mod Hub: Playing, GameMode::Sandbox, no scenario live.Silence
  4. Load gap: Playing, GameMode::NewGame, no scenario yet.Keep the song
  5. A scenario started a song with PlayMusic and it still plays. A fight takes over only when the action says in_combat: Yield.Mod song
  6. Combat mood is on (see the diagram below).Combat
  7. Threat mood is on.Enemies near
  8. Your ship is docked at a station: DockedShip on your ship, and its connection has StationDock.Docked
  9. Anything else in flight: open world, chapters, lessons, editor test flight.Cruise

Fights do not make the music flap

The mood moves up fast and comes down slowly. A lock that blinks on and off never leaves threat. A short fight blip never cuts a cruise song.

  • A song that already plays is not restarted. If the mood goes back while that song still fades out, the song comes back up.
  • After combat, the cut cruise song does not resume. The combat song plays on for 10-20 s, then a new cruise song crossfades in over 4 s.
  • Where a change would leave a pause with no music (to cruise or docked), the song that plays fills the pause, then crossfades to the new song. If it ends first, the rest of the pause is silent. If the situation changes back during the pause, the song plays on.
  • A hit is a hit on your ship or by your ship. A hostile that only flies near you is not a threat.

Background, not foreground

Music sits under the guns and under comms. Levels from the sound table, after every clip is evened to one loudness.

  • One level for every song. The game evens each song, so a loud combat master does not jump out.
  • About one minute in five of cruise is quiet: songs average 4:25 and gaps 75 s.
  • Long fades: 3-4 s in calm moods. Only the step into combat is quick, 1.5 s.
  • Pause lowers the music by 8 dB. It does not stop it.
  • The Music slider in Settings scales music only. At 0 the song runs silent, so raising the slider brings it back mid-song.

Mod music

A mod plays its own song from a scenario event, the same way it plays a sound today with PlaySound. The song is a file in the mod, found with self://. When it ends or stops, the normal rule takes over again.

Scenario actions Proposal

// In a scenario's events. Field names follow PlaySound.
(
    name: OnEnter,
    once: true,
    filters: [Entity((id: Some("wreck_field"), other_id: Some("player_spaceship")))],
    actions: [
        PlayMusic((
            sound: "self://music/wreck_theme.ogg",
            fade_in: 3.0,
            then: Loop,
            in_combat: Keep,
        )),
    ],
),
(
    name: OnDestroyed,
    once: true,
    filters: [Entity((id: Some("wreck_guardian")))],
    actions: [
        StopMusic((fade_out: 6.0)),
    ],
),
FieldValuesMeaning
soundasset ref, .oggThe song. Any content sound ref: self:// for the mod's file, dep://base/music/... for a base song.
fade_insecondsThe crossfade from what plays now.
thenResume | LoopResume: when the song ends, the rule picks the next song after its normal gap. Loop: the song repeats until StopMusic or the scenario ends.
in_combatYield | KeepYield: a fight takes over with combat songs, and the mod song does not come back. Keep: the mod song plays through fights, for a boss theme.
StopMusic.fade_outsecondsFades the mod song out and hands back to the rule at once. The rule then waits its normal gap.

Every field is required, as in PlaySound. When the scenario ends, its song fades out in 2 s and the menu theme takes over. PlaySound with category: Music stays a lint error: music goes through the music player.

Songs for the normal rule Proposal, optional

A mod can also add songs to a situation. The game shuffles the songs of every enabled mod together. The base game adds its own songs the same way.

MusicTrack((
    id: "drifter_cruise_1",
    sound: "self://music/drifter_cruise_1.ogg",
    contexts: [Cruise, Docked],
    licence: CcBy4,
    credit: "\"Drifter\" by A. Author, CC BY 4.0: https://example.org/drifter",
)),

contexts takes Menu, Cruise, Threat, Combat, Docked. licence is a closed list (Cc0, CcBy3, CcBy4, Own), so the lint fails a song with no licence.

File format: OGG Vorbis only

  • The game decodes OGG Vorbis with no extra decoder. A 4 min song at quality 4 is about 3.5 MB.
  • WAV plays, but a 4 min WAV is about 40 MB, in the download and in memory: the game keeps each song's file in memory while it plays. FLAC and MP3 need decoders the game does not build.
  • The lint refuses any other extension and shows the fix. The load refuses what the lint refuses.
error[music_format]: "self://music/wreck_theme.wav" is not OGG Vorbis.
  Music must be an .ogg file. Convert it:
    ffmpeg -i wreck_theme.wav -c:a libvorbis -q:a 4 wreck_theme.ogg

The lint also measures each song, as it does every sound: a file that does not decode or is silent is an error. PlayMusic in a menu backdrop scene is an error: the menu plays the theme.

Decisions for you

  • What starts "enemies near"?RecommendA hostile lock on you or a hostile AI engaging you, held 2 s. It matches the lock alarm you already hear. The other choice, any hostile within a distance such as 5000 m, also fires for hostiles that only pass by or sit at a station.
  • Does combat get its own songs?RecommendYes: Intense Electronic Action and The Hunter, with Digitised as a spare. Threat and combat then sound different, as you asked.
  • Music in the editor and Mod Hub?RecommendSilence. The editor is a work tool with its own sounds. A test flight uses the flight rules. The other choice is one docked song.
  • Mod music format?RecommendOGG Vorbis only, with a lint error that shows the ffmpeg command. WAV costs ten times the size.
  • Can a mod song hold through a fight?RecommendThe required in_combat: Yield | Keep field, so the author chooses per song. The other choice: a fight always takes over.
  • How many songs in the first release?RecommendAll 10 (42 min). As OGG quality 4 they measure 34.4 MB, about 31 MB at quality 3. That adds to every release zip and to git history. A 6-song start (theme, 2 cruise, 1 threat, 1 combat, 1 docked) is about 18 MB.
  • Silence between cruise songs?Recommend30-120 s at random, then tune after a play session.
  • Pause: lower or stop?RecommendLower by 8 dB. The Tab ship computer does not lower it.
