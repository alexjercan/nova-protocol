1. Recipe content format

Recommend: a new content kind Recipe, one entry per recipe, that replaces the refine field on Item. Every field is required. The station fee sits inside the site, so a ship recipe cannot carry credits by mistake.
Recipe((
    id: "base/pdc_round",
    inputs: [("IronOre", 1), ("StoneOre", 1)],
    output: ("PdcRound", 100),
    duration_secs: 8.0,          // game seconds per batch
    site: Ship,
)),
Recipe((
    id: "base/alloy_plate",
    inputs: [("IronOre", 2), ("CarbonOre", 1)],
    output: ("AlloyPlate", 1),
    duration_secs: 10.0,
    site: Station((fee_cr: 30)), // docked at a living station that offers Craft
)),

Rust types (in nova_gameplay, new module crafting.rs)

Station gate

StationServiceType::Refine becomes Craft. Generated stations offer it from advancement 0.25, as Refine does now (REFINE_ADVANCEMENT becomes CRAFT_ADVANCEMENT). The base waystation keeps it. No per-recipe gate in this slice: every station recipe works at every Craft station.

Lint (shared by content lint and the load merge)

Refused, with the recipe id: empty id; no inputs; an input count of 0; an input listed twice; an input that is the output; an unknown input or output item; an output count of 0; duration_secs not finite or not above 0; no-loss price rule (inputs at ask plus the fee are worth less than the output at bid, lot-exact, the rule refine_price_fault has now); mass rule (the output weighs more than the inputs). The mass rule means an output always fits the hold once its inputs leave it, so the runtime needs no "hold full" refusal.

Format break, marked (breaking): refine on an item, Refine in a station's services and the OnCreditsChanged cause Refine all stop working. The item refine field shipped in v0.18.0, so the changelog says how to migrate. The example mod gains one Recipe (example/survey_core: 1 SalvagedParts to 1 survey core, ship site) and its service list comment gains Craft.

Alternatives: keep the recipe on the item as craft: [..] (one item, many recipes is awkward, and a mod cannot add a recipe to a base item without replacing the item); outputs as a list for by-products (not needed by any first recipe, add later); a separate credits field beside site (allows a ship recipe with a fee, which lint must then refuse).

2. On-ship tier

Recommend: built into every hull. Any player ship can run site: Ship recipes anywhere, in flight, docked or in a fight. Station recipes are the "3x3 table".

Why: the ammo task needs every combat role to make rounds without a refit. A fabricator section needs a new section kind, mesh, editor support, AI and start-hull changes, and every current start hull would lack it on day one.

Alternative: a fabricator section (a stronger "2x2 vs 3x3" story, much larger scope; can be added later as a third site, Fabricator, with no format break for existing recipes).

  • Rate: 100 rounds per 8 s is 12.5 rounds/s, one eighth of one PDC's fire rate. Crafting refills between fights, not during a burst.
  • Price: rounds from bought ore cost about 20 cr per 100, about 1.8x the station ask of 11. Buying at a station stays the cheap path; crafting is the supply path when the station is empty or far. Mined ore costs only time. MEASURE found price is not the limit (611-763 cr net per escort), supply is.
  • Mass: 400 kg of ore for a full 2000-round refill fits the picket's 1316 kg free hold.
  • Stone ore is the cheapest ore (4 cr ask), so it keeps the cost down. Iron alone (1 IronOre to 50 rounds) would cost 32 cr per 100, about 3x the station.
  • Gap: the combat roles (bounty_hunter, military, pirate) have no mining beam, so on board they craft from ore they bought or looted. The ammo task can add ore to their start kits if play shows a need. Feel is unmeasured until the playable example runs.

Alternatives: iron only (simpler input, about 3x station price); a cheaper or faster PDC recipe (makes the station Buy pointless); also put torpedoes on the ship (they are the deliberate, expensive choice; keep them station-made).

4. Crafting as a timed job

Recommend: one craft job per ship: one recipe, N batches, on the gameplay clock. Inputs and fee are taken at the end of each batch, as plate repair spends a plate as it fits it.

Rules

  • Clock: Update on Res<Time>, like advance_ship_repair_jobs. TAB and the pause menu stop it. A ship flies and fights while it crafts.
  • Start (from the Craft pane) refuses, in this order: a craft job already runs; batches is 0; a station recipe while not docked at a living station that offers Craft; the hold (less what running repair jobs hold back, ShipRepairJobs::free_hold) is short of an input for all N batches; credits are short of N fees. A start takes nothing.
  • Each batch end re-checks the site, the inputs and the fee for one batch. On a pass it removes the inputs, adds the output and moves the fee from the ship to the station (OnCreditsChanged cause Craft on both). On a refusal the job stops with the reason; finished batches stay.
  • Station recipe: the ship must stay docked. An undock, a dead or derelict station ends the job as stopped on the next frame, with finished batches kept.
  • Cancel: any time, from the job row. Finished batches stay; nothing more is taken. Nothing is ever held "in the machine", so there is nothing to refund.
  • Concurrency: one craft job per ship. Repairs and the SOS call run beside it. Start is refused while a craft runs; the player cancels or waits.
  • End: a CraftJobEnded message drives a HUD notice (CRAFTED 500 PDC ROUND, CRAFT STOPPED: NO STONE ORE, 300 MADE, CRAFT STOPPED: UNDOCKED) and the pane note. The flight chip shows CRAFT PDC ROUND 40% when no SOS or repair job uses it.

Why inputs at batch end

It is the model the owner already approved for repairs. A cancel, a Load or a lost ship never loses inputs. The save keeps only ids and counts, with no held stack to restore or refund. The mass rule means the output always fits. The cost: if the player sells, jettisons or gives away an input mid-job, the next batch stops and says which input ran out.

Alternative: take all inputs and fees at start (the hold drops at once, but cancel must refund into a hold that may be full, and the save must keep the held stack).

Save

  • Saved as FrozenShip.craft: Option<ShipCraftJob>. No Entity, so no saved mirror type is needed. A v0.18.0 save has no field and loads with none running, the same exception as repairs.
  • The save check refuses an unknown recipe id, done >= queued, and a non-finite or over-long elapsed_secs.
  • A parked hull drops its job (nobody runs it, nothing is held).
  • Shipped-save migration: v0.18.0 saves list Refine in saved station services (FrozenStation::services, the chart's StationSummary::services). These two save fields read Refine as Craft through one save-only deserializer, so content never accepts the old name. A TODO removes it when WORLD_SAVE_VERSION moves past v0.18.0.

Alternatives: a FIFO queue of several recipes per ship (more UI and save shape, add later); keep the service named Refine (no save migration, but the name is wrong for torpedo assembly).

5. UI: the Craft pane

Recommend: its own pane, Craft, in the existing title row: Map, Ship, Inventory, Craft, Jobs, Station. M and gamepad Y step through it. It is always shown, because ship recipes work everywhere.

Why not an overlay on the Inventory pane, like the Ship pane catalogue: the catalogue's open and return flow is the problem task 20261116-133436 is fixing (TAB drops the player out). A pane has no open, no return and no separate camera. The Inventory pane (about 8.3k lines) stays the hold and trade surface. No new bar: the tab sits in the row that exists.

7. What dies, what may break, what fails loudly, proof

Dies

ItemDesign::refine, ItemRefineRecipe, plan_item_refine, ItemRefine, ItemRefineRefusalType, GameItems::refined_from, the refine block of item_pack_faults and its tests, StationServiceType::Refine, CreditChangeCauseType::Refine, REFINE_ADVANCEMENT, the Inventory Refine verb, two examples and their catalog drift rows, and the Refine docs sections.

May break

  • Mods with refine: or Refine (breaking; changelog migration note).
  • Scenario scripts that compare event.cause == "Refine" (breaking; rename).
  • v0.18.0 saves: handled by the save-only service migration and the absent craft field. Needs a test.
  • Editor exhaustive Content matches (compile errors find them).
  • Ammo economics: crafted rounds from bought ore cost about 1.8x the station ask.

Fails loudly

  • A recipe with an unknown item, bad count, bad duration, a loss on price or a gain in mass: lint error, then a load error, keyed by the recipe id.
  • An item that still has refine, or a station that lists Refine: RON parse error (deny_unknown_fields, unknown variant).
  • A save that names an unknown recipe: the world is refused with the id.
  • A running job whose recipe is not in GameRecipes: panic (the save check refuses it first).

Verification plan

  • Unit (nova_gameplay): each recipe_faults refusal; the lot-exact price rule moved over with its existing cases; plan_craft_start and plan_craft_batch refusal order.
  • ECS (nova_ship): a ship recipe finishes N batches on the game clock and takes inputs per batch; no progress while the clock is paused; cancel keeps finished batches; a station job stops on undock.
  • Save (nova_scenario, nova_world_base): a running craft job resumes after a RON round trip and thaw; an unknown recipe id refuses the world; a v0.18.0 save with Refine loads as Craft.
  • Content: content gen then content lint, base and --target example.
  • System example system_ship_craft (replaces system_station_refine), autopilot, asserted markers: (0) PDC rounds crafted undocked from the Craft pane; (1) the AlloyPlate station recipe is refused undocked on the focus card; (2) docked, it crafts and moves the fee; (3) an undock stops the station job and keeps finished batches.
  • Playable example craft_sandbox (replaces refine_sandbox): undocked near the waystation with ore aboard and the Craft pane open; hand-playable with no deadline, autopilot smoke run, README row. Frames of the Craft pane inspected.
