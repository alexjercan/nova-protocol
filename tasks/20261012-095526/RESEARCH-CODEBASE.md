## 1. Open-world bootstrap, streaming, frozen sectors, save/load

**Owners.** `crates/nova_world` is the streaming mechanism and frozen ledger and names no content (`crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:20-27`). `crates/nova_world_base` is the concrete generator `NovaLayeredWorld`, civilizations, clusters, ship planning and the save module, which is native-only: `#[cfg(not(target_arch = "wasm32"))] mod save;` (`crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:1-34`, `:49-50`). `crates/nova_menu` owns the New Game modal, Load screen, leave flow and save notice. `crates/nova_core` adds `NovaWorldBasePlugin` on the default path (`crates/nova_core/src/lib.rs:424-429`).

**How New Game starts.**
1. New Game opens a modal pre-filled with a random `u32` seed and, on desktop, a world name defaulting to "New World" (`crates/nova_menu/src/menu_ui.rs:111-115`; `crates/nova_menu/src/world_setup.rs:53`, `:98-103`).
2. Create (`world_setup.rs:324-370`): on desktop `create_world(root, name)` makes the folder and inserts `WorldSaveSession::created` (`:342-361`); then inserts `OpenWorldSession { seed }` (`:362`), sets `GameMode::NewGame`, enters `GameStates::Playing` (`:363-365`).
3. The base bundle names `new_game_scenario: Some("open_world")` (`assets/base/base.bundle.ron:304`); the scenario is `role: OpenWorld` (`assets/base/scenarios/open_world.content.ron:3-7`) and spawns the Line Warship with the stock in section 11 plus three lights. The player's `allegiance` is `None` (`crates/nova_authoring/src/base_content/scenarios/open_world.rs:79`), so `relation()` treats the player as a bystander until a hostile answers it (see section 8).
4. `sync_open_world` arms the world when an `OpenWorld` scenario is live, `OpenWorldSession` exists and exactly one `PlayerSpaceshipMarker` exists: it inserts `WorldObserver` on the player, builds `NovaLayeredWorld::from_loaded` (pinning the loaded section/item catalog) and inserts `WorldConfig { seed, sector_edge: 32 km, active_radius: 2 }` (`crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:248-342`); a wrong role or no player disarms (`:347-351`).

**Sectors and the window.** `SectorCoord {x,y,z}` centred integer cell, slug `sector_x_y_z` (`crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:289-354`). `OPEN_WORLD_SECTOR_EDGE = 32_000 m`, `OPEN_WORLD_ACTIVE_RADIUS = 2` (125 live sectors, a 160 km cube; `crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:139-145`); `ACTIVE_WINDOW_SECTORS_MAX = 125` (`nova_world/src/lib.rs:239`); radius below 1 is refused so a docked partner across a face stays live (`:564-575`). Pipeline `NovaWorldSystems::{Cleanup, Observe, Request, Collect, Materialize, Adopt, Retire}` (`:745-764`); sectors prepare on `AsyncComputeTaskPool`, one materializes per frame (`:51-92`). Components `WorldObserver`, `SectorRoot(SectorCoord)`, `PendingSectorShip`, `CurrentSector` (`crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:52`, `:60`, `:70`, `:116`).

**What fills a sector.** `SectorManifest { coord, asteroids, planets, ships }` validated by `validate_manifest` (`crates/nova_world/src/generation.rs:295-304`, `:633`; this file has uncommitted validation hardening in the working tree). Environment fields `MaterialDensity`, `Volatiles`, `HumanActivity` as Perlin fBm (`crates/nova_world_base/src/environment.rs:19-30`). Clusters on a 50 km lattice (`clusters.rs:93`), `ClusterType::{AsteroidRich, RockOnly, PlanetHeavy, DerelictField}` with worlds/hulls/rocks bands (`clusters.rs:247-276`, `:192-200`); rocks 25-120 m, planetoids 500-1600 m (`:116`, `:126`); a hull is placed only beside a rock or planetoid of its own cluster (`:33-46`); 500 m clearance (`layered.rs:31`). Civilizations: one per node of a 240 km jittered lattice, 320 km reach; `Civilization { id, centroid, status, advancement, role_preference, allegiance }`; `Living` 70%; advancement saturates at 1000 km from origin (`civilizations.rs:25-47`, `:83-88`, `:115-132`); syllable names (`generation.rs:135-165`). `ShipRoleType::{Civilian, Industrial, Scavenger, Armored}`, `SectorShipConditionType::{Intact, Derelict}`, `SectorShip` (`generation.rs:209-218`, `:99-105`, `:243-267`). `plan_ship` picks civilization (10% secondary), lays out with `generate_ship`/`generate_wreck`, and fills stock and credits by advancement (`sector_ships.rs:1-35`, `:65`, `:92-108`, `:226-235`). Every generated layout has docking ports (`ship_layout.rs:8-11`). Open TODOs: `AdvancementCurveType` and `SHIP_ADVANCEMENT_CURVE` carry `TODO(20260923-110307)` pending the owner's curve pick (`civilizations.rs:103-104`; `sector_ships.rs:62-64`); tuning constants are labelled provisional (`civilizations.rs:12-13`).

**Retirement and the frozen ledger (session memory).** `FrozenSectors(BTreeMap<SectorCoord, Arc<FrozenSector>>)` resource (`crates/nova_world/src/frozen.rs:59-62`); `FrozenSector::{Visited(Vec<FrozenBody>), Arrivals(Vec<FrozenBody>)}` (`:270-279`); a Visited record is the cell's whole state and only planetoids regenerate (`:271-273`). `FrozenBody { id, name, transform, visibility, motion, body }` from the avian pose (`:343-356`, `:486-491`). `FrozenBodyType::{Asteroid (carved field + owed ore), Ship(Box<FrozenShip>), PendingShip{id}, Canister, WreckFragment, OreDrop}` (`:419-438`). `FrozenShip` carries design source, controller, capabilities, `Option<Allegiance>`, derelict, lootable, credits and `FrozenShipState` (surviving sections by id, inventory, pinned health, damage marks, neutralized/defeated/was_armed/had_computer/had_thruster flags, `Option<FrozenAI>`) (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/spaceship.rs:583-615`); `FrozenSection` carries health, inactive, ammo/reload, ejection queue, stow, charge, animations, hinges and fixtures (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:80-92`). `FrozenAI` keeps behavior, patrol, engage grace, threat, evade, leash (`crates/nova_ship/src/input/ai/frozen.rs:27-34`). This supersedes `tasks/20260925-190131/TASK.md:45` ("defeated live ships regenerate ... known temporary limit"): `crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:28-30` now promises a retired sector comes back as it was left.

**Save and load (desktop only).** Root `$NOVA_CONFIG_ROOT/worlds` or `dirs::data_dir()/nova-protocol/worlds` (`crates/nova_assets/src/storage.rs:133-150`). One folder per world slug with `world.ron` (header), `state.<generation>.ron`, `world.lock`; atomic writes; strict format, never migrated (`crates/nova_world_base/src/save/mod.rs:1-21`). `WORLD_SAVE_FORMAT = 3` (`:63`). `WorldSaveHeader { format, name, seed, catalog (digest), mods, game_version, saved_at_unix, generation, player_sector, credits }` (`:77-98`); `WorldSaveState { format, generation, player: SavedPlayer, sectors: FrozenSectors, canister_ids_next, transients }` (`:103-117`); `SavedPlayer { id, transform, motion, ship: FrozenShip, view }` (`:122-133`), so the player's destroyed sections, attached plates, health, ammo, inventory and credits are saved. `FrozenTransient { lifetime, body }` with `{Round, Torpedo, ShedFixture, RockChunk, DetachedPiece}` saves in-flight rounds and debris (`save/transients.rs:61-81`). `WorldRefusal::{Foreign, InvalidName, NameTaken, Locked, Unreadable, Format, Catalog, Io, Unrestored}` (`:167-202`); API `list_worlds, create_world, open_world, check_world, write_world, delete_world` (`:227-691`). ## 6. Combat, damage, section loss, repair, reload

**Owner.** `crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/` (generic health graph, destroy, neutralize, carve), `crates/nova_ship/src/sections/` (weapons, ammo, fixtures, ship integrity adapter), `crates/nova_ship/src/input/ai/` (AI), `crates/nova_gameplay/src/damage.rs` (typed damage, blast).

**Pipeline.** `crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/mod.rs:1-27`: health store -> graph (`components`) -> core lifecycle (disable at zero, destroy leaves, prune, cascade) -> `explode` (detach dead body, `OnDestroyedEvent`) + `pyre` (fireball) -> `neutralize` -> `erosion` (damage level), `carve` (damage marks), `spew`/`chunk` (dust and pieces). Contact impacts deal kinetic damage from the solver impulse with a free-touch speed floor so docking scrapes cost nothing (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/core.rs:1-60`).

**Section loss.** A section at zero health is disabled; ships promote it to immediate destruction; a destroyed leaf is despawned, a non-leaf stays as `SectionInactiveMarker` (`core.rs:5-17`; `neutralize.rs:100-110`). Severed structure becomes a wreck fragment with a 10 m/s minimum separation and a 5% structural-collapse floor (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/integrity.rs:26-40`). A detached piece carries art and physics only, no `Health` or weapon, and despawns after 30 s (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen_piece.rs:1-9`; `crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/explode.rs:79-86`).

**Plating falling off.** Skin plates and greebles are `SectionFixture`s: bolted to a section, they take damage and are shed by `shed_dead_fixtures` with a 12 s drift lifetime and a 3-7 unit/s kick; "a plate leaving IS the damage read" (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/fixture.rs:1-56`, `:286-300`). The skin is a pure function of the structure, never saved; a reloaded ship recomputes it (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/shell_skin.rs:1-33`). Consequence: there is no "re-plate the hull" state; a repaired section regrows its cladding from the structure.

**Neutralized vs destroyed.** Two rules in `crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/neutralize.rs:1-25`: an ARMED ship (ever carried a weapon, `WasArmedCombatant`) is neutralized when it loses every working weapon or the flight computer it once had; an UNARMED ship of any allegiance is neutralized when it loses the last thruster it ever had (`HadThruster`); a hull that never had a thruster is only ever destroyed. A `DerelictShipMarker` root is skipped (`:116-122`). `NeutralizedMarker` is inserted once and never removed; the ship lingers as a drifting wreck (`:50-58`). `DefeatedMarker` makes `OnDefeated` fire once across neutralize and later destruction (`:42-47`).

**Weapons and ammo.** Section kinds Turret (PDC), Torpedo bay, Railgun (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:442-462`). `SectionAmmo` lives on the weapon section; absence means unlimited (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/ammo.rs:1-60`). `SectionReload` is an IDLE batch reload: after each uninterrupted delay without a shot, one batch of the weapon's `ItemDesignId` moves from the parent ship's `ShipInventory` into the magazine, bounded by `min(missing, authored amount, reserve)`; zero reserve makes no progress; an installed magazine starts full without drawing stock (`ammo.rs:14-26`, `:246-262`). The free Reload key is gone (`CHANGELOG.md:78-80`). Cheats `ammo infinite`, `ammo refill`, `ammo refill section`, `item give` exist behind `cheats enable` (`crates/nova_command/src/commands.rs:363-478`; `crates/nova_console/src/cheats.rs:35-139`).

**Repair.** Only plate repair exists. `plan_plate_repair` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/inventory.rs:917-948`): refuses NoIntegrity, Destroyed (zero HP or `IntegrityDisabledMarker`), Full, NoPlates, InsufficientPlates, ExcessPlates, in that order (`:896-911`); one `HullPlate` restores `HULL_PLATE_HEALTH = 20.0` (`:885`). It runs anywhere, undocked or docked, with no dock gate (`web/src/wiki/interface.md:163`). It never rebuilds a destroyed section. The Ship pane hosts it (`crates/nova_interface/src/ship/mod.rs:16-28`). Design decision record: `tasks/20260926-174806/TASK.md:30-41` (M0: "repair your own ship anywhere if the inventory holds plates").

**Wrecks/derelicts/salvage.** Generated derelicts spawn with every active system disabled, `lootable: true`, Neutral, no controller (sections 1 and 4). The owner's design said a destroyed ship drops only its remaining cargo as canisters (`tasks/20260926-174806/TASK.md:46`), but NO such rule is implemented at HEAD: grep for `ShipInventory` in `crates/nova_gameplay/src/integrity/*.rs` and for cargo-on-destroy handlers in `nova_gameplay`, `nova_ship`, `nova_scenario` finds nothing; `tasks/20260925-190131/TASK.md:24` also records "No general cargo-drop-on-destruction rule is approved". A destroyed ship's hold is simply lost with it; only a NEUTRALIZED or derelict hull can be looted.

**What is missing.** No section rebuild or replacement in-world; no plating purchase; no per-section cost; `AIBehaviorState::Retreat` is a stub to `Engage` (`crates/nova_ship/src/input/ai/behavior.rs:29-57`); no flee AI (owner: "do not add flee AI", `tasks/20260925-190131/TASK.md:23`).

## 7. Ship structure and modification

**Closed section vocabulary.** `SectionKind` has exactly nine kinds: Hull, Thruster, Controller, Turret, Torpedo, Railgun, Docking, CargoIntake, Mining (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/base_section.rs:442-462`); a new kind is a Rust variant plus a config module and a plugin (`:438-441`). `SectionConfig = BaseSectionConfig + SectionKind` is the unit in `GameSections` the editor places (`:487-497`).

**Design identity.** `ShipDesignSource::Inline(ShipDesign)` or `Prototype { id: ShipDesignId, section_patches }` (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/ship_design.rs:303-321`). A spawn patch (`SpaceshipSectionConfigPatch`) can move or turn a section and patch its config; it cannot add, remove or re-type a section (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/spaceship.rs:418-440`). `SectionKindPatch` is the bounded per-kind "upgrade" vocabulary: thruster, controller, turret, torpedo, railgun and docking fields; hull, intake and mining have nothing kind-specific to patch (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/patch.rs:155-176`).

**Editor is design-time only.** `nova_editor` is a scene for building a ship and handing it to a scenario; it enters only in `GameMode::Sandbox` (`crates/nova_editor/src/lib.rs:1-35`, `:272-273`, `:327`); `GameMode::NewGame` skips it (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/lib.rs:213-224`). The editor's saved document IS a mod bundle: one `Content::Ship` per design plus one `Content::Scenario` (`crates/nova_editor/src/bundle.rs:1-7`); it never touches a live ship.

**Runtime add/remove/swap.** None. A grep for refit, swap_section, add_section, attach_section, install_section finds no runtime path (grep at HEAD, no hits). The owner removed the in-game ship editor / refit task (`tasks/20261007-085535/TASK.md:12`; `tasks/20260926-174806/TASK.md:62`).

**Saved ship state.** `FrozenShip { design: ShipDesignSource, controller, capabilities, allegiance, derelict, lootable, credits, state: FrozenShipState }` thaws by spawning the ORIGINAL design and applying per-section health, inactive flag, ammo/reload, fixtures, hold, dents and AI progress (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/spaceship.rs:571-600`; `crates/nova_ship/src/sections/frozen.rs:283-310`). A missing section entry means destroyed: a saved ship never regrows a lost section (`PROGRESSION-SYNTHESIS.md:52-55`, cross-checked by the thaw contract at `frozen.rs:294-299`).

**Mass/power/controls.** Mass and torque derive from sections at spawn; the editor readout shows weight/thrust/turn (`crates/nova_editor/src/lib.rs:11`). No power model exists (grep for a power budget type found none; UNVERIFIED beyond that).

**Catalog.** Base hulls (`crates/nova_authoring/src/base_content/ships/mod.rs:31-56`, `:70-130`): `block_cutter` Utility Cutter (small unarmed workboat), `block_hauler` Bulk Hauler (unarmed freight), `block_workship` Utility Workship (Season One player hull, port collar, no gun), `block_frame_tender` / `_damaged` Frame Tender, `block_wreck_plate` Carrier Wreck: Plating (no computer, drive or gun), `block_gunship` Patrol Gunship (six PDC), `block_picket` Salvage Picket (one nose gun), and `block_line_warship` Line Warship, the open-world player hull (six PDC, spinal railgun, two torpedo bays, collar per shoulder, dorsal intake, bow mining beam) (`crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:118-121`; `CHANGELOG.md:111-114`). The `cargoa`/`racer`/`cargob` families now live only in the example mod `webmods/the-ledger` (`webmods/the-ledger/the-ledger.bundle.ron:16-37`), not in base. Every other open-world ship is generated from section facts, never a catalog design (`tasks/20260923-110307/TASK.md:84`).

**How a ship can change in-world today.** Only by: losing sections (combat), losing plating (shed), plate repair of a surviving section, ammo and cargo stock, credits. Nothing adds capability.

## 8. Relations, AI and encounters

`Allegiance::{Player, Enemy, Neutral}`; no Ally variant, allies ARE `Allegiance::Player` (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/relations.rs:37-44`; `tasks/20260925-190131/TASK.md:39`). `Relation::{Own, Hostile, Neutral}`; `relation()` is Own for same combatant side, Hostile for Player vs Enemy, Neutral for anything involving Neutral or `None` (`relations.rs:48-72`). `RetaliationTarget(Option<Entity>)` and `ship_relation()` make a pair Hostile while either answers the other (`:81-110`); projectiles inherit the owner's party (`:113-127`). Retaliation rule (`crates/nova_ship/src/input/ai/threat.rs:188-206`): an armed Neutral answers the ship that damaged it most recently; an unarmed Neutral answers nobody; a Player-aligned ship hit by the player does not turn; the player hitting a docked Neutral forces undock. `AIBehaviorState::{Idle, Patrol, Orbit, Engage (default), Evade, Retreat (stub to Engage)}` (`crates/nova_ship/src/input/ai/behavior.rs:29-49`). `AIPatrolRoute { waypoints, stops, current, hold }` (`:81-94`), `AIOrbitDirective` (`:146-149`), `AILeash { center, radius }` with 0.8 hysteresis (`:157`, `:181-187`), `AIEngageRange` default 4 km (`:202`, `:214`), `AINonCombatant` (`ai/mod.rs:299`). `SpaceshipController::{None, Player, AI(AIControllerConfig { patrol, patrol_stops, orbit, leash, engage_delay, engage_range, .. })}` (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/spaceship.rs:46-54`, `:97-157`). No crew concept; the role decides the loadout.

**Encounter spawning.** No director and no density draw: intact ships from living civilizations become AI at the cluster's planned hull slots, with `leash: 5 km`, `engage_delay: 8 s` for Enemy only, not lootable; derelicts get `controller: None`, Neutral, `lootable: true` (`crates/nova_world_base/src/sector_ships.rs:85-89`, `:286-300`); patrols are 4 waypoints at 1.5 km with 30-60 s stops (`:72-82`, `:342`); each civilization draws Enemy/Player/Neutral at 1/3 each (`civilizations.rs:129-131`). Restock: none; a frozen sector keeps loot and damage (`sector_ships.rs:27-28`). Docking admission (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/docking_section/admission.rs:36-70`): a neutralized ship always admits (boarding); otherwise refused if hostile, Enemy, retaliating or AI-engaged. HUD: green ally / red threat / grey neutral triangles (`crates/nova_hud/src/allegiance_markers.rs:1-4`); `MapContactKind::{OwnShip, Ally, Hostile, Objective, Terrain, Neutral, Planet}` (`crates/nova_interface/src/map/contacts.rs:22-37`).

**Explicitly missing.** No faction standing or reputation (owner deleted the reputation/diplomacy task, `tasks/20261007-085535/TASK.md:12`); no trade AI, no autonomous mining AI, no flee (`tasks/20260925-190131/TASK.md:23`); no inter-cluster travel; no civilization-wide reaction to the player.

## 9. Stations

Nothing station-like exists. "station" appears only as AI station-keeping, ship-layout cross-section stations, the waystation backdrop and a name-template slot (`crates/nova_scenario/src/names.rs:253`). Docking: "There is no station service here: two ships are held in the pose they met in" (`crates/nova_ship/src/sections/docking_section/mod.rs:12-16`); the dock is a `FixedJoint` between two dynamic roots with one helm (`:7-10`, `:18-22`); one connection per ship, a second DOCK is refused (`docking_section/connection.rs:100-108`). The waystation backdrop is a `Backdrop` of a planetoid, two orbiting haulers, three Beacons labelled DOCK-A/DOCK-B/TRAFFIC and 18 rocks; no station hull and not open-world content (`crates/nova_authoring/src/base_content/scenarios/main_menu/waystation.rs:128-260`). `ScenarioObjectKind` has six variants and no Station (`crates/nova_scenario/src/actions/spawn.rs:127-143`). `RigidBody::Static` is used only by planets and anchors (`crates/nova_scenario/src/objects/planet.rs:205`; `objects/anchor.rs:72`); every ship is Dynamic (`spaceship.rs:560`); docking needs a `Docking` SECTION, so a planet or anchor cannot host a port (`docking_section/port.rs:13-15`). `ClusterType` has no station type (`clusters.rs:247-257`); `ShipRoleType` is closed (`generation.rs:209-218`).

**Representing a station as a thrusterless Spaceship today.** Precedents: the gauntlet "Battery" is "a Spaceship in kind only... no thrusters, no controller" (`crates/nova_authoring/src/base_content/scenarios/main_menu/gauntlet.rs:100-107`); `block_frame_tender_damaged` has no drive (`ships/block.rs:325`); `block_wreck_plate` has no computer, drive or gun (`ships/mod.rs:47-48`). Rules that apply: a hull that never had a thruster is never neutralized by the thruster rule, only destroyed; an armed one is neutralized on losing all weapons or a computer it once had (`neutralize.rs:2-10`, `:84-89`). What breaks or is unknown: it is Dynamic, so wells and collisions move it; `controller: None` "station-keeps" only if it has thrusters (`spaceship.rs:47`); helm/mass handling of a docked immobile partner is UNVERIFIED; trading needs a calm, non-lootable Neutral or Player-aligned partner; it would persist as a `FrozenShip` with a generated or authored id, but there is no identity for a player-placed body (only canisters mint ids, `save/mod.rs:110-112`); a station would need its own `ScenarioObjectKind` or a flagged `Content::Ship` plus a frozen body type to be addressable and distinct from a wreck.

## 12. Mods and extension points

**Content kinds.** `Content::{Section, Scenario, Campaign, Style, Ship, Lesson, UiTheme, Item}` (`crates/nova_modding/src/lib.rs:78-137`). Adding a kind is one enum variant plus one router arm (`:71-76`). Item is the newest: "a mod adds an item by declaring a new id; only a mod that depends on the item's owner may declare its id again, and then replaces the whole item; two packs that declare one id without that are both refused" (`:124-134`).

**Bundle.** A mod is a directory with `<id>.bundle.ron` listing `content` files, `resources` (GLB, thumbnails) and `meta { name, description, author, version, dependencies }` (`webmods/the-ledger/the-ledger.bundle.ron:1-46`). Shipped example mods: `webmods/the-ledger` (campaign, sections, ships, six scenarios) and `webmods/gauntlet`.

**Lint stages.** `content lint` walks every repo bundle, parses content, runs reference/geometry checks (`nova_scenario::lint`), combat balance, flight-rig input overlaps, lesson lint, theme lint, item pack faults and generated-ship part-pack validation, into one `ContentReport` with `Severity::{Error, Warn}` (`crates/nova_authoring/src/lint_walk.rs:1-60`; `crates/nova_authoring/src/content_report.rs:1-60`; CLI at `crates/nova_authoring/src/cli.rs:57-80`). Base RON is generated from Rust builders by `content gen` (`cli.rs:59-60`); never hand-edited (project rule). Item rules: non-empty id/name/about, `mass_g > 0`, `bid <= ask`, ore fits one canister, duplicate refusal unless dependent (`tasks/20260930-100831/TASK.md:21-24`; implemented by `item_pack_faults(packs: &[ItemPack]) -> BTreeMap<String, Vec<String>>` at `crates/nova_assets/src/items.rs:46`, shared by the merge and the lint). Lint is stricter than the merge: it checks every repo pack together (`TASK.md:23`).

**What a mod can add today that matters for progression.** Items (generic stock with mass and prices), sections (any of the nine kinds, with GLB), ship designs (used only by authored scenarios; generated world ships draw on section FACTS, not mod designs, `tasks/20260923-110307/TASK.md:28`), scenarios/campaigns (not the open world), styles, lessons, themes. A mod cannot add: a scenario object kind, a section kind, an AI behaviour, a civilization role (`ShipRoleType` closed, `tasks/20261007-085535/TASK.md:31`), a service, an event or action, or a save field.

**Owner rules that bind extension design.** Closed vocabulary, no embedded scripting; "anything more is Rust code written for the open-world mode" (`tasks/20260824-125938/RESEARCH.md:502-507`). Explicit authoring, known ids, fail at lint then load; `Option` only when absence has defined behaviour (`AGENTS.md` Change policy). The base game lives in the base bundle, never a mod (`RESEARCH.md:510-512`). Open-world modding (items and other hardcoded identities) is a v0.16.0 preference (`tasks/20261007-085535/TASK.md:11`).

## 13. Death, respawn, fail state

`OnDestroyed[player] -> Outcome(Defeat, "Your ship was destroyed.")` is the only outcome (`assets/base/scenarios/open_world.content.ron:171-185`; `open_world.rs:56-64`). Defeat overlay (`crates/nova_menu/src/outcome.rs`): title DEFEAT (`:72`); primary Retry only if a `NextScenario` is queued, which `open_world` never queues (`:98-101`); "Load last save" when no next scenario, a `WorldSaveSession` exists and the outcome is Defeat, desktop only (`:102-107`, `:183-189`); "Main Menu" always (`:190-194`). On web the only option is Main Menu. Load last save (`leave.rs:1-20`, `:44-51`, `:128-170`) calls `stop_saving` so nothing is written (`session.rs:177-181`), waits for in-flight writes, drops the lock, then `open_world -> resume_world -> LoadScenario`. Pause-menu Retry reads "Load last save" in a saved world (`pause.rs:407-413`, `:628-641`); without a session Retry reloads the scenario pristine with the same seed because `OpenWorldSession` lasts the run (`nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:147-151`) and the ledger clears. Death never overwrites the last good save (`session.rs:76-80`; `leave.rs:10-12`). No permadeath, no ship loss, no respawn in place: the cost of death is progress since the last sector crossing. A NEUTRALIZED player ship (all weapons gone, or computer lost) gets no outcome because the scenario listens only to `OnDestroyed`; what the player experiences then is UNVERIFIED (likely a drifting, unflyable hull with the pause menu as the only exit).

Reading of the table: the game already remembers a physical ledger (what the player broke, took, carved, left) but no abstract ledger (no counters, flags, goals, clock, or history). Progression that is "the ship and the hold" is nearly free; progression that is "a number the world tracks" has no home yet.

### Gaps that any progression direction hits

1. **No station identity.** No `ScenarioObjectKind::Station`, no static dockable body, no frozen body type for a placed structure; a station today is a thrusterless Dynamic Spaceship (section 9; `crates/nova_scenario/src/actions/spawn.rs:127-143`; `docking_section/mod.rs:12-16`). Nothing mints an id for a player-placed body; only canisters have an allocator (`save/mod.rs:110-112`).
2. **No section rebuild or upgrade path.** Sections are a closed 9-kind enum authored per design; a destroyed section is pruned and only plating is repairable (section 7; `crates/nova_ship/src/sections/patch.rs:155-176`; `integrity.rs:26-40`). Refit was removed from the backlog (`tasks/20261007-085535/TASK.md:12`). Any "earn a better ship" direction needs either hull swap (new design source saved into `SavedPlayer.ship`) or a section rebuild rule.
3. **No world clock and nothing frozen simulates** (`nova_world/src/lib.rs:116-117`). Restock, repair-over-time, patrol drift, price drift and "return later" all need a clock plus a catch-up rule at thaw.
4. **Generated bodies are scoped but not addressable** by scenario ids (`crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:176-182`); `OnDestroyed`, `OnDocked`, `OnEntered` and friends can only name authored ids. Open-world goals cannot point at "that derelict" without a naming or query bridge.
5. **No events for the open-world verbs.** Section 11 lists the event vocabulary; there is no event for sector crossing, ore mined, item picked up, trade completed, or credits changed, and no action that grants items or credits (`crates/nova_scenario/src/events.rs:76-160`; `actions/mod.rs:124-481`). Objectives today can only watch destruction, docking, zones, speed and elapsed time (`queries.rs:14-62`).
6. **Scenario state is not saved.** Variables, timers, objective progress and `GameMode` extras die with the run (`save/mod.rs:103-117`). A goal, charter or contract needs a save field and a format bump (`WORLD_SAVE_FORMAT`, `save/mod.rs:63`; no migration by policy, `:1-21`).
7. **Web has no persistence** (`crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:49-50`; `menu_ui.rs:116-122`). Any direction that assumes a return visit is desktop-only unless browser storage is added.
8. **Defeat is total but cheap**: the only outcome is `OnDestroyed -> Defeat`, and the cost is progress since the last crossing (section 13). Neutralization of the player has no outcome at all (UNVERIFIED what the player sees).
9. **No cargo drop on destruction** (section 6): a destroyed ship's hold vanishes, so loot comes only from neutralized or derelict hulls.
10. **Economy is static per civilization advancement** (section 4): prices and stock are planned at generation, there is no restock, and no trade AI moves goods.

### Owner constraints recorded in task files (binding on the design doc)

- Closed vocabulary, no scripting; everything through authored content and lint (`tasks/20260824-125938/RESEARCH.md:502-512`; AGENTS.md "explicit authoring, fail at lint then load").

- Physical ship focus: sections, plating, ammo and hold are the character sheet (`tasks/20260930-100831/PROGRESSION-SYNTHESIS.md`; `PROGRESSION-BUILDER.md`).
- Themed click-based UI; the `:` command modal stays for commands (`tasks/20260926-174836/TASK.md:1`, `:32`, `:40`; `crates/nova_interface/src/lib.rs:1-27`).
- Save points at crossings and leave, never anywhere-saves; no save compatibility across versions (`save/mod.rs:1-21`; `session.rs:47-52`).
- Repair anywhere with plates, not at a facility (`crates/nova_gameplay/src/inventory.rs:885-948`).
- Base lives in the base bundle; mods must have extension points (`Content::*`, `crates/nova_modding/src/lib.rs:71-137`); no silent defaults; `Option` only with defined absence (AGENTS.md change policy).

### Cheapest hooks (ordered by how much existing machinery they reuse)

1. A `SavedPlayer`/`WorldSaveState` field for abstract progress (goal id, counters) plus a format bump: touches `save/mod.rs` only, but requires a reader.
2. Open-world events (`OnSectorEntered`, `OnMined`, `OnPickedUp`, `OnTraded`, `OnCreditsChanged`) in `crates/nova_scenario/src/events.rs` with the existing `Objective`/`Outcome` actions: makes open-world goals authorable without scripting.
3. Item and credit grant actions in `actions/mod.rs`: rewards without a new system.
4. A `Station` object kind or a flagged `Content::Ship` with `RigidBody::Static` and a `Docking` section: unlocks the trade/repair/home-base family; needs a frozen body type and id minting.
5. A clock in `WorldSaveState` with per-body thaw rules: unlocks restock and repair-over-time; largest blast radius (every `FrozenBodyType`).
