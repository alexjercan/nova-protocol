# Story research notes

These notes collect techniques, not setting material to transplant. Each source
review must record what Nova can learn, one risk, and the originality boundary.
No dialogue, distinctive terminology, scene composition, or protected text is
copied.

## Research questions

- How does routine work create attachment before a disaster?
- How does an ordinary crew become involved in a political conflict without
  becoming chosen heroes?
- How can recovered evidence reveal a conspiracy through gameplay?
- How can factions remain understandable without exposition dumps?
- How can a short campaign end at worker scale while leaving a larger setting
  alive?

## Initial source queue

### Leviathan Wakes

James S. A. Corey, Orbit, 2011.

Study the movement from working ship and crew into a conflict larger than the
survivors. Focus on the inciting loss, incomplete evidence, competing public
interpretations, and the difference between personal and political points of
view.

Originality boundary: do not reproduce its political map, protomolecule plot,
missing-ship investigation, named organizations, character pairings, or
specific destruction sequence.

### Alien

Directed by Ridley Scott, screenplay by Dan O'Bannon, 1979.

Study commercial crew hierarchy, compensation concerns, maintenance language,
and how ordinary disagreement makes a ship feel inhabited before danger takes
over.

Originality boundary: do not reproduce its creature premise, distress-call
trap, corporate orders, crew archetype mapping, or scene sequence.

### Planetes

Makoto Yukimura, manga serialized 1999-2004.

Study orbital labor as a complete social world: debris work, safety procedure,
status, institutional pressure, and personal ambition. Test how technical work
can expose values rather than pause the story.

Originality boundary: do not import its debris crew, employers, accident plots,
politics, or character arcs.

### Outland

Written and directed by Peter Hyams, 1981.

Study an isolated industrial community, employer power, worker vulnerability,
and a conflict whose scale remains local and material.

Originality boundary: do not reproduce its marshal role, drug conspiracy,
western plot, station design, or climax.

### Battlestar Galactica miniseries

Developed by Ronald D. Moore, 2003.

Study how sudden destruction changes routine command relationships, how a
surviving group chooses an immediate purpose, and how absence and silence make
large loss comprehensible.

Originality boundary: do not import extinction stakes, machine antagonists,
military command structure, pursuit premise, or fleet politics.

## Review format

Each completed review should contain:

1. Bibliographic reference and the part reviewed.
2. Craft problem being studied.
3. A short technique summary in original words.
4. Why the technique works.
5. A possible Nova adaptation stated at an abstract level.
6. A failure mode or cost.
7. The originality boundary.
8. One concrete question for the campaign outline.

## First comparative hypotheses

These are questions to verify through focused review, not settled conclusions:

- Routine becomes story when procedure exposes status, trust, or disagreement.
- Evidence becomes story when each item changes what the player believes or
  must do.
- Factions become legible through incompatible goals under pressure, not a list
  of historical facts.
- A worker-scale protagonist can affect who learns the truth without personally
  defeating every force behind it.
- The destruction will hurt more if later wreck objects correspond to people,
  places, or routines established before the attack.
