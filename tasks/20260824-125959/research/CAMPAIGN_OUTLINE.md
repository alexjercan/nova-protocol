# Campaign outline workspace

The campaign is outlined in full before later scenarios or final dialogue are
authored. Four to six chapters is a scope target, not a fixed count.

## Whole-story fields

- **Story promise:** Industrial workers survive a political act they do not
  understand.
- **Central dramatic question:** Unresolved.
- **Antagonist and objective:** Unresolved.
- **Cutter One crew objective:** Survive and preserve Meridian's evidence;
  longer objective unresolved.
- **Final worker-scale choice:** Unresolved.
- **Ending image:** Unresolved.

## Current spine

### Chapter 1: First Shift

- **Opening state:** Experienced crew performs routine industrial recovery.
- **Change:** A dark Earth Navy warship destroys Meridian.
- **Player contribution:** Completes the work, reaches the only safe vantage,
  witnesses the attack, and survives by accident.
- **Knowledge gained:** The attacker uses an Earth Navy hull without a fleet
  code. Meridian receives no answer.
- **Closing image:** Cutter One beside the wreck while an automatic beacon
  transmits.
- **Themes and motifs:** work, trust, procedure, home, orbit, silence,
  insignificance, wrong place / wrong time.

### Chapter 2: Second Shift

- **Opening state:** Cutter One returns to Meridian's wreck.
- **Change:** A cleanup group arrives for the evidence.
- **Player contribution:** Recovers three recorders and escapes, quietly or
  under pursuit.
- **Knowledge gained:** Not yet authored. Each recorder needs one distinct
  revelation.
- **Closing image:** Cutter One clears the belt with evidence and no trusted
  recipient.
- **Themes and motifs:** aftermath, fragments, testimony, pursuit, cover,
  responsibility, being seen.

### Chapter 3

Unresolved. It must answer who Cutter One seeks after the escape and why that
party might help, exploit, suppress, or reinterpret the evidence.

### Chapter 4

Unresolved. It must force the crew to act on a more accurate understanding of
the attack rather than only receive more lore.

### Chapter 5 or final chapter

Unresolved. It must close the crew's immediate problem through a consequential
choice at worker scale. The wider political conflict may continue.

## Chapter card

Use this for every chapter before implementation:

- Opening state.
- Crew objective.
- Opposing objective.
- Player verbs and playable pressure.
- New fact or changed interpretation.
- Character relationship under pressure.
- Irreversible turn.
- Closing image.
- Setup paid off.
- New setup planted.
- Theme words.
- Visual motifs.
