  assert step, not by the screenshots.

  not observe stock at generation and cannot tell the floor from a natural share. The floor is proven by the unit test.
- The zero-credit Sell refusal is proven only by the unit test. No run performs a Sell.
- PDC consumed differs a lot between runs (628 / 1033 / 87) with the same seed and commit. No cause is asserted. Do not
  average the runs. Berthing uses the shared walk's fixture moves; flight, fire, hand-in and Buy are live systems.

  unit test (11:16:28Z-11:18:07Z) overlapped run 1's build and first walk minute. No timing claim is made.
