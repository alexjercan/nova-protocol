1. Finding

MEASURED On the arsenal bench fixture, seed 7, the player raised weapons, pointed the nose at ram_target (2.62 km) and pressed the Lance bay with no combat lock. The bay launched and spent a round. The torpedo flew down the nose, touched the target, and broke up with a brief spark (sampled frames 1730 and 1733, gone by 1736). No warhead fired, no explosion sound played, and the target kept 2950 / 2950 health. The contact did push the target from rest to 8.5 m/s, so it drifted from 2.62 km to 3.44 km by the end of the run. Ten seconds after launch the idle reload took one Torpedo from the hold (2 → 1). The matched run with a radar lock on the same bearing fuzed 2.66 units from the target skin, played explosion.wav (recorded pair), and took the target from 2950 to 275.86 health (neutralized, 39 nodes destroyed).

The player gets no cue at the press. The tutorial says "Torpedoes require a combat lock", but the bay does not enforce it for the player. The AI never launches without a ship target (source, S11). So the weakness is a decision/feedback gap with a real cost (finite, purchased stock), not missing guidance: PN and the terminal weave work as designed.

9. Limits

  • One seed, one bay type (Lance), one target class (light freighter). Serpent weave was not run; source says the fuze path is the same.
  • No PDC was engaged in these runs (the raider wakes at 60 s; launches were at ~20 s). PDC interaction with dumb-fire torpedoes is source-only.
  • The headless pairs ran with the scripted cmd: agent, not a human or the pi pilot. It shows the mechanic, not how often players hit it.
  • No existing range or bench play covers an unlocked player launch. system_torpedo_launch, system_torpedo_capital and stress_torpedoes all commit a target or a position (scout survey, spot-checked).
  • No production code, content, asset or test was changed. No checks or test suites were run.
