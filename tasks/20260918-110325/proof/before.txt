Before artifact: the manual speed governor kills a cross-nose burn
=================================================================

Captured 2026-09-18 against the unmodified tree at cf35fe2a7.

Rig: the shared `flight_app` / `spawn_ship` harness in
`crates/nova_ship/src/flight/tests/support.rs`. Nose on -Z, single centered
rear main drive. The ship is given `LinearVelocity(Vec3::X * 20.0)` - pure
crossing velocity, the state a pilot is in after building speed and turning -
plus a held `FlightIntent { burn: 1.0 }`. It runs 600 fixed frames (10 s).
The capped leg carries `FlightSpeedCap(20.0)`; the uncapped leg is the same
rig with the component left off, as the delivery guard.

The temporary test used for this capture was removed after the run. The
permanent inverted proof is D3a in the implementation plan.

Reading
-------

At the cap the held full burn delivers EXACTLY zero delta-v along the nose over
ten seconds - the velocity vector is bit-identical before and after - because
`speed_budget_scale` in `crates/nova_ship/src/flight/manual.rs` shrinks a step
that is purely tangential to the budget sphere down to nothing, so the main
drive is dead until the pilot first brakes along the axis they came in on. The
same rig with no `FlightSpeedCap` gains 209.94 u/s along its nose over the same
600 frames, which proves the harness, the drive and the burn intent all work
and isolates the governor as the cause.
