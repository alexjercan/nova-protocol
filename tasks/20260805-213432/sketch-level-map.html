Shakedown Run - level layout sketch

Orthographic views of chapter one with the belt added and the planetoid brought in. Every coordinate below is a starting point for iteration, not a target. Existing objects keep their positions unless marked. Grid squares are 100 world units.

Legend

What changes

  • Planetoid moves in from (1240,-105,-700) to (520,-40,-520), where it anchors the beacon-1 turn and is the landmark you steer by.
  • SOI is authored, not derived - pinned at 300u instead of swinging 560-960u by seed. That is what lets the planetoid come this close without swallowing the crate beat.
  • Belt = 5 seeded knot discs bending around the planetoid, plus a sparse far layer for parallax. Two scales, like the reference shot.
  • Beacon 4, the coast ring and the orbit band ride along with the planetoid.
  • Beacons 1-3, the crates, the derelict, the pirate and every beat stay put.

Object schedule

Why it is shaped like this

  1. Two scales. Near knots at r 1.2-3.4, far layer at r 4-10 - the reference's split. One size band reads as noise.
  2. The belt is a sector, not a ring. A full annulus at r 380-560 around the planetoid would contain both beacons, the debris cluster and all three crates. The banana stops before the salvage field on purpose, and the crate beat sits in the hollow just past its end.
  3. Gates, not a wall. Three knots alternate sides of the beacon-1 -> beacon-2 line, leaving a 60-70u open lane on the far side of each. At the 25 u/s starter cap that is a weave you can fail cheaply, not a maze.
  4. Pockets are load-bearing. Clear air at the crate cluster, the pirate fight and the orbit ring - the reference scene keeps the same hollow around its subject.
  5. Rocks do not fall in. Asteroids never carry GravityAffected, so belt rocks inside the planetoid's SOI stay where they are placed.

Open questions for this round

  1. Leg length. Beacon 1 -> beacon 2 is 301u, about 12 seconds at the speed cap. That fits three gates. Push beacon 2 out to roughly (430,40,-250) for a 443u, ~18 second slalom with five gates?
  2. Belt heaviness. The knots below are ~26 rocks each, ~130 in the belt plus 30 in the far layer. Heavier, lighter, or start here and look at it?
  3. Planetoid scale. Nominal radius stays 20 (body 70-120u by seed). Bigger reads as more of a landmark but eats more of the frame.
  4. Does the banana bend the right way? It is concave toward the planetoid here - the player flies the inside of the curve with the body always off to their left.
