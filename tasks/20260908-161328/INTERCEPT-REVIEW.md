## Page 13 decision

The script deliberately leaves connecting hardware open. Both accepted ship
models have side transfer collars. Their shapes alone do not establish a sealed
connection, safe clearance, or an assisted passage.

Recommendation: a short rigid sealed connector between the existing collars,
with the ships facing opposite directions and both coasting. This retains the
hull designs and keeps the transfer direct. It still needs a consistent drawing
and clearance check before it becomes scene staging, not an engineering standard.

Alternative: a longer deployable passage. It gives more hull separation but
adds equipment and deployment steps that are not yet authored.

Neither option has been implemented. Page 13 stays scripted until the user
chooses. Owen's exact support and transfer staging remains a later page-14 review.
