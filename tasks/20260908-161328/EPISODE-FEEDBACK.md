# Episode-one feedback: 2078 and the working crew

## Approved decisions

The user accepted the proposed corrections and the small action pass within
four opening pages. The eighteen-page episode remains one arc through the
rescue, homecoming, and delivery penalty. This is not publication approval or
approval of the whole season.

- Use 2078 on Earth's calendar, not a new local era. The first permanent station
  is still roughly 10-15 years earlier; no founding date, day, month, or moon is
  chosen. The approved year is in the encyclopedia introduction and outline.
- Introduce Samir through the residential supply check on page 2 while Leila
  examines the isolated pump. Keep the coffee exchange, window scene, and silent
  departure. No extra page or artificial danger is added.
- Leila travels with Kaveri and inspects the replacement in person at Aquila.
  Samir checks identification and records; Rina handles the load.
- Simplify the EarthWorks call: request funding, refuse, state that scheduled
  recovery is too late, refuse the cost. Preparation continues throughout.
- Stage the connected ships and Owen's transfer in freefall. Guide and secure
  his motion; do not carry his weight or imply that weightlessness cures injury.
- Retain Gantry's ordinary-work introduction, first departure, and recognizable
  intact/damaged form. Preserve qualitative arrival ordering without an orbital
  spreadsheet or invented countdown. Use `practice`.
