<h2 id="page-5-arrivals" tabindex="-1">Page 5: Arrivals</h2>

<h3 id="panel-5a" tabindex="-1">Panel 5a</h3>
<p>Aquila from Kaveri's approach. Give the port useful scale and activity without
adding a roster of identifiable background ships. Kaveri remains the camera's
anchor. A location card says <strong>AQUILA / Saturn system / Later / Farspan's freight
hub</strong>; &quot;Later&quot; is a draft stamp, not a fixed travel duration.</p>
<p><strong>Tomas / comms:</strong> Aquila, Kaveri. Inbound for the Clearwell collection.</p>
<h3 id="panel-5b" tabindex="-1">Panel 5b</h3>
<p>At the collection area, Rina checks the replacement assembly's restraints.
Samir compares its identification with Leila's request. Jonah helps keep the
work area clear; nobody stands idle waiting for a character introduction.</p>

<p><strong>Rina:</strong> Check them before we make it difficult to open.</p>
<h3 id="panel-5c" tabindex="-1">Panel 5c</h3>

<p><strong>Samir:</strong> Even the awkward one?</p>

<h2 id="page-6-another-crews-work" tabindex="-1">Page 6: Another crew's work</h2>

<p>From beside Kaveri's load, Rina can see Gantry's loading area. Nadia waits for
Clearwell's assembly to clear their shared working space. Gantry's name is
visible on the intact hull. Its unarmed workship design should be recognizable

<p><strong>Rina:</strong> Nearly. This end's heavier than it looks.</p>

<h3 id="panel-6c" tabindex="-1">Panel 6c</h3>

<h2 id="page-9-what-it-will-cost" tabindex="-1">Page 9: What it will cost</h2>

<p><strong>Leila:</strong> If their port is sound, we can dock. We don't need to restart their main bus.</p>
<p><strong>Rina:</strong> Then let's get them off it.</p>
<h3 id="panel-9c" tabindex="-1">Panel 9c</h3>

<p><strong>Elena / comms:</strong> You've seen the endurance estimate. Your three people cannot wait for it.</p>

<h3 id="panel-10c" tabindex="-1">Panel 10c</h3>

<p><strong>Elena / comms:</strong> Clearwell will cover it. Bring them home.</p>
<p><strong>Jonah:</strong> Tomas. Take the intercept.</p>

<h2 id="page-18-the-bill" tabindex="-1">Page 18: The bill</h2>

<h3 id="panel-18a" tabindex="-1">Panel 18a</h3>
<p>Jonah and Elena at the familiar window. Ebro remains part of their working view.
Elena has already spoken with Mara; there is no cutaway aboard Foundation.</p>

<p><strong>Elena:</strong> It'll hurt. We can carry it. I told you to bring them home.</p>
<h3 id="panel-18c" tabindex="-1">Panel 18c</h3>

Baikal's ordinary work continuing. End on the object and the people, not an
ominous pirate silhouette or an invitation to cheer an unpaid bill.</p>
<p><strong>Jonah:</strong> At least this one's back on budget.</p>
