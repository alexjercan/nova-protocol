## Page 5: Arrivals

**Purpose:** Arrive at a working freight town and show the crew sharing the pickup.

### Panel 5a

Aquila from Kaveri's approach. Give the port useful scale and activity without
adding a roster of identifiable background ships. Kaveri remains the camera's
anchor. A location card says **AQUILA / Saturn system / Later / Farspan's freight
hub**; "Later" is a draft stamp, not a fixed travel duration.

**Tomas / comms:** Aquila, Kaveri. Inbound for the Clearwell collection.

### Panel 5b

At the collection area, Leila is beside the assembly, inspecting its connections.
Rina checks its restraints. Samir compares the identification plate with the
order and delivery record. Jonah helps keep the work area clear. Leila has
travelled aboard Kaveri with them; she is not back at Baikal.

**Rina:** This is ours?

**Samir:** Matches the order. Leila?

**Rina:** Before I strap it down.

### Panel 5c

Leila finishes checking the connection faces in person. Samir is beside her,
ready to complete the receipt; Rina waits for her check before securing the load.

**Leila:** That's the right one. Keep the protective covers on.

**Samir:** Even the awkward one?

**Leila:** Especially the awkward one.

## Page 6: Another crew's work

**Purpose:** Establish Nadia and Gantry as working colleagues, not doomed victims.

### Panel 6a

**Rina:** Nearly. This end's heavier than it looks.

**Nadia:** I'll keep our load clear.

**Nadia:** Easier than picking both loads off the floor. Sen. Gantry.

**Jonah:** Mercer. Kaveri.

### Panel 6c

**Jonah:** So are we. Safe trip.

## Approved direction and remaining staging

- The opening is in 2078 on Earth's calendar. No day, month, or local era is
  needed for the first card; Kaveri's departure card says "Later that day."
- Keep the four-page opening, with Samir's supply check and Leila's hands-on
  inspection. All five Kaveri crew travel together; Leila checks the replacement
  in person at Aquila.
- Keep the Aquila meeting and Gantry-first departure so the hull is familiar
  before and after damage. The access/loading layout still needs visual staging.
  No exact cargo beyond ordinary supplies or Gantry destination is selected.
  Aquila is a pickup port, not a changed home port.
- Keep timing qualitative: Kaveri reaches Gantry before its reserves run out,
  scheduled recovery arrives too late, and the diversion delays Baikal's repair.
  Nearby communications can be quick; ship travel is not instant. No orbital
  spreadsheet, exact coordinates, or countdown is required for this episode.
- Design Gantry's intact form and damaged version from one model. Check its
  surviving port against Kaveri's transfer arrangement and assisted passage.
- Stage Owen's injury and continuing care without making Samir a doctor or
  establishing Baikal as a major medical centre.
- The wider EarthWorks pressure, Gantry hull recovery, survivors' later careers,
  and season ending remain open. Nothing in this rescue record proves the later
  conspiracy. Daniel and Mara are not assigned to it.
