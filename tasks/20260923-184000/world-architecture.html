1. Verdict: what the model gets right, and the corrections

Correct

  • Sectors are generated on async worker tasks and turned into ECS entities later, on the main thread. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:360
  • The generator only describes a cell. Preparation and spawning are one shared implementation in nova_world. crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:421
  • Asteroids and planetoids spawn through the same nova_scenario object factories that the scenario loader uses. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:4

Inside NovaLayeredWorld::generate

The base generator is a unit struct with no fields. Its policy lives in constants and in the feature field. crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:115 crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:137

  1. Feature field. sector_features(input) builds three Fbm<Perlin> fields (one per FeatureLayer: Asteroid, Planet, Derelict), each seeded from the world seed and the layer slug, 3 octaves, 256 km wavelength. crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:616 crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:238
  2. Candidates. One candidate per layer per lattice node (128 km spacing). The field is sampled at the node, with a fixed origin offset. At or below the layer threshold (Asteroid 0.02, Planet 0.16, Derelict 0.14) there is no candidate. Above it, strength is (value - threshold) / (0.22 - threshold), clamped to [0, 1]. The node seed draws a jitter (up to 0.45 of a half-lattice) and a radius from the layer's band. The centre is then pulled onto its owner cell's 0.7 inset. crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:439 crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:98 crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:292
  3. Thinning. A candidate loses to ANY same-layer candidate within 2 lattice nodes that overlaps it and outranks it (higher strength, then smaller id), whether or not that rival survives. Layers never thin each other, so a planet sphere may sit inside an asteroid sphere. crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:502 crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:366
  4. Cell view. Every surviving sphere that reaches the cell's box is returned, ordered by layer, then node. Each sphere has one owner cell: the cell its centre falls in. crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:622 crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:318
  5. Strengths. sector_strengths sums, per layer, strength * (1 - t^2)^2 at the cell centre (t = distance / radius), capped at 1. The generator uses only the asteroid entry. crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:696 crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:336
  6. Planetoids first (stream "planets"): one per planet sphere this cell owns, AT the sphere centre, mean radius 600 m to 1,200 m, a PlanetType and a seed. crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:152
  7. Derelicts (stream "derelicts"): 1 to 3 ships per derelict sphere this cell owns, within 6 km of its centre on each axis and inside the cell's 0.7 inset. Each has a random yaw and one of two shipped wreck designs. crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:168 crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:50 crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:299
  8. Rocks (stream "bodies"): count = min(ceil(asteroid_strength * 4), 4). Each rock is 30 m to 60 m nominal, placed within the inset around the cell centre, as one of 4 kinds. crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:203 crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:209

9. Invariants and limits

Invariants

  • One generator for each app. A second NovaWorldPlugin with another G panics at build. crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:745
  • No config means no streaming. WorldConfig is the arming switch and has no Default. crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:457
  • Exactly one WorldObserver while armed, or SectorFault::AbsentObserver panics. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:315
  • Decisions use a total order: squared distance from CurrentSector, then the coordinate. BTreeMap and BTreeSet keep worker finish order out of the result. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:464
  • Generation is pure. Every draw comes from SectorGenerationInput::stream(purpose) or the node seed, never the ambient RNG. crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:400 crates/nova_world_base/src/features.rs:419
  • Each body is owned by one cell: its id has the cell slug as a prefix, and its whole clearance sphere is inside the cell. crates/nova_world/src/generation.rs:355 crates/nova_world/src/generation.rs:449
  • Two live roots for one cell panic with DuplicateRoot. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:263
  • Every fault fails loudly on the main thread. No fault is converted to an empty cell. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:572
  • Streamed entities are scenario-scoped but not scenario-addressable. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:111

Budgets

  • Window at most 125 cells (ACTIVE_WINDOW_SECTORS_MAX). The game uses radius 2, which is exactly 125. crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:196 crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:79
  • At most 16 bodies and 4 rocks in each cell. The ship clearance is 400 m. crates/nova_world/src/generation.rs:207 crates/nova_world/src/generation.rs:218 crates/nova_world/src/generation.rs:228
  • Jobs in flight are capped at the task pool thread count. On wasm the cap is 1 and there is no parallelism. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:455 crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:80
  • At most one sector is materialized each frame. crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:69
  • NovaLayeredWorld refuses edges wider than 128 km and edges too narrow for its widest body (about 8.48 km). crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:100 crates/nova_world_base/src/layered.rs:121

Current limits

  • Determinism scope. The documented promise is: the same build on the same platform generates the same pristine sectors from a seed, in any exploration order. The code does not claim a cross-platform or cross-build identity. crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:16
  • No mutation persistence. Nothing a player changes is kept. A retired sector is generated again from the seed, so a destroyed derelict returns. crates/nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:18
  • No floating origin. Positions are world meters from the origin, and the root stays at the origin. A search of crates/ and docs/ for "floating origin" finds nothing. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:103
  • No economy, factions or traffic. Generated ships are inert scenery: SpaceshipController::None and Allegiance::Neutral. A search of both world crates for faction, economy and traffic finds no such system. crates/nova_world/src/streaming.rs:236
  • Unknown ship designs fail late. The worker can refuse only a blank design. An id that is not in the catalog panics at materialization, not at arming. crates/nova_world/src/lib.rs:35
  • Only the base world is wired into the game. AppBuilder adds NovaWorldBasePlugin only on the default plugin path. crates/nova_core/src/lib.rs:428
