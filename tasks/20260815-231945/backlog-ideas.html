Backlog ideation — beyond v0.12.0

Nine broad-on-purpose ideas, one or two sentences each, all backlog at priority 0. Deliberately unscoped — a pick gets a real rewrite before it enters a release. The v0.12.0 board: v0.12.0-tasks.html. Generated 2026-08-24.

The agent thread — grows out of the process channel

  1. idea An agent plays the game over the process channel 20260824-125929

    An external agent drives a full scenario over stdin/stdout in --norender step mode: reads snapshots, writes inputs, completes objectives with no human at the keyboard.

  2. idea Benchmark the agent: score autonomous play across scenarios 20260824-125933

    A harness that scores autonomous runs - completion, time, damage taken, ammo spent - across the scenario set, tracked release over release.

A bigger game

  1. idea Open-world mode: factions, travel, and ship progression 20260824-125938

    Mount and Blade in space, beside the scripted scenarios: freeform travel, factions with standing and conflicts you can join, upgrades and unlocks, trade and salvage - a living sandbox driven by systems, not RON scripts.

  2. idea Space stations: dockable structures with services 20260824-125943

    Dockable stations as first-class scenario objects and open-world anchors: repair, rearm, trade, mission boards. The natural showcase for huge multi-cell structures.

  3. idea More campaign chapters after the ledger 20260824-125959

    The ledger campaign ends; the story does not have to. More chapters or a second arc, authored on the improved scenario language so the beats cost lines instead of handler cascades.

Content and feel

  1. idea Railgun: a spinal kinetic weapon family 20260824-125947

    Charge time, a hitscan-fast slug, brutal recoil that interacts with the attitude model, and a distinct vacuum firing effect. A capital-ship reason to exist.

  2. idea Grow the ship cast beyond the cargo family 20260824-125951

    A capital hull, a station tug, a proper raider silhouette, civilian traffic - each with a role in scenarios or the open world, built on the generated-art pipeline.

  3. idea An audio direction pass: combat and UI soundscape 20260824-125955

    Combat weight, UI feedback, engine and RCS character, station ambience, and a music direction - the audio side has never had a dedicated pass.

Reach

  1. idea Ship packaged native releases to itch.io 20260824-130004

    Packaged native releases on itch.io (maybe Steam later) with the nix package as the reproducible base - the game reaches players who never open a repo.

These are ideation, not commitments: broad by request, so the backlog holds the direction without pretending the scoping happened. Kept beside the v0.12.0 plan in the market-research record.
