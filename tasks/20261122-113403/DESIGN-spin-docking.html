Nova Spin Docking Plan

A wreck that lost its flight computer spins forever today, and no tool can match that spin. The plan: a hull with no live flight computer lets its tumble settle to rest over about half a minute, and the capture gate measures the motion at the two port faces, not at the two hull centres.

Recommendation

E: dead hulls settle, the gate judges the ports

  • Rule. A ship root with no live flight computer, not docked, halves its spin every 8 s. 60 deg/s reaches the 5 deg/s gate in about 29 s and 1 deg/s in about 47 s. A hull with a live computer is the PD's, as today.
  • Scope. Neutralized ships, spawned derelicts, dead stations and the player's own computer-less hull. Not rocks (they tumble by design), not wreck fragments or explode pieces, not a docked pair (the driver's computer turns it).
  • Spin only. Drift stays physical and MATCH handles it. Docking only needs the spin gone.
  • Gate. Relative speed is measured face to face. A slow-spinning big hull (a dead carrier at 2 deg/s, 150 m lever: 5.2 m/s root speed) then docks, and a co-rotating approach can capture.
  • What the player sees. A tumbling wreck slows over half a minute and hangs still. The docking sight goes green when it is calm. No new text, chip or key.
  • Why not B first. The spin that blocks docking is on hulls nobody flies. Settling it fixes the owner's case at a fraction of B's cost, and D makes B possible later.

Decisions for the owner

  1. Which hulls settle?
    • No live computer: neutralized, derelict, dead station, player's own.
    • Neutralized only: as the idea says; spawned derelicts keep any spin they get.
    • Every non-player hull: also hulls with a live computer (they already hold).
    RecNo live computer. That is the exact set that spins forever today.
  2. Spin only, or drift too?
    • Spin only: drift stays physical.
    • Spin and drift: every dead hull comes to world rest.
    RecSpin only. Drift does not block docking, and salvage and rams keep their motion.
  3. How fast?
    • 4 s half-life: 60 deg/s to the gate in about 15 s.
    • 8 s half-life: about 29 s.
    • 20 s half-life: about 72 s; a fight's wreck still tumbles while you loot nearby.
    Rec8 s. Long enough to see a wreck tumble after the kill, short enough that you do not wait for it.
  4. Fix the capture gate to measure port faces?
    • Yes: same numbers, the rule measures the motion that matters.
    • No: keep centre to centre.
    RecYes. Today a perfect co-rotation at 5 deg/s on a 60 m lever is refused TOO FAST.
  5. Spin-matching MATCH or a player DOCK assist (B) now?
    • Later: a separate task if live spinning partners matter.
    • Now: in this task.
    RecLater. Nothing alive spins by design today.
