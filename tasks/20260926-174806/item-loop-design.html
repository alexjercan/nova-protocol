Summary

  • Current Every ship root carries a ShipInventory. The open-world player ship starts with 12 authored HullPlate. Nothing adds or spends items. The Inventory pane is read-only.
  • Current Ship pane Repair and Reload are free and instant anywhere. Weapon magazines also refill by idle batches with no stock. Salvage crates set scenario state, not inventory. Carving removes geometry but yields no material. The streamed world keeps no mutation.
  • Decided M0 is plate repair anywhere. Ship pane Repair on a damaged, non-destroyed section of the player's own ship spends HullPlate from that ship's inventory: one plate restores up to 20 HP, partial stock repairs partially, and the change is instant. No dock, partner or station is read. Zero HP, full HP and no plates refuse with no change. The free full heal dies.
  • Direction Later milestones, in order: M1 dock Take/Give, M2 intake pickup and jettison, M3 ammo reserve, M4 station refit, M5 trade plus mining. Each needs its own code-backed gate.
  • Proposal All item changes go through one validate-then-commit step per transaction. A refusal mutates nothing. A success changes inventory and world state in the same system run. M0 decides this for repair; later transactions still need their gates.
  • Proposal A station repair service is a possible later idea only. It is not part of M0 and is not approved.
  • Open The M0 gate still decides the mutation API, the rule owner, the ownership check, the disabled-reason precedence and the refusal text (TASK.md:48, 113-121).

Proposed item loop

One inventory owner. Several sources put items in. Several sinks take items out. Each arrow is a transaction with its own gate.

Acquisition options

Deliberate physical pickup (replaces proximity collection for cargo)

Current A salvage crate "collects" when any collider enters its sensor (salvage.rs:1-6). Proposal Cargo enters an inventory only through a player-chosen act. Three candidate verbs:

Pickup verbs: player act, physical read, and main consequence
OptionPlayer actPhysical readConsequence
A. Cargo intakeOpen the intake, match speed, fly the body in.Strong: the cargo body touches the hull.Needs an intake section and an open state on the hull; a ship without one cannot collect.
B. TractorLock a body and pull it in.Medium: a visible force moves a real body.Adds a force system and a target rule, and still needs an intake to finish.
C. InteractPress interact within range.Weak: no motion is needed.Cheapest; it is proximity collection with one key press, close to what the user wants to remove.

Proposal Choose A. Add B later only if play shows A is too hard. The intake must also refuse a body above a relative speed limit (value open), so a collision is not a pickup.

Direction Owner chose A (D5) with a change to the player act: the intake opens by itself. A cargo intake component has a two-stage front sensor. A larger detection volume opens the intake with no player action; a smaller capture volume takes a canister that crosses it slowly. Other hull collisions never collect cargo. Sizes, the speed limit and hull placement are Open for the M2 gate (TASK.md:36, 49).

Dock transfer

Current The Inventory pane already reads both sides of a docked pair (app.rs:590-620). Proposal Add Take and Give for a selected stack while docked. Who may take from whom is a partner policy (D4): for example a derelict allows Take only, a station allows what its services allow, and a live AI ship allows nothing. Never infer permission from the fact of docking.

Direction Owner amended D4 for M1: Take only from a docked partner that is neutralized or explicitly labelled derelict or lootable. Never Take from a live healthy ship; getting goods from a live ship is trade for credits (M5). Give works to any valid docked ship, for example a quest delivery. The label name and quantity UI are Open (TASK.md:34, 51).

Salvage sources

Direction Salvage reaches the inventory by two paths: dock with a neutralized or derelict ship and Take its stock (M1), or take a loose canister through the intake (M2). An authored loot table seeds a ship's inventory before destruction; destroying it drops only the cargo it still carries, as canisters, with no bonus roll. Ammo can also come from salvage. Streamed derelicts regenerate on revisit (nova_world_base/src/lib.rs:18-21), so the owner chose D11 (a): loot only on authored scenario objects until task 20260925-190156 decides claim-once (TASK.md:35, 41, 44).

Spending and removal

Plate repair anywhere (M0)

Decided Repair works undocked and docked alike on the player's own ship. A section above 0 HP and below full HP costs min(available, ceil(missing / 20)) plates, and Health becomes min(max, current + 20 * spent). Partial stock repairs partially. A 0 HP section, a full section and an empty plate stock refuse with no change. The owner answered D1 (no partner), D2 (partial, ratio 20) and D3 (instant) on 2026-09-28 (TASK.md:20-29).

Open For the M0 gate: the inventory spend API, the rule owner, how a section resolves to its ship root, the disabled-reason text and its precedence against the reload reason, and whether a refused stale command writes a note line (TASK.md:48, 113-121).

Two owner comments were on text that this reconciliation replaced: the old heading of this subsection and the rule paragraph of Example 1. Both comments are kept in browser storage and show under Detached comments with their original text. Their content is recorded in TASK.md:21.

Reserve-backed ammunition (M3)

Current Magazines refill for free on idle batches, and the catalog test every_authored_magazine_refills states that ammunition is a rate limit, not a budget (sections/mod.rs:297-325). Proposal Keep the idle batch as the rate limit, and draw each batch from an Ammo stack in the ship inventory. With an empty reserve the magazine stops refilling; the ship still flies. Kinetic and Pierce mounts draw the same PDC ammo good and keep their authored damage type (TASK.md:16). This rewrites the rate-limit stance and the torpedo-versus-PDC saturation balance, so it needs its own balance gate. The fate of the free Reload button is part of D9.

Direction Owner chose D9 (b) for M3: idle batch refill draws from an inventory reserve and free Reload dies. Reserve ammo can come from station stock or from salvage of destroyed or neutralized ships. Rates and balance are Open for the M3 balance gate (TASK.md:41, 52).

Jettison (M2)

Proposal Jettison removes a chosen quantity from one stack and spawns one cargo canister body at the intake that carries exactly those items. The canister is a normal pickup target, so jettison then pickup conserves the total. Without capacity (D8), jettison serves deliberate drops: leaving supplies for an ally, a decoy, or an objective delivery.

Direction Owner chose D10 (a) for M2: a chosen quantity leaves as one canister that carries exactly those items. Count-based capacity (D8 option b) arrives with jettison at M2; there is no capacity through M1 (TASK.md:37, 40).

This report only proposes that a refit spends Repair components and produces a visible hull change with the same validate-then-commit rule. It does not choose whole-design swap versus one-section refit.

Recommended first playable flow

DECIDED M0, NOT YET CODE. The owner approved this flow on 2026-09-28. It does not exist in the game until the M0 gate lands.
  1. Current New Game starts the open world with 12 hull plates in the player inventory.
  2. Current Damage lowers section Health (health.rs:117-149).
  3. Decided The player selects a damaged, non-destroyed section in the Ship pane and presses Repair, anywhere. No dock and no partner are needed.
  4. Decided Repair spends min(stock, ceil(missing / 20)) plates and restores up to 20 HP per plate in the same system run; the Inventory pane shows the new count.
  5. Decided Full HP, 0 HP or no plates: the button is disabled with a reason, and a forced command changes nothing. No free heal remains.
  6. Decided Spent plates are session-only; the authored stock returns when the scenario loads again.
  7. Direction Next gate: dock Take from a neutralized or derelict ship and Give to any docked ship (M1), then canisters through the intake (M2).

Why this order: it proves the loop with one inventory write (spend) on stock that already exists, and with no new world object. It avoids docking, persistence, trade and physics pickup in the first gate.

Owner decisions

Each decision lists the options as first proposed, one consequence each, and the recommendation. The owner answered all thirteen in comments on 2026-09-28. The answer follows each block: Decided for M0 scope, Direction for later milestones. Remaining open details are in TASK.md:47-53.

D1. What makes a dock partner a repair service?

  1. A service capability component on an authored ship root (a station is a ship design with a docking port and an addressable id). Consequence: reuses docking unchanged; adds one component and one content field.
  2. A new station object kind. Consequence: clear semantics, but it duplicates docking and ship wiring.
  3. Any docked partner. Consequence: every derelict becomes a repair shop.

First recommendation: (a), authored only (not streamed) for M0. Superseded.

Decided None of (a)-(c). M0 needs no repair partner: the player repairs their own ship anywhere with plates. No partner, service or station feature exists yet. Proposal A station repair service may be proposed later as its own idea; it would never gate plate repair.

D2. Repair quantity rule

  1. Fixed plates per Repair action. Consequence: simple, but a scratch costs as much as a near-kill.
  2. Plates per HP restored, spend what you have (partial). Consequence: a Repair never refuses for shortage while one plate remains.
  3. Plates per HP restored, full-or-refuse. Consequence: a player short by one plate cannot repair at all.

Recommend (b). The ratio value is a separate balance decision.

Decided (b), partial repair. Ratio: one plate restores up to 20 HP; missing HP rounds up to whole plates; leftover capacity of the last plate is lost.

D3. Interruption policy

  1. Instant transaction in one system run. Consequence: cannot straddle an undock; no refund logic.
  2. Timed job, refund on undock. Consequence: needs job state and a refund proof.
  3. Timed job, progressive spend. Consequence: partial results on undock; hardest to prove.

Recommend (a) for M0 and M1.

Decided (a), instant in one system run, for M0 and later transactions; never a timed job.

D4. Dock transfer authorization

  1. Any docked partner, both directions. Consequence: live AI ships can be emptied by docking.
  2. Per-partner policy (derelict Take only; station by service; AI ship none). Consequence: needs a policy owner; since D1 adds no partner component, the M1 gate names one.
  3. No transfer; services only. Consequence: derelict salvage needs another path.

Recommend (b).

Direction (b), amended for M1: Take only from a neutralized partner or one labelled derelict or lootable; never from a live healthy ship. Give to any valid docked ship. Buying from a live ship is trade (M5).

D5. Pickup verb

  1. Cargo intake. Consequence: most physical; needs an intake section.
  2. Tractor. Consequence: more forgiving; adds a force system.
  3. Explicit interact. Consequence: cheapest; nearly proximity collection.

Recommend (a); revisit (b) after play.

Direction (a) for M2, never another verb. The intake opens by itself when a canister enters a larger detection volume; a smaller capture volume takes a canister that crosses it slowly.

D6. Mining tool

  1. Drill. Consequence: hard contact flying.
  2. Mining beam that spawns ore canisters. Consequence: one tool section plus one spawn observer; spew stays decorative.
  3. Severed fragment yield. Consequence: rare, warhead-driven, 30 s lifetime.

Recommend (b).

Direction (b) for M5: a short-range beam, not usable as a melee weapon, that only yields ore canisters.

D7. Which carving yields ore?

  1. Mining tool only. Consequence: combat near rocks yields nothing.
  2. Any weapon carve. Consequence: every fight near rocks spawns loot and bodies.

Recommend (a).

Direction (a): combat carving yields nothing.

D8. Capacity

  1. None. Consequence: jettison has no pressure reason.
  2. By stack or unit count. Consequence: simple, ignores item size.
  3. By mass or volume per hull. Consequence: needs authored mass per good and per hull.

Recommend (a) through M1; decide (b) or (c) with M2.

Direction (a) through M1; (b), count-based capacity, at M2 when jettison exists. The count is open.

D9. Ammo policy and free Reload

  1. Keep free idle refill; no Ammo goods. Consequence: Ammo category stays empty.
  2. Idle refill draws from a reserve; free Reload dies. Consequence: ammo becomes a budget; balance tests change.
  3. Dock-only restock of magazines. Consequence: a ship out of ammo fights with nothing until it docks.

Recommend (b) at M3 with a balance gate.

Direction (b) at M3; free Reload dies then. Ammo also comes from salvage. Rates are open for the balance gate.

D10. Jettison form

  1. Spawn a cargo canister with the items. Consequence: conserves items; adds world bodies to persist.
  2. Destroy the items. Consequence: simple; loses the ally-drop play.

Recommend (a).

Direction (a) for M2.

D11. Salvage persistence before task 190156

  1. Loot only on authored scenario objects until 190156 lands. Consequence: no streamed loot yet.
  2. Allow streamed loot, accept regeneration. Consequence: sectors are farmable by retire and revisit.

Recommend (a). Owned jointly with 190156.

Direction (a) until task 20260925-190156 lands.

D12. Item identity for new goods

  1. Extend closed ItemType. Consequence: mods cannot add goods.
  2. Open registry with ItemKindId and authored catalog. Consequence: lint and load must reject unknown ids; more content surface.

Recommend (a) until trade; revisit with M5.

Direction (a) until trade; revisit at M5, possibly moddable.

D13. Salvage crate object

  1. Keep SalvageCrate as a scenario trigger; add a separate cargo canister. Consequence: two similar-looking objects with different rules.
  2. Replace it with the cargo canister; scenario counting moves to a pickup event. Consequence: breaking for mods and examples/playable/first_shift_map.rs; changelog marks it (breaking).

Recommend (b) at M2.

Direction (b) at M2, as a follow-up once the loop works.
