**Tomas Vega** is the pilot and navigator aboard
[Kaveri](../../ships/kaveri/), [Jonah Mercer's](../jonah-mercer/) Clearwell
workship. Precision, rather than
recklessness, defines the job. Tomas wants the crew to arrive where it intends
to go, with a workable plan for what happens next.

## The cost of a promise

Tomas questions risks that other people volunteer the crew to take. A generous
intention does not settle who will bear the consequences, or whether the ship
can finish the work already promised.

That caution can sit uneasily beside
[Rina Okafor's](../rina-okafor/) determination to help station residents. The
disagreement is about obligations, not a simple division between courage and
cowardice.

## A future elsewhere

Like Jonah, Tomas expects the posting to end with a return to Earth. A working
life at Saturn does not have to become a permanent one to deserve respect.

Tomas shares that intention with the captain without becoming an automatic
supporter of every command. The people aboard remain entitled to question how
their common future is being spent.
