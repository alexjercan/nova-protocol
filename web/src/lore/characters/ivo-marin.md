**Ivo Marin** is the work operations technician aboard
[Gantry](../../ships/gantry/), an
[EarthWorks Industrial](../../organizations/earthworks-industrial/) workship.
Practical responsibility extends to the people aboard, not only the equipment
and the assigned job.

Ivo works with captain and pilot [Nadia Sen](../nadia-sen/) and engineer
[Owen Park](../owen-park/). All three are cross-trained civilians. Their small
crew depends on its members helping each other, not merely completing separate
assignments.
