**Owen Park** is the engineer aboard [Gantry](../../ships/gantry/), an
[EarthWorks Industrial](../../organizations/earthworks-industrial/) workship.
Owen's contribution includes the engineering judgment that helps the crew
understand the ship's condition and what it can safely support.

Captain and pilot [Nadia Sen](../nadia-sen/), Owen, and work operations
technician [Ivo Marin](../ivo-marin/) form a three-person, cross-trained crew.
Their different responsibilities contribute to the operation of one civilian
working vessel.
