**Nadia Sen** captains and pilots [Gantry](../../ships/gantry/), an unarmed
[EarthWorks Industrial](../../organizations/earthworks-industrial/) workship
associated with [Keystone](../../places/keystone/).

Professional and composed, Nadia expects the company to meet its obligations
to the people doing its work. Confidence in those obligations is not the same
as unquestioning confidence in every manager.

Nadia works with engineer [Owen Park](../owen-park/) and work operations
technician [Ivo Marin](../ivo-marin/). All three are cross-trained. Command and
piloting are Nadia's principal responsibilities, not a reason for the others
to have no judgment or initiative.
