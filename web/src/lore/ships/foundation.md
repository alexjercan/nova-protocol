**Foundation** is a large construction and repair support ship operated by
[EarthWorks Industrial](../../organizations/earthworks-industrial/). Its regular
regional port is [Keystone](../../places/keystone/), the settlement hub founded
through the company's construction and supply work.

Foundation's role is industrial support, not long-haul freight or naval command.
Its size does not make it a capital warship. The ship belongs to the civilian
working fleet, while vessels such as [Bastion](../bastion/) have a separate
security role.

The name follows EarthWorks' architectural tradition. Its individual service
history, including which installations it helped construct, has not yet been
established.
