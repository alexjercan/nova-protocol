**Bastion** is an [EarthWorks Industrial](../../organizations/earthworks-industrial/)
security vessel associated with [Keystone](../../places/keystone/). Its work is
protection and escort, distinct from the ordinary unarmed ships carrying out the
company's industrial operations.

A bastion is a defensive structure. The name belongs to the same architectural
family as Keystone and [Foundation](../foundation/), with an emphasis on
protecting what has been built.

Company security is not a sovereign navy. Bastion's role does not grant it state
military authority or establish a capital-warship classification. Its exact
weapons, size, command crew, and permissions remain undefined.
