**Ebro** is a water hauler operated by
[Clearwell Waterworks](../../organizations/clearwell-waterworks/) in support of
[Baikal](../../places/baikal/). Transport is part of the work that makes the
station's processed water useful beyond the plant itself.

Ebro's regular cargo work differs from the maintenance and recovery work of
[Kaveri](../kaveri/). Both are unarmed civilian ships serving the same small
operator. Their river names identify Clearwell's fleet without giving the ships
interchangeable jobs or crews.
