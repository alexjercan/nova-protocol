**Kaveri** is a workship operated by
[Clearwell Waterworks](../../organizations/clearwell-waterworks/) from
[Baikal](../../places/baikal/). Its work includes maintenance, recovery, and
small cargo jobs. Like ordinary civilian and industrial ships, it is unarmed.

## Work and crew

The ship is a working tool and a shared workplace, not the captain's personal
property. [Jonah Mercer](../../characters/jonah-mercer/) is an established
employee who knows the work and is responsible for the people aboard.

- [Leila Haddad](../../characters/leila-haddad/): chief engineer.
- [Tomas Vega](../../characters/tomas-vega/): pilot and navigator.
- [Rina Okafor](../../characters/rina-okafor/): work operations lead.
- [Samir Bell](../../characters/samir-bell/): systems technician and first-aider.

All five are cross-trained. Their responsibilities meet in the operation of one
ship, from planning an approach to making its arrival useful to the people
waiting at the destination.

## Name

Kaveri takes its name from an Earth river, following Clearwell's ship-naming
tradition. [Ebro](../ebro/), the company's water hauler, belongs to the same
family of names.
