**Gantry** is an [EarthWorks Industrial](../../organizations/earthworks-industrial/)
workship associated with [Keystone](../../places/keystone/) and the surrounding
supply operation. It belongs to the ordinary, unarmed working fleet, not the
company's security force.

Its name comes from the built world, following EarthWorks' architectural and
engineering tradition. Gantry represents the everyday work behind the company's
large construction projects, alongside vessels such as
[Foundation](../foundation/).

## Crew

[Nadia Sen](../../characters/nadia-sen/) is Gantry's captain and pilot.
[Owen Park](../../characters/owen-park/) is its engineer, and
[Ivo Marin](../../characters/ivo-marin/) handles work operations.

All three are cross-trained. Their small crew combines command, flight,
engineering, and practical operations rather than assigning a separate
specialist to every shipboard function.
