# v0.18.0 - The world answers back

v0.17.0 was the release where [the world comes to you](/news/0.17.0/).
Traders flew to stations, traded and posted jobs. But the world said little
about what you did in it. A docking clamp closed with no sound. A railgun slug
landed with the same click as any other penetrating hit. A rescue cost
nothing, no station cared what you carried, and a scenario could hear a job
accepted but not a job finished.

v0.18.0 makes the world answer. A clamp latches with a sound, a light and
`DOCKED <NAME>`. Stations refine ore into alloy plate, and the plate builds a
second tier of sections. A rescue spends a medkit. Lawful stations do not
trade contraband. Station Control speaks about your first job. The key row
shows only the keys that apply, and a gamepad can dock. And scenarios can read
what an event carries, follow a job to its end, and pay, give, take, post jobs
and set stances.

## Cargo that matters

### Refine ore into alloy plate

Some stations now offer **Refine**. Docked at one, click an ore a recipe uses
in the Inventory pane, then **Refine** beside **Sell**. The form counts
batches and states each one whole:
`2 Iron ore + 1 Carbon ore + 30 cr -> 1 Alloy plate`. Confirm takes the ore
from your hold, adds the plate and pays the fee to the station. A refine that
cannot run changes nothing and says why.

### A second tier of sections

An alloy plate is a part, and five new sections spend one. Each is the second
tier of a section that had one variant. Its recipe is the first tier's parts
plus one alloy plate, at one and a half times the credits.

| Family | First tier | Second tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cargo intake | door opens at 40 m, takes at 1 m, 90 health | **Wide Intake**: door opens at 60 m, takes at 1.5 m, 120 health |
| Mining beam | 100 m reach, a pulse each 1 s, 90 health | **Long Mining Beam**: 160 m reach, a pulse each 0.8 s, 110 health |
| Controller | 100 health, 9,760 torque | **Armoured Controller**: 160 health, 11,000 torque |
| Docking port | captures at 10 m and 15 degrees, 90 health | **Wide-Gate Docking Port**: captures at 15 m and 25 degrees, 110 health |
| Railgun | 300 Pierce damage, 1,800 power | **Heavy Lance**: 450 Pierce damage, 2,700 power |

A station builds only the sections its civilization knows. Four of them open
at advancement 0.3 and the Heavy Lance at 0.6, so the second tier is also out
in the advanced civilizations. The chart scope above shows how far out. A
station that builds one swaps it in for the first tier from the Ship pane's
**Catalogue**.

### The Dart

`dart_torpedo_section` loads the **Dart**, a third torpedo type. It flies the
Lance's straight run at a cruise of 450 m/s against the Lance's 350. Over a
3 km run-in it arrives in 7.9 s where a Lance takes 9.3 s. Point defense
solves it as it solves a Lance. The bay opens at advancement 0.25, and its
recipe is the torpedo bay's plus one alloy plate.

### Contraband and lawful stations

Half the civilizations of a generated world are now **lawful**. The market of
a living lawful station does not deal in Contraband: it buys none and sells
none. Open a Sell of the item there and the form reads
`Refused: <station> does not deal in contraband`, with Confirm disabled. The
station still lets you dock with the item in your hold, and its storage keeps
it. Sell the item at a station that is not lawful.

The base game has one such item, **Unmarked cores**: 10 kg, with a 300 cr ask
and a 240 cr bid. Scavengers deal them. Half the intact scavenger ships carry
one unit.

## The ship tells you

### A clamp you can hear

Every docking port of a station now wears a beacon: a cold light that
breathes on a 3 s pulse. It burns amber while a ship holds the port and is
dark on a dead station. The beacons follow the Flash lights setting: Off,
which the Low preset uses, lights none.

When the clamp closes, your port plays the latch, a cold light blinks on the
collar, and the HUD posts `DOCKED <NAME>`. Letting go plays the release and
posts `UNDOCKED`. A dock that a Load rebuilds is not a new clamp, and a dock
that damage ends has no release, so both stay quiet. The loop at the top of
this post shows the sequence.

### A railgun hit you can see

A railgun slug landing is now its own event. The entry face flashes the
slug's steel blue, throws a dense burst of spall and plays the lance's own
impact sound. It happens once per shot, at the first body the slug meets,
however many cells the corridor crosses.

## Keys that apply

### The key row shows what you can do

In v0.17.0 the flight key row kept one fixed slot per verb and drew a verb
dark when it did not apply. Now the row shows only the chips that apply. Five
core chips always sit left of the centre line: INTERFACE, DRIVE, STOP, RCS
and RADAR. The rest join right of it while there is something for them to do:

- **GOTO** while you hold a travel lock, **ORBIT** near a gravity well and
  **CANCEL** while the autopilot is engaged.
- **DOCK** while your lock carries a docking port or you are docked, and
  **HELM** while you are docked.
- **MINE** on a ship with a bound mining beam, and **AIM** on a ship with a
  turret, a torpedo bay or a railgun.
- **FIRE**, **TORPEDO** and **RAILGUN** while the weapons are hot.

A shown chip you cannot press right now still draws dark. That includes RCS
on an empty magazine, which v0.17.0 drew lit.

### Dock and take the helm from a gamepad

DOCK and HELM had no default gamepad button. They are now chords on the RCS
button,
like MATCH: hold **L3** and press **Y** to dock or let go, hold **L3** and
press **A** to take or hand back the helm. The key row draws both caps on the
DOCK and HELM chips.

New hulls put the weapons on free buttons. A torpedo bay takes **LB**, a
railgun takes **View**, and a ship's first mining beam takes **RT** as well
as `V`. LB is also free look and View is also the HUD toggle, so while the
weapons are hot those two do nothing on a pad; `Alt` and the grave key still
work. RT is also FIRE: with the weapons cold it only mines. See
[Keybinds](/wiki/keybinds/).

## Scenarios hear more and do more

### Station Control speaks

Station Control now speaks in the open world, three times, each once per
world. When the work of your first job is done, it sends you back to the
station that posted it. On your first hand-in it pays a 50 cr bonus over the
reward. And when the world takes a job you hold, it calls the contract void.

### Jobs, stances and sectors as events

`OnJobAccepted` came with v0.17.0. Three more events now follow a held job to
its end: `OnJobDone` when its work in the world is finished, `OnJobHandedIn`
when the reward is paid, and `OnJobDropped` with a `cause` of `Lost` or
`Abandoned`. All four carry `id`, `kind`, `station`, `reward_cr` and
`post_key`. A procurement has no work in the world, so it raises no
`OnJobDone`.

`OnStanceChanged` fires once per step of a civilization's stance toward the
player, with `civilization`, `from` and `to`. `OnSectorEntered` fires when
the player crosses into another sector, with `sector`, `x`, `y`, `z` and
`from`. See [Job events](/create/events/#job-events).

### Read what an event carries

An expression can now read a field of the event its handler runs for. In RON
it is `Event("field")`; typed in the editor it is `event.field`, as in
`event.price_cr > 100`. It works in a handler's filters and in its own
`VariableSet` and `TimerStart`. Lint refuses a field the event does not
carry and names the fields it has.

Three new queries read the world: `entity("id").credits`,
`entity("id").items("Item")` and `scenario.sector`. See
[Event fields](/create/expressions/#event-fields).

### Five actions that change the world

- **`GrantCredits`** grants credits to a ship or a station, or charges it
  with a negative amount.
- **`GiveItems`** and **`TakeItems`** put items into a hold or take them out,
  all or nothing.
- **`PostJob`** posts a procurement on a generated station's board under a
  `key`. The job has no time limit, a world save keeps it, and the job events
  carry the key as `post_key`.
- **`SetStance`** puts a civilization on `Ally`, `Neutral` or `Enemy`.

No author can write the id of a generated station or civilization, so
`PostJob` and `SetStance` take a binding, `$id` or `$other_id`, that the
event fills in. Under `OnDocked`, `$id` is the station the player docked
with:

```ron
(
    name: OnDocked,
    once: true,
    actions: [PostJob((station: "$id", key: "ore_run", item: "IronOre", count: 4, reward_cr: 250))],
),
```

An action that cannot run changes nothing and raises the new
`OnActionRefused` event with a `reason`, such as `insufficient_credits`,
`hold_full`, `short_items` or `duplicate_job`. See
[Ship state](/create/actions/#ship-state).

### The new blocks in the editor

The Events canvas of the mod editor has blocks for the new events and
actions. The Query block is now picked, not typed: one chip lists every read,
a read of a ship adds a chip that lists the scenario's objects, and `Items`
adds a chip that lists the items the mod sees.

## More in v0.18.0

- **The editor binds new weapons to free pad buttons.** A new torpedo bay
  takes LB and a railgun takes View, in place of LT (raise weapons) and R3
  (interface). A ship's first mining beam takes RT as well as `V`.
- **Ports and lances carry their own sounds.** `DockingSectionConfig` gains
  optional `clamp_sound` and `release_sound`, and `RailgunSectionConfig`
  gains optional `impact_sound`. The base port and the base lance author all
  three.
- **Refining and scenario grants name their cause.** `OnCreditsChanged`
  gains the causes `Refine` and `Scenario`.

## For modders

Run `content lint` on every bundle before you publish for v0.18.0. Three
changes break content that v0.17.0 accepted.

- **(breaking) `Medkit` is a base role item.** It is `Repair`, 2 kg, with a
  60 cr ask and a 45 cr bid, and a distress rescue spends one. A mod item
  with the id `Medkit` replaces it and must keep the category `Repair`.
- **(breaking) A job event needs a generated world.** A scenario with no
  generated `world` that names `OnJobAccepted`, `OnJobDone`, `OnJobHandedIn`
  or `OnJobDropped` is a lint error and does not load: no station there posts
  a job board.
- **(breaking) A reserved pad or key source fails lint.** A player
  `input_mapping` source that the flight or camera rig holds is now an error,
  not a warning. A thruster on Space or RB and a torpedo bay on LB are shared
  defaults and pass. A torpedo bay on LT does not: move it to LB.
- **Generated worlds only.** `SetStance`, `PostJob`, `OnStanceChanged`,
  `OnSectorEntered` and `scenario.sector` are lint errors on a scenario with
  no generated `world`.
- **`GiveItems` and `TakeItems` raise no hold event.** `OnPickedUp` reports a
  canister and `OnTraded` a trade, and these are neither. Read the result
  with `entity("id").items("Item")`.
- **Refine is content.** `Item` gains optional
  `refine: Some((inputs, fee_cr))`, and station `services` take `Refine`.
  Lint refuses a recipe whose inputs and fee cost less than its output's bid.
  The authored waystation refines, for a scenario that spawns it; a New Game
  world does not. See [Refine](/create/stations/#refine).
- **Contraband is a category.** Item `category` takes `Contraband`: the
  market of a lawful generated station does not buy or sell it. Base adds
  `UnmarkedCores` and a `Scavenger` `Contraband` price rule; a pack needs no
  new rule of its own. An authored station is never lawful.
