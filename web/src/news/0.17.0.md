# v0.17.0 - The world comes to you

v0.16.0 was the release where [the world keeps score](/news/0.16.0/).
Civilizations built stations, stations posted jobs, and every hand-in or kill
moved what a civilization thought of you. But the stations sat and waited. The
ships around them flew patrols and escorts; nobody came to a station to do
business, and nothing on a board was there because somebody else had been.

v0.17.0 puts traffic in the world. Generated traders now fly to a station,
dock, trade real cargo for real credits, post jobs and leave. GOTO flies
around the rocks in its way, Q and E roll the ship, and Shift + X matches a
moving target. A fresh world tells you what to do first. And the Sandbox is
now a mod editor: a hub of your mods, the whole mod as tiles, an Events
canvas of blocks, forms for every content kind, a colour picker for themes,
and a Package window that writes the zip.

## A living world

### Traders visit stations

Every living station now has two generated ships of its own civilization 2 to
4 km from it, so most new worlds have a trader in visit range of the start
station. A trader with a trade profile picks a station within 20 km, flies
there and docks. Docked, it trades: goods and credits move between its hold
and the station by the lines of one receipt, so nothing is made or lost. It
posts its jobs, waits 60 s, undocks and flies 1 km clear.

A station takes one visitor at a time. A visitor also docks at a station that
orbits a planet or spins, and waits up to five minutes for a clear approach
before it gives up. What a trader buys and sells comes from a new
`TradeProfile` content kind: weighted buy and sell lists with a quantity and
a per-unit price limit.

### Jobs from the traffic

A visitor posts up to three jobs beside the station's own three slots. They
are Procure jobs: bring the station what the trader wanted. An open one goes
at the next slot expiry or after 20 minutes; one you accepted stays until you
finish or lose it. The world save carries the visits and their jobs, so a
docked visitor stays docked across a Load and never trades twice.

### Roll and match

**Q and E roll your ship** about its nose. The controller section turns the
view and the hull together, and a roll costs no RCS. **Shift + X is now
MATCH**: it matches your travel lock's velocity on RCS alone, keeps the
hull's attitude and never docks. With no target, no RCS or an empty magazine
it says why. Hold the RCS key and the dock row's STOP chip turns into MATCH,
and goes dark when MATCH would refuse.

### Torpedoes you can read

A launch throws a cold light from the bay, and the drive lights with its own
ignition sound and a short flare. A lit torpedo leaves a faint cold trail:
1.5 s of path on High, 0.75 s on Medium, none on Low. A warhead blast opens
with a faint shell that expands to its blast radius, and a hull your blast
defeats confirms with a cockpit stinger and a camera jolt. A torpedo that dies
unfired now says how: a grey puff and a clank on contact, a pop when shot
down.

closes. Each batch a reload lands in a bay plays a rack clunk.

### Wrecks, canisters and stopped ships

Cargo canisters now hold up to 1,000 kg. A wreck packs its drop into mixed
canisters, and an intake takes the whole items that fit your hold and leaves
the rest in the canister. A neutralized ship now drops its patrol, escort slot
or order and brakes to a stop on its own RCS; with no flight computer, no
RCS, an empty magazine, a dock or a strong well, it drifts on.

Events is a block canvas beside a palette grouped by role, with a rail that

save.

### One bar, one Add menu, a real camera

The editor has one top bar, a Hierarchy tree, an Inspector with folding
groups, and a strip of Assets, Problems, Ship and Log. Undo and redo cover

before you leave. The **Add menu** groups what the document takes in

### Settings, sections and the package

**Mod Settings** edits the manifest and its dependencies. It refuses to
remove a dependency that content still needs, refuses a cycle, and picking
from an undeclared mod adds the dependency in the same undo step.
**New Asset > Section** builds a section in the mod: sockets on the gizmo, a
`.glb` import and live Problems. Ships can carry it, and Play flies it.

**File > Package** opens a window: version bump, lint check, the file list
and the name. It writes `<id>-<version>.zip` and shows its path and size. Hub
Import refuses unsafe paths. Packaging is desktop only.

## Engine

The game now runs on **Bevy 0.20**, **avian3d 0.8** and **bevy_hanabi
0.20**. The built-in shaders moved from WGSL to WESL
(`assets/shaders/*.wesl`). Mods cannot ship shaders, so nothing changes for
them.

## More in v0.17.0

- **The HUD holds still at any interface scale.** The lock bracket, the
  target and objective markers and the flight chips sit where they belong at
  a scale other than 100%, and the chips beside your ship no longer overlap
  in a burn.
- **DOCK says why.** A DOCK press that does nothing names the reason in the
  HUD notice: no lock, no free port, too far, not facing or too fast.
- **Key hints follow your device.** The dock row, the TAB card, objectives
  and the outcome screen show pad buttons after a pad press and keys after a
  key press.
- **Esc backs out one level.** In the TAB card, the main menu and the pause
  menu. Buy station, Reset Defaults and mod Uninstall ask twice.
- **The Jobs pane scrolls and wraps.** Long titles end in an ellipsis, and an
  empty list or an undocked board says what to do next.
- **The PDC round has a lot price.** 10 cr ask and 7 cr bid per 100 rounds,
  and a station that stocks rounds opens with at least 2,000.
- **Buttons sound like buttons.** Panes, catalogue and editor buttons tick on
  hover and click on a press; most refused presses buzz.

## For modders

Run `content lint` on every bundle before you publish for v0.17.0.

- **`UiTheme` palettes are one fixed set** of job-named colours in every
  theme. A root theme sets all of them; an unknown colour name fails to
  parse. See [UI themes](/create/ui-themes/).
- **`Item` requires `price_lot`**, the item count one `ask_cr` or `bid_cr`
  prices: 100 for a PDC round, 1 for the other base items. An item without it
  fails lint, then load.
- **A section with `health` 0 or less fails lint**, and so does a mining
  section or cargo intake whose sound or mesh ref names no file.
- **`ObjectiveComplete` must name a posted objective.** An id that no
  `Objective` in the same scenario posts fails lint and load; it was a
  warning.
- **New content and actions.** A `TradeProfile` kind; the `OnJobAccepted`
  event; the `ObjectiveChipHold` and `SetTradeProfile` actions; optional
  torpedo and kill sounds. `ObjectiveChipHold` is not saved: run it from
  `OnResume` too.
- **Editor ship saves carry the mod's namespace.** An older `editor_` save
  opens and converts on its next save.
