# Episode-one staging constraints

The [production outline](../../../lore/seasons/season-1.md) owns shared events.
The TypeScript page files own the script. All eighteen pages are illustrated.
The complete episode is approved and marked `released` for website builds.
Live publication requires a separate comic tag and deployment.

Each panel's `action` is the reader-facing transcript and describes only what
the panel shows. Every illustration directive, negative instruction, and
production constraint belongs in this file instead.

- The opening is in 2078 on Earth's calendar. No day, month, or local era is
  needed for the first card; Kaveri's departure card says "Later that day."
- Keep the four-page opening, with Samir's supply check and Leila's hands-on
  inspection. All five Kaveri crew travel together; Leila checks the replacement
  in person at Aquila.
- Nothing before Gantry's distress call previews the attack, the pirates, or
  the coming rescue. Kaveri's departure from Baikal shows no pursuit, and no
  threatening shape watches Gantry leave Aquila. No pirate appears later at the
  separation or at the closing window either.
- Keep the Aquila meeting and Gantry-first departure so the hull is familiar
  before and after damage. Use the approved pressurized, non-rotating transfer
  hall with freefall handholds, windows onto both exterior berths, and a freight
  lock feeding Kaveri's external cradle. This local staging does not establish a
  full station layout. No exact cargo beyond ordinary supplies or Gantry
  destination is selected.
  Aquila is a pickup port, not a changed home port.
- Page 5 gives Aquila useful scale and activity without a roster of
  identifiable background ships; Kaveri stays the camera's anchor. Jonah keeps
  the shared access clear just outside the closer view of 5b.
- Only one freight-lock door is open at a time, and no open doorway connects
  the pressurized hall to space. The sealed lock and exterior handling gear
  move the assembly to Kaveri's external cradle between 6c and 7a, and the crew
  never stand outside without suits in 7a. Page 6 stays a working exchange
  rather than a meeting-room conversation; in 6c Nadia's frontal likeness is
  unchanged and her reaching arm carries the movement.
- Keep timing qualitative: Kaveri reaches Gantry before its reserves run out,
  scheduled recovery arrives too late, and the diversion delays Baikal's repair.
  Nearby communications can be quick; ship travel is not instant. No orbital
  spreadsheet, exact coordinates, or countdown is required for this episode.
  Page 9 shows an arrival ordering, not a scaled time axis or orbit. The assembly
  remains outside the pressure window; the assisted passage stays inside.
- Pages 8-10 remain aboard Kaveri. Nadia and Daniel are heard over comms; no
  attack, Gantry-interior cutaway, or new Daniel likeness is introduced. Elena's
  display uses her existing frontal portrait. The last instruction is framed
  from Tomas's station, without an approval indicator.
- Everything the crew learn arrives over the channel from Nadia, not from an
  omniscient map labelling every danger. Page 9 does not isolate Tomas as the
  scene's unkind person. Page 10b keeps the panel on the labelled exchange
  rather than crowding the other preparations behind Samir.
- Use the accepted Gantry intact/stranded model and crew likenesses. Check its
  surviving port against Kaveri's transfer arrangement and assisted passage.
- Pages 11-12 show the coast, the existing stranded hull, an exterior port
  camera, and receiving preparations aboard Kaveri. The first-aid case has no
  specified contents or medical capability. Owen and Ivo remain on comms.
- Page 11 draws the coast without looping jet-fighter trails or a continuous
  burn used to fill the panel. Page 12 crops the real external hull instead of
  revealing interior systems through an impossible scan, and its receiving
  staging establishes no equipment specification or permanent passenger layout.
  Samir's readiness is help aboard, not a diagnosis or a promised treatment.
- Page 13 uses a short rigid sealed connector between the existing side collars,
  with opposite ship headings. One rigid arrangement supplies the separated
  view and connected close-up. Neither hull is resized; the spare stays secured.
  Both ships coast, and the existing hatches remain closed for seal and pressure
  checks. Drawing-clearance checks are not an engineering compatibility claim.
  Page 13b keeps Leila and Tomas on their own parts of one operation; neither
  explains basic flight controls to the competent captain. The connected
  close-up is quiet rather than triumphant.
- Page 14 uses the chosen padded stretcher with body straps. It passes feet
  first through the lined pressure passage. Rina controls the receiving end;
  Ivo controls the head end. Both keep their grips while Samir closes two
  temporary rigid frame clamps to an existing receiving rail. Do not replace that
  handover with carrying Owen under the arms or letting him drift unattended.
  The local interior view is not a full cabin plan or a dimensioned fit claim.
  Give the secured body, the guides' hands, and the clear route enough space to
  show how they start and stop together. Weightlessness removes neither his
  injury nor his inertia.
- Page 15 puts Nadia's final crossing before Jonah's acknowledgement. Her
  restrained record case has no plot-revealing label. Rina checks the clear
  passage while Leila closes it; the exterior then withdraws along the same
  connection axis. The connector stays with Kaveri, the load stays secured,
  and Gantry remains stranded without a new failure or an explosion. Owen stays
  with Samir; 15b does not crowd the cast onto the flight deck for a group
  portrait.
- Pages 16-17 keep Owen on the same padded support. The closed receiving clamps
  hold it during the return; Samir controls it at arrival. His exact injury and
  continuing care remain unspecified. Do not make Samir a doctor or establish
  Baikal as a major medical centre. The arrival is an interior welcome after
  transfer, not a new docking arrangement or a complete cast portrait. Nadia
  asks for the record rather than joining an accusation she cannot yet support,
  and Jonah's acknowledgement stays small rather than becoming a speech about
  what kind of person he is. The welcome is practical and relieved, not a civic
  ceremony.
- The replacement arrives in processing with every cover still on. Use the same
  assembly and the page-two pump and pipe anchors. A labelled time cut precedes
  the completed checks and return to service. Ebro's load remains pending; no
  new internal repair mechanism or instant production is shown.
- Page 18 returns to the page-three window framing and waiting Ebro. Mara stays
  off-panel and receives no new likeness. The cost is painful but recoverable.
  End with the same borrowed mug set within Elena's reach, not a threat, triumph,
  or suggestion that the successful rescue was a mistake.
- The wider EarthWorks pressure, Gantry hull recovery, survivors' later careers,
  and season ending remain open. Nothing in this rescue record proves the later
  conspiracy. Daniel and Mara are not assigned to it.
