            1. Base price table

              A PriceTable holds the factors a generated trader prices every item by: the scarcity curve, the demand of each cluster type and civilization role, and the clamp on the product. There is one table, not a registry. The base game states it, and a mod that states one replaces the whole table.

              This page is the field-by-field price table reference. For general RON spelling rules, see RON spelling rules.

              How a trader prices

              A living generated station and an intact generated ship price each item at the moment you trade, from its market:

              ask = reference ask_cr x scarcity.ask x cluster.ask x civilization.ask
bid = reference bid_cr x scarcity.bid x cluster.bid x civilization.bid

              Each side's product is clamped to clamp, then the price rounds to the nearest credit.

              factor from
              reference the item's ask_cr and bid_cr, see Content files
              scarcity the trader's stock of the item against its target, the stock it spawned with. At no stock the factor is starved, at the target 1, at glut_share times the target and above glut, and linear between. An item the trader spawned without uses unkept
              cluster the clusters rule for the cluster type the trader stands in and the item's category, or 1 when no rule names the pair
              civilization the roles rules for the item's category, each weighed by the civilization's preference for that role, a missing rule counting as 1

              An authored station or ship has no market. It deals at the reference price. A Buy at a station the player owns pays the bid.

              With the base table, a trader at its target stock in a rock-only cluster of a civilian civilization sells a hull plate at its 40 cr reference and buys one at 30 cr. At no stock it asks 1.5 and pays 1.25 times reference; at three times its target or more, 0.9 and 0.6. At its target stock, iron ore pays 14 cr at an industrial trader among planets and 6 cr at one in an asteroid-rich cluster. With one role at full weight the base table never reaches the 0.25 to 3.0 clamp.

              How a trader restocks

              A generated trader's stock stands still while its cell stands frozen. On the thaw it drifts toward its target stock by the play time it stood frozen: a starved trader buys back up, a glutted one sells back down, each by target * elapsed / recovery_seconds whole units, never past the target. An item the trader's target does not name is untouched - what you sold it stays sold. A reload alone restocks nothing: the clock stops while the game is paused, so no play time passes.

              Neutralizing a civilization's generated hull or station is an offence against it, not a price rule: see Civilization stances.

              How a job offer expires

              A living station's job board has three slots, each on its own posting. An offer the player does not hold expires after jobs.offer_seconds of play, or jobs.escort_offer_seconds for an escort, measured from its posting. The slot then posts a new offer under a new id. A held job is pinned to its slot and never expires, however long you hold it; handing one in, or losing it (its hauler lost), renews the slot at once. The clock is play time, so the pause menu, the interface and a Load all freeze every offer's countdown along with everything else. See Jobs for the full posting and renewal rule.

              The PriceTable item

              [
    PriceTable((
        clamp: (min: 0.25, max: 3.0),
        scarcity: (
            starved: (ask: 1.5, bid: 1.25),
            glut: (ask: 0.9, bid: 0.6),
            glut_share: 3.0,
            unkept: (ask: 1.0, bid: 0.9),
        ),
        restock: (recovery_seconds: 1800.0),
        jobs: (offer_seconds: 1200.0, escort_offer_seconds: 3600.0),
        clusters: [
            (cluster: AsteroidRich, category: Raw, factor: (ask: 0.6, bid: 0.6)),
        ],
        roles: [
            (role: Civilian, category: Food, factor: (ask: 1.25, bid: 1.25)),
        ],
    )),
]

              Every field is required.

              field type meaning
              clamp (min, max) the lowest and highest total factor on each side
              scarcity scarcity rule the stock curve, see below
              restock (recovery_seconds) the play seconds a thawed trader's stock takes to return to its target from none, see How a trader restocks
              jobs (offer_seconds, escort_offer_seconds) play seconds an unheld non-escort or escort offer stands before its slot posts a new one, see How a job offer expires
              clusters list of cluster rules demand by cluster type: cluster is AsteroidRich, RockOnly, PlanetHeavy or DerelictField; category is an item category; factor is (ask, bid)
              roles list of role rules demand by civilization role: role is Civilian, Industrial, Scavenger or Armored; category and factor as above

              A scarcity rule has four fields:

              field type meaning
              starved (ask, bid) the factor at no stock. Each side at least 1
              glut (ask, bid) the factor at glut_share times the target and above. Each side at most 1
              glut_share number the stock, as a multiple of the target, where glut applies in full. Above 1
              unkept (ask, bid) the factor for an item the trader spawned without

              Overlay and lint

              The last bundle in load order that states a table wins, whole. The base bundle must state one.

              content lint and the content check at load both report an error for a pack that:

              • states two tables;
              • is the base game and states none;
              • states a clamp whose min is 0 or less, or that does not hold 1;
              • states a factor that is not a finite number, or that lies outside the clamp;
              • states a factor whose bid is above its ask, which could push a bid above its ask;
              • states a starved side below 1, a glut side above 1, or a glut_share of 1 or less;
              • states a restock.recovery_seconds, jobs.offer_seconds or jobs.escort_offer_seconds that is not a finite number above 0;
              • names one cluster type or one role with one category twice.

              With every factor's bid at most its ask, a trader's bid is never above its ask.

              Base price table

              rule factor
              clamp 0.25 to 3.0
              starved ask 1.5, bid 1.25
              glut, at 3 times the target ask 0.9, bid 0.6
              unkept ask 1.0, bid 0.9
              restock recovery 1800 s (30 min of play)
              non-escort offer window 1200 s (20 min of play)
              escort offer window 3600 s (60 min of play)
              AsteroidRich Raw 0.6
              RockOnly Raw 0.8
              PlanetHeavy Raw 1.5
              DerelictField Parts 0.7
              DerelictField Repair 0.85
              Industrial Raw 0.8
              Civilian Food 1.25
              Scavenger Parts 0.8
              Armored Ammo 1.25
              Armored Repair 1.15

              The table is generated into assets/base/market/base.content.ron.

              The proof is market_sandbox: it sells iron ore at a living asteroid-rich station and at a living planet-heavy one through the Inventory pane, checks each Sell pays the computed bid, and checks the asteroid-rich bid is the lower. The restock proof is system_station_restock.
