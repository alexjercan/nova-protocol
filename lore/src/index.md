**Nova Protocol** follows people making a living around Saturn. Trade offers
choices, but EarthWorks Industrial controls much of the equipment, housing,
and services needed to turn work into a life.

Start with [Setting](setting.md): the agreed facts and the questions that will
shape the world, its map, and its language. This is an author's reference;
what is true is not necessarily what its people know.

The other chapters are earlier drafts awaiting review. They are not answers
to the Setting page's open questions. Illustrations remain visual proposals.
