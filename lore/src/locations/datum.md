**Datum** is EWI's largest established station in the rings and the workplace
under [Calloway's](../characters/calloway.md) authority. It is built at the site
where the Shelter was destroyed. The change from Shelter to Datum represents
reconstruction and a transfer of power, not a renamed intact station.

By the start of Y0, Datum is the largest station in the rings. The measure of
"largest" has not yet been fixed: resident population, occupied volume, or
industrial capacity could give that claim different practical meanings.

## Reconstruction

EWI took over Kestrel's interests after the destruction and forced sale.
Calloway received promotion and became Datum's station director. These outcomes are fixed;
the construction sequence and exact appointment date are not.

<aside class="proposal">
<strong>Proposed dates: Y-2 and Y-1</strong>
<p>Put the first occupied phase of Datum in Y-2 and its direct Earth relay service in Y-1. Expansion continues to the established Y0 position. These dates require a credible construction and supply model; they do not assume that a station of this size can be built from nothing in two years.</p>
</aside>

Whether surviving structure was reused, replacement modules were brought in,
or construction occurred around residual ruins remains open. So does the fate
of the physical evidence at the site. Reconstruction is not proof that every
trace of the attack has vanished.

## A corporate home

Datum is a direct EWI operation, not merely a licensed independent station.
EWI's employment system ties work to housing, supplies, and access to passage.
That control affects people who maintain the station as well as people whose
labour directly produces cargo.

<aside class="proposal">
<strong>Proposed social geography</strong>
<p>Separate well-maintained management spaces from crowded worker accommodation and maintenance areas. The difference should appear in quiet, privacy, repair priority, and access to services, not just decorative luxury. The exact districts and room arrangements are not established.</p>
</aside>

A resident need not have worked for Kestrel or know that the station stands on
the site of a deliberate attack. The extent of public knowledge about the
site's history, memorials, and permitted discussion remains to be decided.

## Relay and regional influence

Datum has direct communication to Earth. It has not been established as the
only way to send a message, nor as an instantaneous channel. Alternative
relays, ship-carried information, personal access, delays, prices, and
monitoring all remain open.

EWI's regional influence also does not automatically mean every ship must
visit Datum. Its particular cargo, repair, administrative, or passenger
functions need review before routes can be drawn around them.

## Relationships

- **EWI and the Board:** Datum is a major direct operation; local authority
  does not make Calloway the owner of the corporation.
- **Former Kestrel workers:** EWI acquired their employer's interests, but no
  rule places every transferred worker at Datum.
- **Earth Fleet:** aligned institutional interests do not establish a Fleet
  garrison or permanent patrol here.
- **The Kites:** the site's history matters to the community, but an attack on
  or seizure of Datum is not a prescribed future event.

## Open questions and gaps

Datum needs a viable growth history, a clear set of services, and a population
model. The relay needs rules that explain its importance without making all
other communication impossible by convenience.
