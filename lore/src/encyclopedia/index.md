# Encyclopedia

A reference to the people, places, and institutions of the Saturn settlements.
The [Setting](../setting.md) chapter gives the overall context; encyclopedia
articles examine individual subjects and their relationships.

Articles describe the agreed world, not an official account published by an
in-world authority. Unknown names, dates, and specifications remain open rather
than being supplied for completeness.

## Articles

- [Corporate operators](corporate-operators.md): the major companies' economic
  role, authority, workforce, and relationships with public institutions.

## Illustrations

The encyclopedia combines technical diagrams with atmospheric concept
illustrations. Captions distinguish their roles:

- **Reference diagrams** explain established relationships. A relationship
  diagram is not an orbital map or a complete administrative chart.
- **Visual concepts** explore appearance. They do not establish a station
  design, ship specification, location, or scale unless separately approved.

The initial images are original SVG artwork. They can be opened at full size
from their article captions and image links. Future articles can include other
image formats without changing the distinction between reference and proposal.
