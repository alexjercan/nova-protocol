# Setting

This is the working foundation for the book. Start with the confirmed facts,
then resolve the questions below before expanding the description. The Atlas,
Story, and detailed articles await their own rewrite; their existing prose
does not settle these questions.

## Confirmed facts

These are the anchors confirmed in the current setting discussion, not a new
chronology or a complete account of the Shelter incident.

- EWI is an abusive Earth-origin mining company that expanded into space mining; the Board is its leadership.
- Saturn is the principal space-mining region, and EWI controls mining operations for most workers there.
- Trading partners are not restricted to EWI, but even independent operators depend on equipment, housing, and services it provides.
- Kestrel formed at Saturn as an independent mining company with its own equipment, rather than an Earth company's branch.
- Kestrel offered better working and living conditions at Saturn; guaranteed Earth passage is not its defining advantage.
- EWI sabotaged Kestrel and absorbed its interests; the precise legal and financial process remains open.
- Pell founded the Kites, chiefly from former Kestrel people and EWI people who followed him after the catastrophe.
- Halloran was forced from Kestrel into EWI employment and is assigned to Meridian.
- Okono is an engineer who has been assigned to Meridian throughout his career.
- Earth Fleet is Earth's military and lacks real combat experience; EWI's resource power corrupts Earth politics.

These are authorial facts, not universal public knowledge. EWI's sabotage does
not become something every worker knows merely because it appears here.

**Working crew concept:** Halloran, Okono, and the player character work aboard
a small repair and collection craft operating from Meridian. Its name,
design, and individual crew duties are not fixed.

## Questions to resolve

### Saturn and the map

1. Has Saturn been home to permanent communities for one generation or several?
2. Which resources make Saturn economically central, and who buys them?
3. Are the main population centres ring stations, moon settlements, or both?
4. How long does an ordinary work craft take to travel between its base and a job?
5. What most limits Earth passage for ordinary workers: price, seats, journey time, or company permission?

### Work and independence

6. Does an EWI licence grant access to company assets, legal mining rights, or both?
7. Which essential supplies and services can an independent crew obtain without EWI?
8. What happens to a crew that cannot pay its EWI fees?
9. Was Kestrel privately owned, worker-owned, or a mixture of the two?
10. Which concrete worker protections made Kestrel a better place to live and work?

### Life and power

11. Where do families, retired workers, and people unable to work live?
12. What most damages workers' health: overwork, injuries, chronic exposure, or lack of treatment?
13. Who enforces local property and contract claims: EWI security, Fleet, or civilian authorities?
14. Where can outlaw crews trade and resupply?

### Language

15. Is English the shared working language, or a reader-facing translation of a multilingual world?
16. Should dialogue use mostly familiar industrial language, or a distinct Saturn vocabulary?
