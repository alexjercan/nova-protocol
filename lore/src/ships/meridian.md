**Meridian** is an EWI ship and [Okono's](../characters/okono.md) engineering
assignment throughout his career. That places it in the employment history
around Y-4 without making it an established participant in the Shelter attack.
Okono had no complicity in the attack.

Its name follows EWI's survey vocabulary. It does not establish a ship class,
size, weapon fit, bridge layout, or colour scheme.

## Work and life aboard

A career-long assignment makes Meridian a useful place through which to
develop everyday EWI life: maintenance, familiarity, working relationships,
and dependence on decisions made elsewhere. The ship's actual business must
be chosen before those activities can be described precisely.

<aside class="proposal">
<strong>Proposed role to review</strong>
<p>A ring-side industrial service vessel would connect work sites, repairs, and local transport without making Meridian responsible for every kind of mining or a regular Earth passage. A dedicated mining vessel would instead concentrate the crew's experience around extraction. The first is recommended for variety of ordinary work; neither role is established.</p>
</aside>

No technical specification is supplied to force a later chase or battle.
Propulsion, endurance, capacity, living conditions, and external appearance
remain open. The ship's captain and the scope of an engineer's authority also
need decisions; calling Okono an engineer does not make him chief engineer.

## Crew relationships

Okono's assignment is established. Halloran's is not. Making them current
crewmates would give their different employment histories regular contact,
but the book does not assume that answer while the question remains open.
Neither Pell nor Calloway has an established command assignment to Meridian.

See the separate articles for [Halloran](../characters/halloran.md),
[Pell](../characters/pell.md), and [Calloway](../characters/calloway.md). Their
shared connection to EWI is not proof that they served together on this ship.

## During the fall of Kestrel

Meridian existed in Okono's career during the period of the Shelter's
destruction. Its location, task, and observations are not fixed. An eventual
account must preserve the difference between being employed by EWI, witnessing
part of an incident, and knowing its deliberate purpose.

The [story brief](../story/fall-of-kestrel.md) does not depend on Meridian being
at the attack site. It can give us a view of ordinary corporate life without
making every established ship a participant in the catastrophe.

## Open questions and gaps

- What is the ship's primary work, and who directs its assignments?
- Who commands it, and which other crew roles are needed?
- Is Halloran assigned here at Y0, or working elsewhere?
- What does a career aboard the same ship mean when crew and equipment change?
- Where was Meridian in Y-4, and what could anyone aboard actually observe?
- What conditions make this ship familiar to Okono without making it safe?
