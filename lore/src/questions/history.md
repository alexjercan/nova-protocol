# History and people

These 42 entries develop the history before and at the start of Y0. They do
not design a future investigation or campaign. In particular, asking what
evidence exists does not promise that it will be found or acted upon.

The [Atlas timeline](../atlas/timeline.md) separates known order from proposed
years. [The Fall of Kestrel](../story/fall-of-kestrel.md) connects the established
historical events and preserves the attack's knowledge boundary. Its story
material and the new character profiles do not close these questions by being
more detailed.

## Calendar and chronology

**Gap:** Y-4 and Y0 are fixed reference points, but the wider history and the
calendar used by inhabitants are not established.

1. What real or invented calendar do people use, and how should the book show
   dates without making the author's Y0 reference an in-universe epoch?
2. Does the proposed forty-year span of EWI space operations provide the right
   historical depth, or should that industrial history be shorter or longer?
3. Which dates can be precise from public records, and which should remain
   broad periods because the relevant event was gradual or disputed?
4. How do the proposed Saturn expansion, Kestrel growth, and Shelter milestones
   fit travel, construction, and a plausible working population?
5. When did EWI become dominant on Earth, and how did its terrestrial power
   support expansion rather than appear only after space mining succeeded?
6. Which histories need to run alongside the Shelter sequence: Fleet,
   recruitment, other companies, migration, independent communities, or piracy?

## The attack

**Gap:** A charge believed to be for ice work destroys the Shelter. Its placement
must remain credible without adding an informed conspirator by accident.

1. What legitimate job did the placement crew believe they were doing, where
   did they believe it was safe to work, and why were they sent there?
2. What physical relationship between that work site, the charge, and the base
   permits the destruction? The station and work-site geometry need to fit
   the cover account.
3. What did the crew know about the charge and its timing, and why did they
   not understand that their own lives were being deliberately sacrificed?
4. What were Pell's actual orders, what did he perceive, and what practical
   choice made his rescue a clear act of disobedience?
5. What casualty and rescue numbers are useful to establish, if any, and what
   population and rescue capacity must be settled before choosing them?
6. Were there any additional participants or witnesses, and what precisely did
   each know? Do not extend the original circle of knowledge without review.

## Collapse and transfer

**Gap:** The forced sale and worker absorption are established; their legal,
financial, and practical sequence is not.

1. What essential capacities did Kestrel lose with the Shelter, and which
   surviving assets could not function independently of those capacities?
2. Who could provide emergency shelter, supplies, repairs, or finance, and how
   did access to those necessities narrow the company's remaining choices?
3. What debts, liabilities, claims, or agreements made the final transfer
   possible? None should be imported from a retired version of the setting.
4. What actually happened to workers' employment, housing, accounts, and return
   rights during transfer, and when could they refuse?
5. Who within Kestrel negotiated or contested the sale, and how did their
   interests differ from the interests of displaced workers?
6. Can the proposed Y-3 formal completion support the established outcome,
   including Halloran's transfer, flight, and the Kites' early formation?

## Public accounts and evidence

**Gap:** EWI's reactor-failure account exists, but its circulation, reception,
and evidential basis have not been defined.

1. Who issued the official account, through which institutions or channels,
   and how quickly did it become the accepted working explanation?
2. Was any investigation conducted, by whom, with what access, and under what
   limits? An investigation is not yet an established event.
3. What could survivors directly observe, and what could not be distinguished
   from an ordinary industrial failure from their positions?
4. What records, objects, testimony, or inconsistencies exist at Y0, who has
   access to them, and what can each actually prove?
5. Who suspects deliberate wrongdoing rather than negligence or exploitation
   of the aftermath, and how did they arrive at that suspicion?
6. How are the dead and the lost workplace remembered publicly and privately,
   and what pressures shape what people will say aloud?

## Reconstruction and the Roost

**Gap:** Datum's growth and the Roost's availability both depend on the remains
of Kestrel, but those practical histories are not connected yet.

1. What survived at the Shelter's site, what could be reused, and what had to
   arrive from elsewhere for Datum to become habitable and productive?
2. When was Calloway promised or given authority, and how much of Datum's
   redevelopment plan existed before the attack rather than after it?
3. Do the proposed Y-2 occupation and Y-1 relay milestones fit the available
   workforce, equipment, transport, and ongoing reconstruction?
4. What was the Roost's original purpose, when and why was it listed as
   disabled, and who was responsible for that status?
5. Why did EWI's acquisition of Kestrel's interests not prevent Kite use of the
   Roost, and what do the relevant people now believe about the property?
6. When did the Kites occupy it, what activity is visible or explainable, and
   who knows its real scale and allegiance? Do not make paperwork a cloak.

## Personal histories

**Gap:** Four names have established connections, but their earlier lives,
experience, and relations to the wider world remain largely open.

1. What are Pell's age, upbringing, reasons for joining EWI, rank, and service
   history before working under Calloway?
2. What are Halloran's first name, age, role, background, and reasons for
   joining Kestrel? Which rights mattered personally before the transfer?
3. What are Okono's first name, age, engineering background, career length,
   and reasons for spending his career assigned to Meridian?
4. What are Calloway's first name, previous authority, route to advancement,
   and relationships with the Board before Y-4?
5. Where were Halloran and Meridian during the Shelter's destruction, and
   what did Halloran and Okono experience without assuming complicity or loss?
6. Which family, friendship, work, or community relationships predate the
   incident, and which people should have no personal Shelter connection?

## Current wants and relationships

**Gap:** A history does not yet provide a present life. Proposed concerns need
review without becoming assigned future arcs.

1. What does Pell want at Y0, what authority does he actually hold, and which
   actions would make other Kites support, resist, or call him a fanatic?
2. What does Halloran want now beyond disliking EWI, and what obligations make
   acting on that desire difficult?
3. What does Okono want outside keeping Meridian functioning, and where does
   his acceptance of familiar conditions end?
4. What does Calloway need to maintain his position at Datum, what does he
   fear losing, and what ordinary responsibilities does he handle competently?
5. Do Halloran and Okono currently work together, know Pell, or have other
   loyalties? Which relationships are chosen rather than assumed from proximity?
6. What gives each person pleasure, pride, shame, attachment, or disagreement
   outside the main institutional conflict, and who sees that side of them?
