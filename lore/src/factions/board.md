**EWI's Board** is the leadership behind the corporation's expansion and
resource power. It is not a separate faction with its own established fleet,
territory, or naming system. Its importance lies in its authority over EWI and
its influence beyond the company.

The Board and Calloway were the only people who knew the demolition charge's
true purpose at the time of the Shelter attack. Its membership is not yet
named, but that lack of names must not dilute the Board's established
responsibility.

## Corporate and political reach

EWI controls enough resources that public dislike rarely produces an effective
challenge. The Board's influence reaches policy and senior appointments,
including Fleet leadership. This does not yet specify whether it acts through
formal appointment powers, financial pressure, patronage, or several methods.

The Board's priorities treat workers as expendable. Expansion preserves and
extends the system from which it benefits. The greed and cowardice of senior
figures are compatible with extreme violence: they expose other people to
risks they do not bear themselves.

<p>Use production targets, service budgets, and promotion decisions to make local managers compete for approval. Managers conceal failures and pass costs down to workers. ## Calloway and the Shelter

[Calloway](../characters/calloway.md) shared knowledge of the attack's purpose and
was later rewarded with promotion. He runs [Datum](../locations/datum.md), not
the whole corporation. His personal ambition and the Board's monopoly project
align in the historical outcome.

Whether Datum's exact redevelopment plan existed before the attack remains
open. The reward does not by itself establish the contents of a prior promise
or a specific planning meeting.

## Limits without innocence

Political dominance is not omniscience. Local arrangements, falsified reports,
private loyalties, and the cost of intervention can all limit what reaches or
interests senior leadership. Those limits do not excuse the Board or guarantee
that opposition will remain undetected.

The number of directors, their personal relationships, their public
visibility, and any internal divisions are still undecided. No benevolent
minority, secret reform programme, or future split is implied.

## Relationships

The Board directs **EWI**, rather than operating as a separate organisation
outside it. **Calloway's** advancement serves its interests, but his exact
reporting chain and promises made before the attack remain open. **Earth and
Fleet** are subject to its political influence; the precise mechanisms do not
yet establish a formal corporate right to issue every public order.

Its relationship to **Kestrel** includes knowing responsibility for the attack
and the benefit of acquisition. Its opposition to **the Kites** does not imply
complete knowledge of their supporters or the Roost's activity.

See the [relationship summary](../atlas/relationships.md).

## Open questions and gaps

The Board needs a concrete chain of authority and a defined public face.
Otherwise "the Board decides" substitutes for an explanation of how a
particular appointment, acquisition, or act of suppression happens.
