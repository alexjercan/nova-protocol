# Operators and independent communities

**Licensed operators, unlicensed workers, scavengers, and pirates** do not form
one organisation. These terms describe different relationships to EWI,
recognised property, work, and force. A person can occupy more than one position.

The [Kites](kites.md) are the largest independent community at Saturn, not the
only people living outside direct EWI employment.

## Licensed operators

Smaller businesses work under EWI terms while remaining distinct from its
direct departments. Access to transport, servicing, purchasing, or facilities
limits their practical independence. The exact licence and the authority that
grants it are not yet established.

A licensed operator may own equipment, employ workers, and retain a business
name without being able to refuse EWI's prices. Nominal ownership and freedom
to operate are different things. Licensed operators need not provide better
conditions than the company; some pass its pressure directly down to workers.

<aside class="proposal">
<strong>Proposed commercial detail</strong>
<p>A small operator is free to choose how to meet an order but depends on EWI for essential servicing and a buyer. Lost production becomes a bargaining weakness: accepting harsher terms may be the only way to resume work. This is a proposed mechanism, not a settled licence clause.</p>
</aside>

## Informal and unlicensed work

Salvage, repair, and irregular trade connect people outside corporate
employment to those inside it. Recovered material and skilled work can be
useful to local managers who would not openly support political resistance.
Managers may tolerate, exploit, or suppress the same activity in different
places.

This does not make marginal communities secretly indispensable in every
transaction. It means that eliminating them has local costs, while tolerating
them can serve a manager's immediate interests. The Board need not have
approved each arrangement.

## Salvage, theft, and piracy

Recovering abandoned material is not the same act as taking cargo from a crew
by force. Whether material counts as abandoned, however, depends on ownership
claims and enforcement that remain to be specified.

[Fleet](fleet.md) protects recognised trade. EWI's goods receive that
recognition; unlicensed recovery can be described as theft. Official reporting
can group salvage crews, people providing refuge, and armed raiders under a
single pirate label without making their actions equivalent.

Actual piracy exists, including beyond Saturn. The Kites are not the only
armed raiders. The conduct and organisation of other groups are still open.

## Work, allegiance, and trust

An EWI worker can privately support the Kites. A scavenger can refuse to help
them. An independent operator can trade with both corporate workers and
fugitives without joining an organised resistance. These possibilities explain
why a political map cannot be drawn from employment records alone.

<aside class="proposal">
<strong>Proposed social detail</strong>
<p>Trust often rests on work done and obligations kept rather than an announced political identity. People disagree over unpaid favours, unsafe repairs, and whether helping a fugitive endangers everyone sharing a berth. Such disagreements need not turn every community into a miniature version of EWI.</p>
</aside>

## Relationships

**EWI** supplies services, customers, employment, and pressure in different
combinations. Dependence varies between a licensed business, a salvage crew,
and people living outside corporate housing. None is automatically a direct
corporate department.

**Fleet** distinguishes recognised trade from perceived threats, often through
claims that favour corporate property. **The Kites** are one large community,
not the government of every independent person. Shared grievances do not
establish a shared code, alliance, or obligation to shelter an armed crew.

See the [relationship summary](../atlas/relationships.md).

## Open questions and gaps

The book needs rules for ownership, payment, access, and dispute resolution.
It also needs independents with reasons to exist beyond helping the Kites or
illustrating EWI's cruelty. No extra company or community names are fixed here.
