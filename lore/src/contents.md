# Browse subjects

This encyclopedia describes the world of the Saturn settlements. Start with the
[world overview](setting.md) for the shared context, or browse by subject below.
Links into the overview provide background where individual articles have not
yet been developed.

The main narrative will be told through the web comic. The encyclopedia offers
optional context, not required reading before the story. Both formats share the
same established world; a protagonist, central conflict, and plot remain open.

## Places

- [Keystone](places/keystone.md): the founding station, built as a construction
  and supply hub and now a substantial home.
- [Junction](places/junction.md): Farspan's newer freight-hub town, competing
  with Keystone for traffic and local business.
- [The Saturn settlement network](setting.md#time-and-place): the first
  construction and supply hub, later station towns, and specialized moon
  outposts.
- [Station homes and working sites](setting.md#homes-and-working-lives): rotating
  residential sections, industrial ships, and life at the frontier.

Other station identities, selected moons, and exact orbits remain open.

## Reading the illustrations

The encyclopedia combines technical diagrams with atmospheric concept
illustrations. Captions distinguish their roles:

- **Reference diagrams** explain established relationships. A relationship
  diagram is not an orbital map or a complete administrative chart.
- **Visual concepts** explore appearance. They do not establish a station
  design, ship specification, location, or scale unless separately approved.

Green is the encyclopedia's presentation palette, not a claim that the world
or its company liveries are uniformly green. The comic's art direction is
restrained full color with recurring green accents. Approved designs are shared
between the two formats, while their color treatments differ.

Images can be opened at full size from their articles. Unknown names, dates,
and specifications remain open rather than being supplied for completeness.
The articles are a reference to the agreed world, not an official account
published by an in-world authority.
