# Atlas

The Atlas collects **what exists, who people are, how institutions operate, and
when events happen**. This page is the summary and starting point. Individual
articles carry the detail; [The Fall of Kestrel](story/fall-of-kestrel.md)
develops the connected historical story rather than repeating the entire world
reference.

Saturn is the focus, not the limit of the universe. Only the places, ships, and
people actually established or explicitly proposed are listed here. A naming
example does not create an object.

## Locations

| Place | What it is | Position at Y0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Earth](locations/earth.md) | EWI's origin; industry, capital, recruits, customers, and family ties | Active EWI mining, damaged landscapes, and strong corporate influence |
| [Saturn and the rings](locations/saturn.md) | The principal mining region in this book | EWI dominance with licensed operators and independent communities |
| [The Shelter](locations/shelter.md) | Kestrel's former main base and a home to its people | Destroyed in Y-4; its site rebuilt as Datum |
| [Datum](locations/datum.md) | Direct EWI station under Director Calloway | Largest station in the rings; direct Earth communications relay |
| [The Roost](locations/roost.md) | Former Kestrel station and the Kites' base | Listed as disabled; practical concealment still needs an explanation |

The Shelter and Datum are successive stations at the same site, not two
current destinations. The Roost's earlier ownership does not settle when it
closed, who controlled its records, or how the Kites gained access.

Earth and Saturn exchange more than cargo. Workers carry skills and obligations;
credits can return to families without the people earning them. Datum belongs
to that economy, but is not an established compulsory stop for every ship or
message. The Roost's connections to it must be explained, not assumed invisible.

## Timeline

The [timeline](atlas/timeline.md) records established order and a separate
proposed chronology. Y-4 fixes the Shelter's destruction; Y0 fixes the reference
point four years later. Earlier proposed dates are not approvals of a calendar,
a technological history, or the ages of the characters.

The drawing distinguishes fixed anchors, undated developments, and proposed
years through labels and shapes. It is a chronological reference, not a scene
sequence. It draws on the Kestrel story and on wider world history.

## Relationships

EWI needs labour but does not obtain loyalty merely by employing someone.
Fleet protects recognised trade but cannot identify a concealed community from
employment records alone. Independents may trade with EWI workers while opposing
corporate control. The Kites can depend on that trade without every trader
being a member.

The [relationship summary](atlas/relationships.md), at the end of the Atlas,
compares those dependencies and tensions. It does not replace the individual
accounts or turn conflicting interests into a single neutral public story.

## Gaps in the Atlas

Unmapped space includes Earth's mining and recruitment regions, other Saturn
workplaces, off-Earth customers, Fleet facilities, and the Board's operating
locations. There is no agreed orbital map, travel-time model, station population
scale, or technical design for Meridian.

Start with [the review guide](questions/index.md), which identifies the most
consequential gaps. The [language guide](language.md) owns terminology and forms
of address; images do not silently supply uniforms, rank systems, or machinery.
