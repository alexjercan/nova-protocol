Gantry is an EarthWorks Industrial workship associated with Keystone and the surrounding supply operation. It belongs to the ordinary, unarmed working fleet, not the company's security force.

Its name comes from the built world, following EarthWorks' architectural and engineering tradition. Gantry represents the everyday work behind the company's large construction projects, alongside vessels such as Foundation.

Crew

Nadia Sen is Gantry's captain and pilot. Owen Park is its engineer, and Ivo Marin handles work operations.

All three are cross-trained. Their small crew combines command, flight, engineering, and practical operations rather than assigning a separate specialist to every shipboard function.
