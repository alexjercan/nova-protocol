Ivo Marin

Ivo Marin is the work operations technician aboard Gantry, an EarthWorks Industrial workship. Practical responsibility extends to the people aboard, not only the equipment and the assigned job.

Ivo works with captain and pilot Nadia Sen and engineer Owen Park. All three are cross-trained civilians. Their small crew depends on its members helping each other, not merely completing separate assignments.
