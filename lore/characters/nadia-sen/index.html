Lore / Nadia Sen

Nadia Sen

Nova Protocol / Characters

RoleCaptain and pilot

EmployerEarthWorks Industrial

Nadia Sen captains and pilots Gantry, an unarmed EarthWorks Industrial workship associated with Keystone.

Professional and composed, Nadia expects the company to meet its obligations to the people doing its work. Confidence in those obligations is not the same as unquestioning confidence in every manager.

Nadia works with engineer Owen Park and work operations technician Ivo Marin. All three are cross-trained. Command and piloting are Nadia's principal responsibilities, not a reason for the others to have no judgment or initiative.
